# Research answer: Seattle venues of 5,000 or more (Oct 6, 2026)

Kylie ran `docs/archive/research/seattle-venue-table-prompt.md` and pasted the answer as a PDF on Oct 6, 2026. The PDF is saved beside this page as `docs/archive/research/seattle-venue-table-answer.pdf` (9 pages); this is its full text, re-set as tables, with every link the PDF carried. Folded into `src/data/venues.ts` the same day.

The researcher's own summary: 21 venues clear 5,000 in at least one setup (Memorial Stadium is closed until its 2027 rebuild). Two of the app's figures were stale: T-Mobile Park is 47,943 per the Mariners (MLB.com, March 2026), not 47,929 (a 2019 figure); Husky Stadium is 72,132 per UW, not the 70,083 most sites still show.

Labels: **official** = the venue, team, owner or league publishes it; **reported** = a news outlet, Wikipedia or a ticketing listing; **estimated** = the researcher's figure where nothing is published. Coordinates marked ≈ were estimated from the street address and were geocoded against OpenStreetMap before shipping. Scope: King, Snohomish and Pierce counties plus the Gorge.

## Could not confirm
- Husky Stadium: the year capacity moved from 70,083 to 72,132. UW's page shows only the current figure.
- Tacoma Dome: per-setup counts (end-stage, center-stage, basketball). The Dome publishes only 1,000–21,000; the press room or booking office is the next stop.
- T-Mobile Park and Lumen Field: concert capacities. Neither venue publishes one.
- Pacific Raceways: the 30,000 figure is uncited; no official spectator count found.
- Emerald Downs: no published capacity; the table uses the 2023 peak crowd.
- Everett Memorial Stadium (football side): 12,000 is Wikipedia-only; the Everett School District doesn't publish one.
- Evergreen Speedway: 6,000 vs 7,500; neither from the fairgrounds itself.
- Washington State Fair grandstand: when it took the Umpqua Bank name (the concert series was Columbia Bank's through at least 2021).
- Marymoor Live: the year capacity rose from 5,000 to 6,500.
- Seattle Center grounds: no fixed festival capacity; Bumbershoot numbers are attendance estimates.
- Old Memorial Stadium: pre-demolition capacity (2016–2025).
- Gorge campground: the 5,000-site count comes from a weak source.
- Coordinates marked ≈: estimated from addresses, not surveyed.

## How it was folded in (Claude, Oct 6, 2026)
- Coordinates: OpenStreetMap where it knows the building; the research's figure, marked approximate in a comment, for the Gorge, the fair grandstand and Memorial Stadium.
- Roofs: the app has three kinds (open, covered, indoor). T-Mobile Park's retractable roof, and the sideline roofs at Husky Stadium and Lumen Field, are stored as `covered`, per the research's reading. Amphitheatres with a lawn (White River) and partly covered grandstands (Cheney, Evergreen) stay `open`.
- Festival caps are stored as `concert` setups with a note, since the venue table has no festival kind.
- Memorial Stadium keeps a 12,000 figure from the Wikipedia infobox, labeled reported and unconfirmed, so nights there before the rebuild still size.
- Hard access is the measured rule (`scripts/venue-access.mjs`); the research's yes/no is kept as the fallback flag on the four sites it named. Measured Oct 6: Husky Stadium, Pacific Raceways, the Gorge, White River and Cheney Stadium meet the rule; Remlinger Farms does not.
