## The app's existing figures, checked
Dodger 56,000 confirmed (no official source for 56,500). Crypto.com 18,910 / 18,145 keep, labeled reported (Wikipedia 19,079; another listing 18,997). Coliseum 93,607 through 2018, 77,500 from Aug 2019. BMO 22,000 confirmed; 24,000 concert not found anywhere. SoFi 70,240 confirmed; add 100,240 expanded. Intuit 18,000 confirmed (18,300 also reported). **Kia Forum: 17,505 is the basketball figure; concerts 17,500 (up to 18,000), hockey 16,005.** Hollywood Bowl 17,500 confirmed. Rose Bowl 89,702 keep. DHSP 27,167 confirmed; add 30,510 concert. Angel 45,517 confirmed; 45,483 before 2019. Pauley and Galen confirmed. Honda 17,174 confirmed; add basketball 18,336 and concerts 18,900. Empire Polo 125,000 confirmed as the cap from 2017; 99,000 before.

## Near the line, left out
Titan Gym 4,000 [O]. DHSP Track & Field Stadium 2,000 permanent (to 20,000 temporary). Hollywood Palladium 3,714, Dolby Theatre 3,332. LMU's Gersten Pavilion, the Matadome, Cal State LA's Eagle's Nest and Cal Poly Pomona's Kellogg Arena look under 5,000, not verified. Outside the boundary: Toyota Arena, Ontario (11,089); Glen Helen Amphitheater (65,000); Acrisure Arena and Indian Wells Tennis Garden are Coachella Valley but not festival grounds.

## What Claude did with it (Oct 6, 2026)
- Folded in every ✓ row: 20 new venues and the updates to the existing ones (Kia Forum corrected; Coliseum timing; SoFi expanded figure; DHSP concert; Angel Stadium before/after 2019; Honda setups; Empire Polo before/after 2017; The Forum as the old name; BMO's rename date Jan 19, 2023). Each figure's note says official, reported or estimated.
- Geocoded every ≈ coordinate against OpenStreetMap: all within a few hundred meters of the approximations; OSM's point was used. OSM had no point for the Pomona Dragstrip (the approximation stands, marked in a comment) or FivePoint (the Great Park point is used).
- Left out: Great Park Live (no coordinates, temporary) and Industry Hills (unconfirmed). The Pyramid is in with both names; its rename date is unconfirmed, so the new name shows from 2026 and older nights keep "Walter Pyramid".
- Did not change Dodger Stadium's access flag. The research calls it a hard-access site (hilltop, few gates); the app marks only the Hollywood Bowl, the Rose Bowl and now the Greek that way. Flagging it to Kylie: turning it on would raise Gridlock on every Dodgers night.
- BMO's 24,000 concert figure stays, relabeled an estimate, since nothing better was found.
