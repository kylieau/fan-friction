**Deliver the answer as a downloadable Markdown file (`.md`)**, named **`docs/archive/research/promo-history-research-answer.md`**. ## What to find
For each team below, for each season listed, **every home game that carried a promotion**: the date, the opponent, the promotion's name, its kind, and the source. A game with no promotion needs no row. A season where you found a full schedule is worth more than scattered nights, so say for each team-season whether the list is **complete**, **partial**, or **not found**.

**Kinds**, pick one or more per promotion:
- *giveaway* (bobblehead, cap, jersey, shirt, plush, pin, poster, bag, any item)
- *crossover* (a character, franchise or brand theme night: Hello Kitty, anime, Star Wars, Marvel, Pokémon, Peanuts, Nintendo…)
- *special ticket* (the item or experience requires a themed ticket or ticket package, not a regular ticket)
- *fireworks* or a post-game show or concert
- *discount* (dollar nights, family packs, student or military pricing)
- *championship* (rings, World Series or title items, banner nights)
- *heritage or theme night* (Pride, Japanese Heritage, Filipino Heritage, Faith and Family, Bark at the Park…)
- *celebrity* (a named celebrity appearance, first pitch or crossover with a show or film)
- *star player* (the item names a player, e.g. "Shohei Ohtani Bobblehead")

Also note, when a source gives it: the sponsor, the quantity ("first 40,000 fans"), and whether the game was announced as sold out.

## Where to look
- The team's own promotional-schedule announcement each spring or fall (a press release or MLB.com / NBA.com / NHL.com news story: "Dodgers announce 2023 promotional schedule"), which usually lists every date.
- Archived copies of the team's promotions page on the Internet Archive (web.archive.org): paste the team's promotions URL and pick a capture from that season. Say which capture date you used.
- Local news previews of a season's giveaways (LA Times, Newsday, Seattle Times, Union-Tribune, NJ.com, SNY, Dodger Blue, Dodgers Nation, Pinstripe Alley, Lookout Landing, Gaslamp Ball, Amazin' Avenue, Halos Heaven).
- Bobblehead and giveaway databases (collector sites list team, date and item by season; label those *reported*).

Do not use paid databases or anything behind a login.

## Output
1. **One Markdown table per team** (in the .md file; the column order below is fixed so it converts to CSV), one row per promotion: `team, season, date (YYYY-MM-DD), opponent, promotion, kinds, sponsor, quantity, sold out?, source link`. Dates are the local game date.
2. **A coverage table:** team · season · complete / partial / not found · main source.
3. **Caveats:** promotions that moved dates (rainouts), items that ran for a series rather than one night, and anything you could not date.

Do not invent a date or a promotion. A team-season you could not find is a useful answer.
