# Chicago region: how crowds arrive at large venues

*Prepared October 7, 2026 for Fan/Friction. Every number carries a label: **Official** (published by the venue, team, agency or in a filed plan), **Reported** (a team or agency figure relayed by news coverage), **Projection** (a pre-opening plan figure), or **Estimated** (my inference, with the reasoning shown). "Car" means private car, carpool, rideshare and taxi together. Park-and-shuttle trips (campus garages to Ryan Field, McCormick Lot B to Soldier Field) are counted as car, because the car is what has to leave the area afterwards.*

---

## 1. Verdict

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Is Chicago a driving region, a transit region, or in between?** | **In between, leaning driving.** Only Wrigley Field ([37% car, team survey](https://www.wbez.org/wbez-newsletter/2026/03/12/the-rundown-cubs-push-for-more-parking-near-wrigley-field)) and the downtown festivals run majority non-car. The United Center is at Los Angeles levels ([86.9% car, 2023 patron survey](https://chicago.gov/content/dam/city/depts/cdot/CDOTPRC/TDM/Q1/United%20Center-TDM%20Study.pdf)) despite three L stations within 0.6 mile. The suburban ring (Rosemont, Hoffman Estates, Tinley Park, Bridgeview) is near-total car. Soldier Field and Rate Field sit around 70–75% car (estimated). |
| **Why** | Strong radial rail into the Loop and along the Red Line, so the two lakefront/North Side sites with a station at the door perform like New York; but large on-site lots, suburban-heavy fan bases (43% of UC ticket buyers live in Chicago; 35% of Cubs attendees come from 50+ miles), and thin evening transit frequency push most other buildings to 80%+ car. |

## 4. Where your expectation holds and where it breaks

| Venue | Your expectation | What the evidence says | Car share to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wrigley | Majority transit + walking | **Confirmed.** 63% non-car per the team's own survey. | **37%** |
| Rate Field | Mixed | **Leans car.** Only ~17% rode the L by the team's older estimate; big lots dominate. | **75%** |
| United Center | Majority car despite Pink/Blue | **Confirmed and stronger:** 86.9% car, 8.1% transit. Correction: the stations are Damen (Green), Ashland (Green/Pink) and IMD (Blue); there is no Pink Line stop at the arena, and Damen on the Pink Line is not the one serving it. | **87%** |
| Soldier Field | Mixed (lots + Metra, CTA, 18th St) | **Car-leaning mixed.** No official split; CTA alone carried ~11% at the Swift shows. 18th St is a Metra Electric stop. | **70%** |
| Rosemont / Hoffman Estates / Tinley Park / Bridgeview | Near-total car | **Confirmed** (estimates; no official figures). | 93 / 98 / 97 / 93% |
| Ravinia | Real train share | **Real but minority, and unverified.** No published share; the only observed count is a 2021 monthly alighting total. | **80%** |
| Northerly Island | Shuttles and walking | **Partly corrected.** The current official page lists no shuttle; access is the #146 bus, a ~1-mile walk from Roosevelt, and Soldier Field garages. | **60%** |

## 5. Gaps and how to close them

| Gap | Best next source |
|---|---|
| Wrigley transit vs. walk/bike split | The Cubs' completed traffic study (awaiting City input) and the rewritten Wrigley Traffic Management Plan |
| Soldier Field, Rate Field, Wintrust observed shares | CTA daily station entries on the City data portal (Roosevelt, Sox–35th, 35th–Bronzeville–IIT, Cermak–McCormick Place) against announced crowds; I could not reach the portal from this environment |
| Ravinia and Lollapalooza train shares | Metra's monthly ridership memos for June–August (Ravinia Park alightings; Lollapalooza-day counts) against announced attendance |
| Ryan Field observed split | Northwestern's community advisory council review after the 2026 season; Metra/CTA post-game counts from Oct 2 onward |
| Suburban venues | No public studies found; treat the estimates as provisional |

**One methodological note for the egress model:** at the United Center, ridehail drop-offs before events run about 50% above pick-ups after, so rideshare is a smaller share leaving than arriving; the TDM plan reads this as more sharing or a switch of mode on the way out.
