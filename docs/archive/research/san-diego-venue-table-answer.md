Kylie ran `docs/archive/research/san-diego-venue-table-prompt.md` and pasted this back. Kept as pasted, lightly trimmed. Labels: O official, R reported, E estimate. Coordinates were the researcher's approximations except Viejas; all were geocoded against OpenStreetMap before use (see the bottom). Folded into `src/data/venues.ts` the same day.

**Biggest finding:** Frontwave Arena in Oceanside, missing from the brief. Opened September 2024, 5,500–7,500 by setup; the Sockers moved there from Pechanga. The app's Petco figure, 39,860, is correct.

## Where sources disagree, and the figure to use
Petco: keep 39,860 for baseball; treat 42,445 as a standing-room figure. Snapdragon: 32,000 for Wave matches in 2022, 35,000 after. Pechanga: 12,920 hockey, 14,500 basketball, about 12,000 for a typical end-stage show (estimate between 8,900 and 14,800), 16,100 for combat sports only; the Pechanga naming deal runs through December 2026 and Midway Rising plans a replacement 16,000-seat venue. North Island: 20,500, about half of it lawn. Rady Shell: 4,700 typical, 10,000 only for the six max-capacity nights, so most Shell nights fall under the 5,000 line. Frontwave: 7,500 concerts, 6,000 basketball, 5,500 indoor soccer.

## Near the line, left out
Cal Coast Credit Union Open Air Theatre 4,500–4,600 R; LionTree Arena (RIMAC Arena until Jun 16, 2022) 5,000 O for ceremonies only, 4,500 for productions, 4,000 basketball; RIMAC Field (Sun God, 17,000, students only); Del Mar Arena 3,500 and The Sound 1,900; Humphreys ~1,400.

## Could not confirm
Concert capacities for Petco and Snapdragon; Del Mar race-day seating and O'Brien Hall (Songkick says 6,800); Comic-Con's daily count; the Seals' 2026 move to Frontwave; the Cal Coast naming renewal; a KAABOO revival; Frontwave's road access; all coordinates except Viejas. Two name-matching notes: "Snapdragon Stadium" in December 2011 meant the old Qualcomm Stadium; the old stadium's last event was March 2020.

## What Claude did with it (Oct 6)
- Folded in 13 venues plus the Petco note. Geocoded against OpenStreetMap: Frontwave's approximation was 1.7 km off (OSM's point used); Torero and Jenny Craig moved about 300 m; the rest matched within a block. OSM had no point for the demolished stadium, Del Mar Fairgrounds or Embarcadero Marina Park North, so the approximations stand, marked in comments.
- Names: the old stadium carries Qualcomm → SDCCU (Sep 19, 2017) → San Diego Stadium (2021); Jack Murphy predates the app's 2016 horizon and is left off. Pechanga's rename month is not confirmed; the record says Dec 1, 2018 with a comment. North Island's three names since 2016 are in with their dates.
- The Rady Shell is in at its 10,000 maximum with the typical 3,500–4,700 in the note; most of its nights are under the floor and will read as such once events exist.
- The convention center is in at Hall H's 6,500 with the Comic-Con note; a daily Comic-Con crowd would need its own event record.
- Hard access is measured by the rule, not taken from the table's yes/no column; `scripts/venue-access.mjs` covers the new venues on its next run.
- Feeds: SDSU football and basketball, San Diego FC and the Wave joined the ESPN feed with Snapdragon and Viejas as their buildings; the attendance pull covers them.
