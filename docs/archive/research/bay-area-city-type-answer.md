# Bay Area: how fans get to the game

Researched October 7, 2026. Every figure carries its own date. Labels: **Official** (agency or venue published), **Reported** (a named official quoted in the press, or a team statement), **Estimated** (my number, built from the nearest comparable; never observed). "Car" includes drive-alone, carpool, rideshare and robotaxi drop-off.

## Short answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Whole Bay Area | **In between, leaning driving.** Two anchor venues (Oracle Park, Chase Center) sit at about half by car; nearly every other big crowd is 80–95% car. |
| San Francisco + inner East Bay | **In between, at the transit edge.** Oracle Park and Chase Center about 50% car; Outside Lands and Bill Graham below that; the Coliseum and Cow Palace well above. |
| Peninsula + South Bay | **Driving region.** Levi's, SAP Center, PayPal Park, Shoreline, Stanford Stadium all about 85–95% car. |
| One type for the metro | **Driving or hub (0.85 default).** Wrong by about 35 points at Oracle Park and about 10 at Shoreline. A transit type (0.40) would be about 10 off at Oracle and about 55 off at Shoreline. |
| Biggest risk to these numbers | **The November 3, 2026 regional transit tax vote (Prop RTM).** If it fails, BART plans to end service at 9 p.m. from January 2027, which would push every evening transit share in the core sharply toward car. |

## 4. The extra question: one type for the whole metro

| Type for metro | Default car share | Error at Oracle Park (0.50) | Error at Shoreline (0.95) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Driving or hub | 0.85 | **+35 points** | **−10 points** |
| Transit | 0.40 | −10 points | **−55 points** |

**Pick driving or hub (0.85).** Most venues that would fall back to the default are suburban and car-led, and the core venues that matter most already have their own figures. The real cost lands on SF venues with no figure of their own: Bill Graham (~45 points off), Outside Lands (~50 points off), the Greek (~25 points off). Give those three their estimated figures above and a per-metro type costs little. Between driving and hub I can't judge spillover from this research; the default share is the same either way.
