## Why this is needed
Fan/Friction is a personal log of live events. Each night gets a friction read from how many big crowds share a date. Upcoming concerts have no crowd figure, so today every concert is treated as a sellout of the room. The fix is a ladder: an official sellout first, then the same headliner's past show in the same room, then **the room's own published average per show**, then one shared default fill ratio. This brief collects the third rung, and from it the shared default is computed. No fame tiers, no resale data, nothing behind a paywall.

## What to find, per venue
For each venue below, as far back as 2022 and no further:

1. **Published annual concert attendance and number of shows**, by calendar year. Best sources, in order:
   - The venue's or operator's own year-end release (many arenas publish "X shows, Y fans").
   - Public-agency reports for publicly owned rooms (city, county, state or authority annual reports, budget documents, council presentations).
   - Billboard's year-end venue rankings as quoted in free news coverage (tickets sold and shows by capacity class). Say if the figure counts all events or concerts only.
   - Pollstar figures only if quoted in a free article. Do not use paywalled pages.
2. **Whether the count is concerts only or every ticketed event** (sports, family shows, comedy). If mixed, give the split if one is published.
3. **The concert capacity by setup** (end-stage, in-the-round, general-admission floor, lawn) when the venue publishes it. Where our table below says "none on file," this matters most.
4. **Notable gaps:** renovations, rebuilds, renames, or capacity changes of more than ~15% since 2022, with dates.

Compute **attendance ÷ shows** for each year where both are published, and mark it *estimated*.
