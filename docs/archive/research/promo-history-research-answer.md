## How to read the tables

- One row per promotion, and only for home games.
- **MLB opponents** come from the [Retrosheet game logs](https://github.com/chadwickbureau/retrosheet), not the promo sources. Every MLB date was checked against an actual home game, and promos whose date wasn't a home game are listed under Caveats. "(DH)" means a doubleheader that day.
- **Other-league opponents** are as the source states them.
- **Sold out?** is filled only where a source said so; "—" means not reported. Only one game in the whole set was announced as a sellout.
- **Quantity** is shown only where the source states it.
- **Kinds** use the brief's labels: giveaway, crossover, special ticket, fireworks (including drone shows and postgame concerts), discount, championship, heritage/theme, celebrity, star player.
- **Stadium Giveaway Exchange** rows come from a collector database. They are labelled *(reported)* in the source column, as the brief asked.

### Labels to treat with care

- Several soccer theme nights name a sponsor but no item.

### Method notes for the model

- **MLB's promotions feed has nothing before 2025.** A 2023 pull with `hydrate=game(promotions)` returned empty.
- **The Internet Archive doesn't help for MLB.com.** Captured promotions pages hold no promotion data, because the list loads from a ticketing service.
- **Stadium Giveaway Exchange** (`stadiumgiveawayexchange.com/<team>/<team>-<year>-promotional-stadium-giveaways/`) was the best free source for MLB giveaways by season. It covers MLB only.
- **Retrosheet game logs** give date, opponent, day/night and **paid attendance for every MLB game, 2021–2025**. That covers the outcome side of the promo-lift test.
