# Dallas–Fort Worth venues of ~5,000+ — capacity table for Fan/Friction

Prepared 7 October 2026. Scope: Dallas, Tarrant, Collin and Denton counties.

## How to read this

**Labels on every number**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **(O)** | Official: the venue's, team's, university's, league's or owning city's own page or release |
| **(R)** | Reported: press, trade publications, Wikipedia, ticketing or tourism listings |
| **(E)** | Estimated by me; treat as a placeholder to verify |

**Coordinates.** AT&T Stadium, Globe Life Field and American Airlines Center coordinates come from Wikipedia infoboxes (R). Every other coordinate is my own approximate building centroid (E, roughly ±150 m). Run them through a geocoder before the road-overlap logic depends on them; the Grand Prairie and Mansfield points are the least certain.

**Sort order.** Rows are sorted by the figure I recommend as the app's default for the building's main use today (shown in bold first in the capacity cell), largest first. Standing-room, festival and record figures are listed separately and do not drive the sort.

**Corrections to the brief** (details in the rows):

- **Dallas Wings did not move in 2026.** Memorial Arena's renovation slipped; the Wings played 2026 at College Park Center (plus three games at AAC). The move is now targeted for 2027.
- **Choctaw Stadium has no regular tenant in 2026.** The Renegades moved to Toyota Stadium (renamed Dallas Renegades), North Texas SC moved to the new Mansfield stadium, and Dallas Trinity FC plays at the Cotton Bowl, not Choctaw. The rename to Choctaw Stadium happened in August 2021, not 2020.
- **Billy Bob's Texas is over the line** (the venue says up to 6,000), so it is in the main table.
- **Dickies Arena 13,300 is basketball, not hockey.** Hockey is 12,200.
- **AT&T Stadium's World Cup crowds were about 70,000, not 94,000.** FIFA's pre-tournament listing said 94,000; actual 2026 crowds ran near 70,000.
- **Credit Union of Texas Event Center was renamed in October 2021**, not 2023. **Dos Equis Pavilion was renamed in April 2018**.
- **Fort Worth Convention Center Arena** (13,000+, not in the brief) held its final event in September 2026 and is being demolished.
- "Globe Life," listed alone among the stadiums in the brief, looks like a duplicate of Globe Life Field; I treated it that way.

**Boundary.** I would keep the four-county line. Two notes: Texas Motor Speedway is inside Fort Worth city limits but in Denton County, and Mansfield straddles Tarrant, Johnson and Ellis counties (the new stadium appears to be on the Tarrant side; verify). I found no 5,000+ venue just outside the line that would justify moving it.

I could not find published per-day attendance for any of these centers' biggest events, so I can't give numbers. My reasoning for "no": convention crowds arrive across the day rather than in one gate-open and gate-close wave, much of the audience stays in nearby hotels, and both downtown centers sit on rail. If one specific event is huge (a multi-day expo or the marathon packet pickup), add it as a one-off event with an estimated daily figure rather than giving the building a permanent capacity.

### State Fair as a daily event

**Yes, treat the State Fair as a daily event for all 24 days**, using the official daily gate figure (or, for future days, a forecast from the same weekday and phase of last year's run). Reasons:

- Its smallest 2025 day (44,797) is bigger than every arena in the region, and its average (~84,200) is bigger than every building except AT&T Stadium and the Cotton Bowl.
- Crowds peak on weekends and the final week, so a flat daily figure would mislead; use day-of-week and week-of-run averages.

**Don't double-count the Cotton Bowl on fair days.** Game tickets include fair admission (R), so the fair's gate count most likely already includes the game crowd; I'm inferring that from the ticket policy, and the fair hasn't said so outright. Model a fair-day game as the fair's daily figure with the Cotton Bowl crowd as a subset that arrives and leaves in a concentrated wave around kickoff. Red River day 2025 (193,960 through the gates, with a 92,100-seat stadium inside) is the template.
