# Fan/Friction build 7 research: cost categories, Gemini answer (pasted by Kylie, 2026-10-22)

Same prompt as 03-cost-categories-claude.md.

## Summary
- Tension: logging friction vs data payoff. Cost tracking is an optional layer on a vibe-based diary, not a ledger.
- Minimal (Ticket, Parking, Other): low friction, low value; Other swallows a $150 jersey and a $15 beer alike.
- Rich (Ticket, Food/Drink, Merch, Travel, Lodging, Fees): higher friction (splitting one card charge), high value ("$400 on Dodgers food", "away games cost 3x home").
- Mental models: Letterboxd (fast, optional enrichment); Copilot/YNAB/Monarch (avoid ledger feel); Flighty (users love rich stats when input feels effortless); Splitwise (avoid group math; keep it about the individual).
- Per person vs total: default to Your Share (per person). Logging the full amount for 4 tickets friends paid back breaks yearly stats.
- Free/comped/season tickets: "Comped / Free" toggle or chip; ticket explicitly $0, excluded from average ticket cost, still counts toward attendance.
- Watched from home: hide the standard categories; offer only a single optional "Stream / PPV" field (food at home would dilute venue pricing).

## Recommended default set (chips in the bottom sheet)
1. Ticket: final checkout price; no separate Fees.
2. Food & drink
3. Merch
4. Getting there: parking, rideshare, transit (a key friction metric, esp. in LA).
5. Travel & stay: flights + lodging; for away games/destination festivals; separate from local friction.
6. Other: catch-all (clear bag at the gate, poncho).

## Comparison with Claude (03)
- Agree: same six categories; fees in Ticket; Free chip; Travel & stay merged.
- Differ: amount (Claude out of pocket + optional quantity; Gemini your share); watched (Claude Stream/PPV + Food & drink + Merch + Other, remote spend on its own line; Gemini a single Stream/PPV field); Claude adds Travel & stay excluded from per-venue/per-event averages by default, not-entered/free/paid states, "N of M logged" completeness, and season-ticket handling.
