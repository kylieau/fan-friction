## Research answers kept in `docs/`
- `docs/research/city-builds/los-angeles/la-venue-table-answer.md`: the venue research, with the verdict on each figure the app already had.
- `docs/research-oct-2026/`: October 2026 events in four cities (from the other session).
- `docs/research/second-opinions/sport-list-second-opinion-answer.md`: how a game is classified.
- `docs/research/topics/hard-access-weight-answer.md`: what hard access and transit do to egress (StreetLight, Hwang/Humphreys/Pyun, FHWA); the ×1.25 and the car-share scaling.
- `docs/research/city-builds/seattle/seattle-city-type-answer.md`: how Seattle fans arrive, per venue (UW's 2022 game-day survey, Climate Pledge Arena's transit dashboard, the 2026 World Cup mobility deck, Sound Transit); the `carShare` on every Seattle venue and the city type.
- `docs/research/city-builds/atlanta/atlanta-city-type-answer.md`: how Atlanta fans arrive, per venue (MARTA fare-card counts at the two stadium stations from a 2018–19 Georgia Tech study, MARTA's World Cup situation report, the Braves' 2016–17 transportation plan, venue parking pages); the `carShare` on every Atlanta venue, and the city type: driving. 17 of its 20 rows are estimates; only Mercedes-Benz Stadium and State Farm Arena have an observed count.
- `docs/research/city-builds/new-york/new-york-city-type-answer.md`: how New York fans arrive, per venue (the Belmont Park FEIS, LIRR and MTA ridership reports, the 2013 Barclays TDM survey, NYC DOT's 2012 Yankee Stadium survey, NJ Transit event counts); the `carShare` on every New York venue, and the city type: hub, not transit. 18 of its 28 rows are estimates from the nearest venue with a count; MSG and Harrison are the biggest risks.
- `docs/research/topics/venue-egress-answer.md` and `docs/archive/formula/gridlock_inputs_all_43_venues.csv`: parking counts, mode shares, occupancy and clearance times for all 43 venues (EIRs, city plans, LA Phil, MTS, SacRT). Exit lanes are not published anywhere, so the cars-per-lane rule is shelved; `carShare` can be filled from the official/reported rows.
- `docs/archive/formula/formula-review-response.md`: the formula review, including the calibration plan (§11).

## Not yet used, checked as available
- Ticketmaster Discovery API: **used since Oct 7, 2026** for concerts in every covered city and for sports listings at buildings no feed covers (`ticketmasterSource.ts`; the key is a GitHub secret and in `.env.local`). Free developer key, 5,000 calls a day, 5 a second. Since Oct 9 the pull asks by venue id (`ticketmasterIds` on each venue, 188 of 213), a dozen venues a call, so a busy city is never cut at Ticketmaster's 1,000-result ceiling; a venue with no id is searched by name near its spot, and proximity within 300 m is accepted only when exactly one room is nearby. Multiple rooms on one campus require an exact room name or venue id; promoter names cannot substitute for room names.
- MLB promotions (bobbleheads): not in the schedule feed; still to find.
- Setlist.fm API: terms of use to review before any setlist link is filled automatically.

## Milwaukee (Oct 11, 2026)

The [implementation register](../research/city-builds/milwaukee/milwaukee-implementation-register.md) records 41 active room identities in the approved four-county scope. Three school gyms added from the coverage-gaps research are available in hand logging; their public schedules are not connected, and no school sports feed or Ticketmaster room IDs were verified. MLB covers the Brewers; ESPN covers the Bucks and Marquette/Milwaukee men’s and women’s basketball; HockeyTech covers the Admirals. Milkmen and DockHounds MLB team ids are verified, but that feed returned no historical games. Other local team records are not claims of schedule coverage. Room-matched Ticketmaster listings supplement the sports feeds; ambiguous Rave-complex listings are omitted pending a specific room. Potawatomi Event Center remains out of the venue catalog because Ticketmaster's combined casino/Northern Lights/Event Center record cannot safely distinguish its room from other casino events. No festival-wide attendance is used as a concurrent fixed-room crowd.

Milwaukee uses provisional `sprawl` with no venue travel overrides. Local measured mode shares are unavailable; the 0.85 fallback is the existing model default. Public elevation measurement is pending a successful service response, rather than manual access flags.
