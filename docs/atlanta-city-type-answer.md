# Atlanta venue arrival mode

As of Oct 7, 2026.

## Bottom line

**Atlanta is a driving region for a night out.** Its two best-served buildings land around 70–85% car, and everything outside the Downtown–Midtown rail spine is 95%+ car. Only one-off mega-events (World Cup, Super Bowl) and carless park festivals push transit toward half.

Your expectation holds with one correction. State Farm Arena sits on top of a MARTA station, but its observed rail share is only about 9–12%, smaller than Mercedes-Benz Stadium's. Truist Park, Gwinnett, Alpharetta, Kennesaw and Hampton are car, as expected. Kennesaw State adds a campus shuttle share.

**Region:** driving region. The 2019 ARC commuter survey found 76% of weekly commute trips are drive-alone ([ARC](https://atlantaregional.org/what-we-do/mobility-services/regional-commuter-survey)). No regular-season crowd in the venue list is observed below about 70% car.

## How to read the numbers

Only two venues have an observed transit count. Every other figure is an estimate built from parking, transit access and the nearest comparable.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Official | A share the venue, team or agency published as a share. None of the venues has one. |
| Reported | A published rider count divided by published attendance. |
| Estimated | An estimate from parking supply, transit access and the nearest venue with a count. Treat the range as the honest answer. |

**What the MARTA counts measure.** The Georgia Tech study counted Breeze-card entries at the two stadium stations in the post-game window, above the normal baseline, for 2018–19 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.05359)). That undercounts, because it misses fans who walk to Five Points or Peachtree Center, leave early, or skip the fare gate. MARTA itself warned in 2013 that teams report tickets sold rather than people in seats, which understates the transit share ([AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/the-news/nXYrmihS3AVjVXpHboRlvJ)). The observed Hawks and Falcons shares are raised 1–3 points to account for that.

**MARTA's World Cup figures** are unlinked passenger trips, so a rider who transfers at Five Points counts twice on each leg ([MARTA report](https://itsmarta.com/marta-world-cup-situation-report.aspx)). Halving them for round trips overstates the number of riders.

**Station name.** GWCC/CNN Center station became **SEC District** station on Dec. 1, 2025 ([MARTA](https://itsmarta.com/marta-renaming-gwcc.aspx)). Older sources use the old name.

**Rideshare.** No Atlanta source splits rideshare out of the car share. Every venue that publishes guidance has one designated pickup zone, which is the exit choke point for that share.

### How the Downtown shares were built

- **Falcons and college football:** 28 games averaged 69,477 fans and 10,846 post-game riders at the two stations, which is 15.6% ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.05359)). The Georgia Dome next door ran 19.6% in MARTA's 2013 estimate ([AJC](https://www.ajc.com/news/the-news/nXYrmihS3AVjVXpHboRlvJ)). This uses 17%.
- **Atlanta United:** the same study found 8,037 riders against 52,712 fans, which is 15.2%. MARTA and the club say an average of 17,000 fans rode to 2023 matches ([Atlanta United](https://www.atlutd.com/news/atlanta-united-forms-brand-partnership-with-marta-may-2024)). Against the 47,526 average crowd ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Atlanta_United_FC_season)), that is 36%. The club figure likely counts trips or all stations, and the study undercounts, so this uses 25%.
- **Hawks:** 67 games averaged 15,278 fans and 1,425 post-game riders, which is 9.3% ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.05359)). This uses 11%.
- **World Cup 2026:** the eight match days added 117,000–147,000 rail trips each over a typical day ([MARTA report](https://itsmarta.com/marta-world-cup-situation-report.aspx)).
  - The three match days with no Fan Fest (June 18, July 7, July 15) still added 117,000–127,000 trips, so the stadium drove most of the surge.
  - 52% of match-day demand was at SEC District and Vine City ([MARTA](https://itsmarta.com/marta-safely-moves-millions.aspx)).
  - Halved for round trips, that is about 34,000 riders against a 68,000 average crowd (544,516 over eight matches, per [Atlanta News First](https://www.atlantanewsfirst.com/2026/07/16/by-numbers-more-than-500000-fans-attend-fan-festival-world-cup-matches-atlanta/)), or about 50%.
  - Transfers and fans without tickets inflate the trip count, so treat 50% as a ceiling.
- **Centennial Olympic Park:** days with only Fan Fest added 26,000–53,000 trips each ([MARTA report](https://itsmarta.com/marta-world-cup-situation-report.aspx)). 43% of that demand was at Peachtree Center and 34% at SEC District ([MARTA](https://itsmarta.com/marta-safely-moves-millions.aspx)). Daily visitors averaged about 27,000 ([Fort Wayne Business Weekly/AP](https://www.fwbusiness.com/news/national/article_8fc63047-965f-5719-8598-0c402cbf774e.html)). General World Cup traffic inflates the implied share, so this uses 35% for festivals there.
