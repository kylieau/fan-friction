## Reading and import rules

**Membership is by each room’s drive:** inside means a normal, non-event drive of **60 minutes or less** from the supplied hall; Further out means beyond that hour up to **about three hours**. County, region and state names below locate rooms only. A new room within another covered city’s hour belongs to that other city. Further out records are reference-only and never count in the city’s friction verdict. Already-filed identities are excluded even when their older grouping differs from today’s drive rule.

**Labels:** **official** = owner, operator, institution, public authority, ticketing operator, venue representative or governing sports series; **reported** = identified independent report, destination/booking inventory or live map/inventory; **estimated** = calculation, routing model or stated inference. Every capacity, trip and coordinate claim is labeled. Policy thresholds, dates, addresses and source identifiers are not unlabeled capacity claims. A recommendation drawn from “about,” “over,” or a historical benchmark retains that qualification. It is one filing value, not certification of a new exact maximum. Separate rooms and areas are not added together; no annual or cumulative multi-day attendance becomes capacity. Festival rows distinguish a published simultaneous/day limit or ticket-population ceiling from actual attendance. Where only a weak or dated benchmark was recovered, **CAPACITY HOLD** remains explicit.

**Driving method retained from Delivery 10:** free public [OSRM road routing](https://project-osrm.org/docs/v5.24.0/api/) from the exact supplied hall coordinate to each documented room point. The displayed **estimated range** rounds the raw modeled minutes up to the next five-minute step, then adds ten minutes for access uncertainty. Every row links its coordinate-specific route and retains the raw duration. These are reproducible road/access screens, **not observed traffic-aware normal-day drives**. Gates, construction, toll choices and parking approaches can change them. Main tables follow the raw screen: inside at or below the hour, Further out above the hour through three hours, and complete outer-boundary records beyond it. “About” is handled through boundary holds and supplementary planner disagreement, not a hidden hard exclusion. Generic city-to-city planners are supplementary and never replace hall endpoints.

**Coordinates:** latitude first. Mapped feature representative points and live inventory building/field points are labeled **reported**. Means of actual mapped outline vertices are labeled **estimated**; they are not surveyed spectator doors. Leads without a reliable room point stay outside import tables and disclose that gap.
