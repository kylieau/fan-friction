# Research brief: crowd sizes for open-site and free events

## What this is for
Fan/Friction is a personal log of live sports and concerts, with a "friction" read computed for each night from public schedules: how many big events share a date, how close their start times are, and whether their crowds hit the same roads. It covers 26 metros: Atlanta, Austin, Bay Area, Boston, Chicago, Columbus, Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston, Kansas City, Las Vegas, Los Angeles, Mexico City, Milwaukee, Minneapolis–St. Paul, Montreal, Nashville, New Orleans, New York, Philadelphia, Phoenix, Sacramento, San Diego, Seattle, Tampa Bay (built as "Tampa", code TPA), Toronto, Vancouver.

The app sizes events by crowd, using venue capacity or an expected draw. Events at open sites (festivals in parks, parades, marathons, street fairs, free concerts, fan fests) have no fixed capacity, so the app leaves them off the map and keeps them on an unsized list (`docs/reference/unsized-events.md`). Crowds use three separate fields: **Estimated** (independent: police, media, researchers or the app), **Announced** (the organizer's official figure) and **Final** (a counted headcount, rarely public). The display order is Final, then Announced, then Estimated.
