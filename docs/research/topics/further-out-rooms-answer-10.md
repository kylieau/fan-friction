## Reading and import rules

**Inside:** normal, non-event drive of **60 minutes or less** from the supplied hall. **Further out:** beyond that hour, up to **about three hours**. For Raleigh–Durham, **either hall’s inside hour controls**; the shorter hall route screens Further out eligibility. County, state and regional names are location only. Nothing is outside simply because it crosses a border. Further out references never count toward the city’s verdict. A room within another covered city’s hour belongs to that city; user-specified existing-room exclusions and earlier delivered identities are retained.

**Labels:** **official** = room/operator, owner, resident team, identified project professional, ticketing operator, public authority or governing sports series; **reported** = identified independent report, destination inventory or live mapping/inventory; **estimated** = road-model output, calculation or explicit inference. Dates, addresses, source identifiers, policy thresholds and coordinate precision are not unlabeled capacity claims. One recommendation is given per full table row. A number described as “over,” “approximately” or a historical benchmark retains that meaning; rounding to one recommended integer does not certify a new exact maximum. Do not combine separate rooms, seats with an already-inclusive lawn total, temporary capacities that have not actually been sold, multi-day attendance or annual visitors. Festival attendance alone does not establish simultaneous capacity.

**Driving method retained from Delivery 9:** coordinate-specific free public [OSRM routing](https://project-osrm.org/docs/v5.24.0/api/) from the exact supplied hall point to the room’s published/map point. The displayed **estimated range** rounds the raw modeled duration up to the next five-minute step and adds ten minutes for access uncertainty. The original raw duration and reproducible route appear in every full row. These are road/access screens, **not measured traffic-aware normal-day journeys**. Road-speed assumptions, construction, toll options, parking and gate selection can change them. Main tables use the raw model: inside at or below the hour, Further out above the hour through three hours; records past the three-hour model screen go into the separate complete outer-boundary table. “About” is preserved through holds and second-planner disagreements; a slight model overrun does not conclusively disqualify a normal-day trip. Generic city-to-city planner checks below are explicitly supplementary, never substitutes for the requested hall route.

**Coordinates:** latitude first; labels distinguish live OpenStreetMap feature points from live inventory coordinates. Springfield’s grandstand and Du Quoin’s racetrack points are explicitly **estimated means of live OpenStreetMap outline vertices**, linked to the actual mapped feature. A representative point is not a surveyed spectator door. Broader grounds, complex or fairground-district points have **DATA HOLD**. Similarly named foreign venues, roads, banks, museums, different-state tracks, town centers and unrelated sports fields were rejected. Missing reliable points stay disclosed as unconfirmed leads, not invented import records.

**Holds:** **INNER-BOUNDARY HOLD** = near the hour; **OUTER-BOUNDARY HOLD** = near/past the outer road screen or planner disagreement; **CAPACITY HOLD** = current count, qualifying paid layout or historical benchmark unresolved; **DATA HOLD** = point, naming, actual paid recurrence or another required fact unresolved; **PROVISIONAL INSIDE** = model screens inside pending ordinary gate/traffic confirmation. Full outer-boundary records preserve all requested fields for review and are excluded from the main Further out screen until resolved. Exact current Ticketmaster names and some complete naming timelines remain unconfirmed where stated; no listing label is guessed from a sponsor name.

**Alias check:** supplied existing names and earlier full table identities were checked using name normalization, former sponsor names, institution/town and physical points.
