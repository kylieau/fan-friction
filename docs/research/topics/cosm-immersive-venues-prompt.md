## What this is for
Fan/Friction is a personal log of live events people attended (sports, concerts, anything else they went to), in any city in the US, Canada and Mexico, with more countries later. Each event has one **type** from a fixed list, which sets its icon, its label on every screen and how its name is written. The app pre-lists events of about 1,000 people or more; only events of 5,000+ count toward the "friction" read for that night.

The type list today (36): Baseball, Softball, Basketball, Football, Soccer, Hockey, Lacrosse, Volleyball, Tennis, Golf, Combat sports, Motorsports, Rodeo, Horse racing, Rugby, Cricket, Track & field, Sports event, Concert, Festival, Classical & opera, Theatre, Comedy, Dance, Film, Talk & lecture, Spectacle & magic, Circus, Ice show, Kids & family, Fair, Expo & convention, Market, Pop-up, **Watch party**, Event.

Today a Cosm visit is typed **Watch party** (television icon) and named "{Matchup} · Watch party" with Cosm as the venue, e.g. "Ohio State at Michigan · Watch party". Kylie decided that on Oct 14 on the condition that Cosm doesn't sell it as a screening. She has since confirmed it is sold as a screening, and that Cosm also shows films and brands itself as a new form of live entertainment. So the type and the name need a fresh decision, backed by facts.
