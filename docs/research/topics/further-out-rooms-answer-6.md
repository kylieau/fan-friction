## Reading and import rules

The controlling rule is the October 23 city-hall rule: a normal, non-event drive of **60 minutes or less is inside**; **beyond 60 minutes to about three hours is Further out**. Further out rooms are reference entries and never count toward the covered city’s verdict. Counties, regions, town names and national borders identify location; they do not determine inside/outside status. A room inside another covered city’s hour belongs to that city. Already-on-file rooms are excluded, including the Toledo records and Mid-Ohio already delivered.

**Labels:** **official** means an owner/operator, resident team, public authority or official ticketing partner publishes the number; **reported** means a named independent source or mapped inventory publishes it; **estimated** means a calculation, modeled journey or explicitly approximate inference. Dates, addresses, names, policy thresholds and source identifiers are identifiers rather than unlabeled attendance claims. A published rounded official figure remains labeled official and retains its approximation in the setup text. A recommended capacity is a single labeled import value, not a promise that every show sells that many places. Do not add capacities of separate courts, stages, halls, lawns or sports layouts together.

**Driving method retained from Delivery 5:** live public [OSRM road routing](https://project-osrm.org/docs/v5.24.0/api/) was queried from the exact user-supplied hall coordinate to each listed venue point. Each row links the reproducible coordinate-specific route. The displayed **estimated range** rounds the model up to the next five-minute mark, then adds a ten-minute access allowance. This is a consistent road/access screening estimate, **not a traffic-aware measurement of a typical day**; closures, rush-hour/event traffic, stadium queues, toll choices, mountain weather and international border processing are not validated by that range. A raw model at either boundary needs a normal-day planner check; those rows are explicitly marked. Source responses can change with the public road model. The local evidence files retain the request and returned duration used for this delivery. A nearby city-to-city journey is never substituted for a hall-to-room route.

**Points:** decimal pairs below are latitude first. Public [Photon map search](https://photon.komoot.io/) was used to identify OpenStreetMap features; the linked feature or live inventory supplies each coordinate. Outline/grounds representative points route to nearby roads, not necessarily the public door. Broad complexes and stadium-specific point gaps are disclosed. Unrelated geocoder matches, bus stops and town centers were rejected. No coordinate has been invented to fill a missing room.

**Import holds:** do not auto-import rows marked INNER-BOUNDARY HOLD, OUTER-BOUNDARY HOLD, CAPACITY HOLD, DATA HOLD or PROVISIONAL INSIDE without resolving the named issue. All table rows, including inside leads, still include a single provisional recommended value and a usable mapped coordinate. Inside entries remain outside the Further out group even if useful to add to a city’s own-room inventory. Lead bullets with unresolved points or capacities are not import rows.

**Cost flags:** 🚩 Public event tickets, parking, toll roads and any private room rental cost money. Ticketmaster resale links may carry resale pricing; a catalog label does not establish the primary seller. All research used publicly readable pages and free map/routing requests. No ticket, rental, paid database, signup or outreach was purchased or initiated; listed rental/contact pages are evidence only.
