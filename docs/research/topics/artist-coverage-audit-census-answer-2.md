## Reading and counting rules

- **Official observation:** dates, published wording, times, room assignment and purchase routes are observations on the linked room, operator, resident or primary seller pages unless a cell says Unknown or a note says reported. Source links are attached to rows and room findings. Line breaks are normalized in titles; presenter subtitles remain with the act where useful. No feed, attraction, genre or coverage-rate fields are assessed.
- **Read** means the published room/operator listing was readable through the window and into later dates, or both covering month grids were read. It is not a guarantee against subsequent additions. Admission questions can remain unresolved even when the calendar is read. **Partial** means a positive/resident extract, unclosed pagination, room-wide calendar Unread, or unresolved room/calendar inconsistencies. **Unread** means no usable dated room-calendar body was obtained; it is never an empty-calendar finding. **Reported closed** is the user-supplied inventory status, not a closure re-verification.
- Each main row is one explicitly dated performance or daily admission with a public ticket-purchase route or paid admission stated by an official source. Sold-out shows remain. Run ranges are not expanded into assumed weekly patterns. A tour with a date but no start would be labeled date-only; no such tour entry was established in this pass.
- Explicit **show/performance** starts take precedence over headline ticket timestamps or doors. If only doors, registration or an ambiguous seller timestamp is readable, the performance start is Unknown and the observed clock time is retained in the note where available. No time is borrowed from another night. Unknown is not midnight.
- Free admission, private hire, subscriptions/packages as products, parking, table upgrades, meet-and-greet tiers and free add-ons are not separate event rows. A paid performance sold with a reception is counted once as its performance. Separate physical rooms are filtered when the operator names them.
- Ambiguous admission, conditional room allocation and unresolved candidate dates stay in the room’s unresolved table. A listed seller does not imply a completed checkout, ticket availability or an observed API record. No login, purchase, paid access, archive, hidden endpoint, mirror or access-block bypass was used. Normal visible pagination was used for readable calendars at the Grove and McCallum.
