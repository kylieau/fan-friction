## Reading and import rules

**A normal, non-event city-hall drive of 60 minutes or less = inside; beyond 60 minutes to about three hours = Further out.** Ottawa–Gatineau has two controlling halls; a room within the hour of either is inside, and every row gives both routes. Regions, counties, municipalities and provincial/state lines describe location only. Further out rooms are reference rooms and never count toward the city's verdict. A room within another covered city's hour belongs to that city. User-listed on-file exclusions take priority.

**Labels:** official = owner/operator, resident team, public authority, governing body or identified project architect/engineer/seating supplier; reported = identified independent report, destination inventory or live map/inventory; estimated = calculation, road model or explicit inference. Canadian capacities as published; Canadian drives in minutes. Approximate counts keep their source wording. Do not combine separate rooms, fixed seating with an inclusive total, festival days, annual attendance, parade crowds or unverified floor/standing additions.

**Hold markers:** INNER-BOUNDARY (near the hour); OUTER-BOUNDARY (near or beyond three hours); CAPACITY (current number, qualifying setup or paid deployment unresolved); DATA (name, point, paid recurrence or other field unresolved); PROVISIONAL INSIDE (road model inside, normal-day/gate check incomplete). A historic capacity can be a labeled conservative benchmark only. Held rows are reviewable research, not import approval. Calgary was checked against a second planner because its road model exceeds the outer screen.

**Alias check:** candidates compared with the supplied lists and earlier deliveries under apostrophe, space, punctuation, hyphen/en-dash and ampersand variants and former sponsor names. Logan's Maverik Stadium is distinct from the on-file Maverik Center; Kingston's Slush Puppie Place is distinct from Gatineau's on-file Slush Puppie Centre; Alamo Stadium is distinct from the Alamodome; the Cabaniss football/soccer stadium is distinct from its baseball field. "New" means newly researched against the exclusions, not newly built.

**🚩 Costs:** free public pages and free map/route endpoints only; no purchase, registration, subscription, venue hire or outreach. Unavailable live pages are listed; no cached or archived copy was substituted.
