### Evidence and interpretation

- **Official**: a venue, owner, operator, team, university, public agency, sponsor or documented project designer supplies the number. This is not necessarily a legal occupancy certificate. Contractor/designer evidence is identified as such.
- **Reported**: a secondary publication, directory or mapping source supplies the number. An official figure quoted through a secondary account remains **reported** unless its primary page was available. **Estimated**: an approximation, including a public authority's explicitly approximate estimate.
- Capacity figures, drive durations, coordinate pairs and numerical event frequencies carry those labels. Dates/years, delivery numbers, venue names, road identifiers and addresses identify things rather than measured quantities.
- Capacity means people in a particular setup or an explicitly identified site-wide/event-day limit. Cumulative attendance, parking spaces, floor area, cars, and turnout forecasts do not establish spectator capacity. Separate rooms in one complex are not added together; a published combined-site setup is identified explicitly.
- Tables descend by the **recommended usable maximum**, not the largest unsupported figure found. Some maxima depend on standing room, opening the hall to a lawn, or temporary bleachers. The relevant condition is stated; an advertised but unverified larger setup does not silently become the sorting figure.
- Recurring paid team seasons, concert seasons, and multiple separately programmed paid days of an annual festival qualify as regular use. Private rentals, merely hypothetical configurations, free admission tickets, and invitation-only ceremonies do not suffice. When concerts are included in paid fair admission, that relationship is stated.
- **Driving:** all table ranges are **estimated downtown-to-venue ranges** adapted from the linked planners' **reported** city-to-city or city-to-venue times, allowing for local access. They are not consistently verified City Hall/Zócalo-to-public-door measurements. The Bay Area core is downtown San Francisco; Seattle, Toronto and Mexico City use their respective central cores. Exceptional congestion, event queues, mountain closures and border processing are excluded. Border crossings and routes exceeding the target are explicit. A base route to a different city or identically named venue was rejected.
- **Coordinates:** latitude first, longitude second, in decimal degrees. Published mapping pairs are **reported**; classification as a building/grounds-outline representative point is **estimated** unless the source explicitly maps the outline. Broader complex points are identified and never sold as precise theater/arena pins. Missing coordinates remain **unconfirmed**; no city-centroid or photo-camera substitute is invented. No supplied point is a verified public door.
- **Names:** every recovered post-2016 operating name/project alias is recorded, with effective dates where established. An announcement date is not automatically the operating-change date. “Same name documented” means no intervening rename was confirmed, not that every temporary alias has been proved absent. Exact Ticketmaster labels are explicitly unconfirmed where a reliable match was not recovered; an old URL slug is not treated as the current displayed name.
- **Live-source rule:** no cached or archived copies were used. A live publisher still hosting an old report is dated historical evidence, not an archive reconstruction. Failed pages and reader size limits are listed per city; their search snippets are not used to settle a disputed current capacity. Successfully loaded independent sources are cited instead.
- **Cost:** research used public pages, without registration, payment or outreach. 🚩 Event tickets, tolls, venue rentals, paid streaming, premium mapping and paywalled reporting may cost money. No purchase was made, and no one was contacted. Ticket pages are evidence of public paid use, not a recommendation to buy.
