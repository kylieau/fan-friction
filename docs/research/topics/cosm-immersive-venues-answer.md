# Answer: Cosm and other immersive screening venues

Status: answered. Researched Oct 14, 2026, for the brief "Research brief: Cosm and other immersive screening venues". Answers the five sections in order, then a self-audit.

**Short version.** Cosm's own pages don't call a sports showing a "watch party" — but, as far as I could find, they don't call it a "screening" either. A ticket is sold under the bare matchup (for example `NCAAF: #2 Notre Dame @ #8 BYU`), and the venue sells the whole format as "Shared Reality". Films are sold as "{Title} in Shared Reality". Press and fans use both "watch party" and "screening". No Cosm building comes near 5,000 people; the largest reported figure for a whole building is 2,600.

My recommendation is a new type, **Live screening**, for any live event shown in a venue whose business is showing screens (an immersive dome or a cinema). **Watch party** stays for gatherings in arenas, bars and plazas, and **Film** covers anything recorded, wherever it is shown. A showing counts toward friction only when the *building's* own crowd reaches 5,000, so a Cosm showing never counts.

## 4. Recommendation for the type list

### 4a. The rule

The type follows **what is shown** first, then **what kind of venue** it is shown in. The venue brand ("Cosm", "Shared Reality") is not part of the decision; it belongs in the venue field.

Add one type, **Live screening**. It covers any event happening live somewhere else and shown to a ticketed audience in a venue whose business is screens. That means immersive domes and cinemas. It also fills a gap the current list has: Met Live in HD and World Cup-at-AMC have no honest home today.

| What's shown ↓ / Where → | Immersive dome (Cosm, Sphere) | Cinema | Arena | Bar / plaza / fan zone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Live sports | **Live screening** | **Live screening** | **Watch party** | **Watch party** |
| Live concert / opera / theatre relay | **Live screening** | **Live screening** (Met Live in HD) | **Watch party** | **Watch party** |
| Recorded concert (concert film) | **Film** | **Film** | **Film** | **Film** |
| Film (incl. dome re-compositions such as "IT in Shared Reality", "The Wizard of Oz at Sphere") | **Film** | **Film** | **Film** | **Film** |
| Original or recorded show (Cirque "O" in Shared Reality, Postcard from Earth, dome art) | **Film** | **Film** | — | — |
| Live performers in the room (e.g. a Sphere concert) | Normal type (Concert, Spectacle & magic…) | — | — | — |

Two tie-breakers:
- **"Encore" relays** (a Met encore shows the recording days later) → **Film**.
- **Join-in-progress "Fast Follow" games** at Cosm → still **Live screening**.

**Why not keep Watch party for Cosm?** It's defensible: teams use that exact phrase for the same activity. But Cosm doesn't use it, and your Oct 14 condition was that Cosm not sell it as a screening. Cosm also sells every item, sports included, the way a cinema sells showtimes. A screen-venue type lets the app treat Cosm, cinemas and Sphere alike in every city.

**Why not rename Watch party to Screening?** That would mislabel the majority case: bars, plazas and arena road-game parties, all of which officially call themselves watch parties.

**Why not just Film?** Film implies a recorded work. A live NFL game isn't one, and Cosm keeps the two apart itself (films carry "in Shared Reality", games don't).

### 4b. How the name reads (phone row ≈ 32 characters)

Today's pattern doesn't fit the row. `Ohio State at Michigan · Watch party` is 36 characters, and `Ohio State at Michigan · Screening` is 34, so both truncate.

Proposed patterns:
- **Row (short):** `{Away short} @ {Home short} · Screening`
- **Full (detail screen):** `{Away} at {Home} · Live screening`, with Cosm Detroit in the venue line

Using "@" in the row matches Cosm's own titles and saves two characters. The type label is "Live screening", and the row tag drops "Live" for space; the icon carries it.

| Real listing (as sold) | Row, short | Chars | Full |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cosm Detroit, Sat Oct 17, 2026: `NCAAF: #2 Notre Dame @ #8 BYU` | `Notre Dame @ BYU · Screening` | 28 | `Notre Dame at BYU · Live screening` |
| Cosm Atlanta, Mon Oct 12, 2026: `NFL: Bills @ Rams` | `Bills @ Rams · Screening` | 24 | `Buffalo Bills at Los Angeles Rams · Live screening` |
| Cosm Detroit, Fri Nov 27, 2026: `NCAAF: #7 Texas @ Texas A&M` | `Texas @ Texas A&M · Screening` | 29 | `Texas at Texas A&M · Live screening` |

For comparison, `Notre Dame @ BYU · Watch party` is 30 characters, so it fits too. Length doesn't decide between the labels; the "@" short form does the work either way.

Where the short form needs short team names, `Ohio St @ Michigan · Screening` is 30 characters.

Rankings (#2, #8) and the "Fast Follow" prefix are Cosm sales copy and should be dropped from our name. A film keeps its own title with no suffix, e.g. `IT`. The venue line already says Cosm, and "in Shared Reality" can go in a subtitle if wanted.

### 4c. Icon idea (Phosphor)

- **Live screening:** a projection screen, ideally with a small live/broadcast signal. Use the projector-screen idea, or the screen plus broadcast-waves idea if a combined glyph isn't available. It needs to read differently from the television (Watch party) and from Film's reel or clapper.
- **Watch party:** keep the television.

### 4d. Friction

Count the building, never the game being shown. A Cosm showing should **not** count, because no Cosm figure reaches 5,000:
- The largest whole-building number is 2,600, a designer's figure.
- The reported dome figures are ~500–700.

Keep one threshold for everything. A Live screening or Watch party counts toward the night's friction read only when the **venue's own** labelled capacity, or its reported attendance, is **5,000 or more**. Cases that could qualify:
- **Sphere:** 17,600 seats, though Oz showings reportedly use ~5,000, so it's borderline.
- **Full-arena watch parties:** e.g. Rogers Place when both bowls are sold. Capacity wasn't checked here.
- **Fan festivals:** e.g. the Zócalo at 55,000–60,000.

Pre-listing is a separate question. A Cosm dome alone (~500–700, reported) is **below** your ~1,000 pre-list bar, but a whole building (~1,500–2,600) is above it. If Cosm showings should be pre-listed, the rule has to use building capacity. That's your call, given how much the reported figures conflict.
