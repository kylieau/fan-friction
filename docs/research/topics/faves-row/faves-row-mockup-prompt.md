# Mockup prompt: the Faves row on Fan/Friction's Following tab

Written Oct 17, 2026 for a mockup model (Mock Mosaic or another). Attach the three images in this folder: `faves-row-today.png` (the live tab), `faves-row-tried-A-three-tiles.png` and `faves-row-tried-B-team-line.png` (two tries, neither chosen). The answer goes beside this file as `faves-row-mockup-answer.md` plus its board images.

## What the screen is
Fan/Friction is a personal log of live events (sports and concerts) with a friction read on each night. The Following tab has two sub-tabs, Faves and Friends. Faves is one line of time for the teams, artists, venues and festivals a person follows: above a "Today · Sat, Oct 17" rule, the last 60 days (a team's results, an artist's past dates); below it, the next 60 days. Month bands ("OCTOBER 2026") separate the months on both sides of the rule. The tab opens on the rule and the past is reached by scrolling up. The Friends sub-tab and the Stubs tab use the same line of time, so the three should feel like one family.

## The ask
Draw the Faves row. It must show, at a glance: the day (the month band carries the month, so the row carries the day), the team or artist it belongs to (the reason it is on this tab), the game or show (title), the venue and time, and the date's read tile at the right. A past game row also shows the result ("W 17–15 at Oregon", the team's own page already writes it this way). Give three or four options at 390 px wide, each as a phone screen with the sub-tab header, the badge strip, two past rows, the today rule, and four upcoming rows, including one away game with the "Away" tag instead of a read tile and one row whose title is long ("Clippers at Timberwolves").

## What exists, reuse it
- **Date tile:** 44 × 44, weekday over the day number, 1 px ink outline, 10 px corners (the Stubs and Friends rows start with it).
- **Team badge:** a blue tile (`#005A9C`) with the code in white ("UCLA", "LAC"), a small white disc astride its top-left corner carrying the sport icon; college programs read "UCLA MBB" only beside another UCLA program of the same sport; a pro team that shares a code and sport with another pro badge in the strip carries its league in 11 pt under the tile.
- **Read tile:** the date's 1–10 read with its word (Chill / Mild / Spicy / Brutal / Cooked), about 60 × 60, at the row's right, opening the date page; "Away" (grey tag) where the app does not cover the building.
- **Month band:** full width, grey, uppercase 13 px bold.
- **Today rule:** a 2 px blue line with "Today · Sat, Oct 17" centred on it, 13 px bold blue.
- **A row's last line** on Friends is 12.5 px blue ("Sam +2 attending"); try B used it for the team ("★ UCLA Bruins").
- **Type:** Manrope; 11 pt minimum; rows at least 44 pt tall; Letterboxd density.
- **Colour roles:** blue is action, gold is crowd, violet is friction. No new colours; no gold text on white.

## Constraints
- One row component for past and future; the only difference on a past row is the result in the title and no time.
- The person's own answer ("Attending") is not on this row; it lives on Stubs.
- No friction mark and no occasion tag on these rows (they show information, not conclusions).
- Mark every option "EXAMPLE data"; team names and venues may be real, crowds and reads are examples.
- Measure what you draw: title width in px at 390, row height, whether any title truncates.
- Say which option you lean to and why; Kylie decides.
