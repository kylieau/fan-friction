# Artist coverage audit: Los Angeles working installment

**Common window: Monday, October 26–Sunday, November 22, 2026, inclusive.** Research began October 19, 2026 (Los Angeles date) and resumed October 20 after a usage-limit interruption. These are the first four full Monday–Sunday weeks starting after the research began. Event times below are local to the room; the window crosses the Los Angeles daylight-saving change, so a fixed UTC offset must not be applied to the whole window.

**Status: incomplete. This file contains a Los Angeles working extract of 100 events, not a completed coverage audit.** The room-by-room reference census has not been fully enumerated. The extract therefore cannot establish citywide feed coverage, represent every format present across the entire window, or support a product coverage claim. New York and Mexico City have not been sampled. The requested city order is preserved; work stops within Los Angeles.

The useful results are the dated event worksheet, public seller checks, explicit unknowns, concrete identity/title problems, and a resumption ledger for every Los Angeles inventory entry. No unobserved event, genre, API response, or attendance figure has been filled in.

## Evidence labels and limits

- **O — official:** facts displayed by the room, operator, resident organization, or seller. Dates, published times, event wording, seller titles and visible labels in the worksheet are O unless marked otherwise. A source being official does not resolve disagreements between official pages.
- **R — reported:** the largest room capacity supplied in the research brief. Those figures are reproduced as reported figures, without treating them as verified capacities for the sampled event’s configuration.
- **E — estimated:** every audit-derived count, quota, format assignment, comparison and proxy size band in this report. The counts are exact tallies of this worksheet, but are estimates of neither the city population nor its coverage.
- **U — unknown/unobserved:** not established in the permitted reading. U never means absent, zero, title-only, or no public source exists.

No API key, account, login, purchase, paid dataset, archive, or block bypass was used. Failed or incomplete reads remain in the ledger. Search snippets were leads; an unopened event body was not substituted with a cached or archived copy.

Ticketmaster requires an API key for Discovery queries. SeatGeek’s documentation also requires client credentials. Consequently a public event page, artist link, lineup or shopping category is **not** being counted as proof of a Discovery event, an embedded attraction, or a returned genre/subgenre. The requested structured fields remain U. [Ticketmaster authentication and response documentation](https://developer.ticketmaster.com/products-and-docs/apis/discovery-api/v2/), [SeatGeek authentication documentation](https://seatgeek.github.io/).

## Collection rule and strata

The rooms’ own pages and operator/resident pages were consulted before following their seller links. From retrievable official event material, the working extract was filled purposively to the two room-maximum quotas, retaining different formats and non-Ticketmaster routes. Repeat performances remain separate when the official page gives a separate date or time. Free admission was excluded; a calendar purchase link alone was insufficient to establish a paid event.

This is a **collection rule for a pilot**, not the brief’s finished sampling rule: the full room-first denominator was not closed before this extract was assembled. There is no random draw, sampling weight, confidence interval, or basis for extrapolation. Capacities by event setup and the independent/promoter-only portion need further verification. A non-Ticketmaster primary route does not establish absence from Ticketmaster or SeatGeek.

The sample boundary is treated as 1,000 ≤ capacity < 5,000 versus capacity ≥ 5,000, so exactly 5,000 belongs only in the larger band. The quotas below use R room maxima as E proxies. **Actual event-setup size band remains U for all 100 rows.** The quota therefore does not demonstrate a verified split by event size or attendance.
