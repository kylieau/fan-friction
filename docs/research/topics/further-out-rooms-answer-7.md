## Reading and import rules

The controlling October 23 rule: a **normal, non-event city-hall drive of 60 minutes or less = inside**; **beyond 60 minutes to about three hours = Further out**. Further out rooms are reference entries and never count toward the city's verdict. Regions and counties describe location only. A room inside another covered city's hour belongs to that city. All user-listed on-file rooms and specified earlier-delivery records are excluded.

**Labels:** **official** = owner/operator, resident team, public authority, official event source or identified project designer/engineer; **reported** = identified independent report, destination inventory or live map/inventory; **estimated** = calculated, modeled or explicitly approximate inference. The capacity column gives **one** labeled recommendation. Never add separate arenas, fields, lawns, stages, layouts or festival days.

**Hold markers:** **INNER-BOUNDARY HOLD** = close to the hour; **OUTER-BOUNDARY HOLD** = close to/about beyond three hours; **CAPACITY HOLD** = current setup count unresolved; **DATA HOLD** = name, point, current paid use or other data unresolved; **PROVISIONAL INSIDE** = road screen puts it inside but the normal-day check is incomplete. Held rows are research references, not automatically import-ready.

**🚩 Costs:** tickets, parking, tolls and rentals can cost money. All research used free public sources; no purchase, registration, paid database, rental or venue contact. No archived or cached copy was substituted for an unavailable live page.
