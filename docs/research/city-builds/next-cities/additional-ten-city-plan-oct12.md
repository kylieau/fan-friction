## Boundary and coverage questions

- Washington, DC and Baltimore must cross-reference shared edge venues so the two catalogs would not double-count them.
- Raleigh–Durham is a proposed research grouping. Require evidence for combining Raleigh, Durham, Chapel Hill and Cary rather than assuming regional branding defines an event metro.
- Ottawa–Gatineau must test the cross-province grouping, reconcile French/English room aliases, and check event-time transit on both sides. Calgary and Edmonton remain distinct event metros; mountain destinations and rural fairgrounds are edge cases to assess.
- San Antonio and Austin must cross-reference shared edge destinations without assuming the I-35 corridor is one event market.
- Salt Lake City must distinguish the local event metro from the broader Wasatch Front, mountain destinations and Provo/Orem.
- Cincinnati, St. Louis and Portland require cross-state scope checks, not state-line exclusions or automatic inclusion of every nearby municipality.
- Search beyond headline pro venues: women's and college sports, minor leagues, theaters, racing, fairs, open sites and separate rooms on shared grounds. Preserve the 1,000 listing / 5,000 friction distinction and event-specific crowd measures.
