# Proposed next twenty cities

Drafted Oct 12, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). This is a research/build queue proposal, not a lock or authorization to build all twenty. The three formerly top-ranked cities in the older cross-city scan (Bay Area, Dallas–Fort Worth, and Chicago) are now covered, so they are removed from this queue. Pair each city's city-type and venue-table prompts; do not build until the research is reviewed and Kylie approves the scope. Philadelphia, Boston and Tampa Bay already have paired answers in the repo; the other candidates still need their research answers.
