# Fan/Friction — Denver venue table (research answer)

**As of:** Monday, October 12, 2026 (America/Denver, Mountain Time). Public sources only.
**Scope of this file:** fixed rooms/venues (Table A), open-site and dated crowd events (Table B), teams/programs, boundary recommendation, conflicts and change log. No app code, constants, ratings or cost commitments.

## How to read this file

- **Capacity labels.** *Official* = published by the owner, operator, team, league or university. *Reported* = news, encyclopedia or trade source. *Estimated* = an approximation stated as such by the source. Capacity is never used as attendance; observed attendance is kept in its own column.
- **Feed / listing coverage.** I did not query Ticketmaster, AXS, SeatGeek or any feed API in this pass. Feed match status is therefore **unknown** for every room unless noted. Where I saw a marketplace or ticketing page, it is recorded as *listing evidence*, not a verified room match.
- **Coordinates.** Decimal coordinates are given only where a source published them; precision is noted. Otherwise the sourced street address is given and coordinates are marked *not verified*.
- **Change labels.** *Confirmed* (happened), *Scheduled* (officially announced with a date), *Planned* (announced, approvals or funding pending), *Proposal* (reported idea only).
- **Official pages not fetched.** I relied on secondary sources for several venue capacities because official fact sheets (Ball Arena, Broncos stadium facts, Red Rocks) were not retrieved in this pass. Those figures are labelled *reported*. No page was blocked; this is a coverage gap, not an access failure.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

**Core scope (recommended): the Denver–Aurora–Centennial MSA.** The "Denver–Aurora–Lakewood" label in the brief is the pre-2023 name; OMB's July 21, 2023 delineation uses Denver–Aurora–Centennial, with an estimated population of [3,092,037](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_statistical_areas). The MSA has [ten counties](https://www.Wikipedia.com/wiki/Greater_Denver): the six continuous urban counties (Adams, Arapahoe, Broomfield, Denver, Douglas, Jefferson) plus four rural ones (Elbert, Clear Creek, Gilpin, Park). Every 5,000+ room found in the core sits in the six urban counties, including Red Rocks (Jefferson County, owned by Denver), Fiddler's Green (Greenwood Village, Arapahoe), Dick's Sporting Goods Park (Commerce City, Adams) and Centennial Stadium (Centennial, Arapahoe).

**Edge cases — separate, not automatically included:**

| Edge area | Statistical relationship | Recommendation | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boulder (CU Boulder) | Separate Boulder MSA, but inside the Denver–Aurora–Greeley CSA ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_statistical_areas)) | **Optional edge tier, off by default; strongest candidate to add.** | Same combined statistical area; two 5,000+ rooms (Folsom Field, CU Events Center) and one very large dated event (BOLDERBoulder). Keep separate so Boulder events can be muted as a group. |
| Fort Collins / Loveland | Separate Fort Collins–Loveland MSA; not in the Denver CSA ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_statistical_areas)) | **Exclude from Denver; treat as its own city if ever needed.** | Blue Arena is about [55 miles northeast of Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Arena); separate MSA and tenant base (CSU, Colorado Eagles). |
| Colorado Springs | Separate Colorado Springs MSA; no CSA link to Denver ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_statistical_areas)) | **Exclude from Denver; separate city.** | Separate metro with its own pro/college tenants (Switchbacks, new UFL team, Air Force). Shares a state label only. |
| Greeley / Weld County | Separate Greeley MSA but inside the Denver CSA ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_statistical_areas)) | **Exclude for now; re-check.** | No active 5,000+ fixed venue confirmed in this pass. A reported 2024 proposal would move the Colorado Eagles toward Greeley ([source](https://www.milehighhockey.com/2024/7/31/24209854/colorado-avalanche-ahl-affiliate-colorado-eagles-may-be-on-the-move-greeley-loveland)) — proposal only. |

**Uncertain edge venues inside the core:** Centennial Stadium is about [20 miles south of downtown](https://www.stadiumjourney.com/stadiums/centennial-stadium-denver-summit-fc) but is inside the MSA, so it belongs in core scope. Red Rocks is roughly [ten miles southwest of Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Rocks_Amphitheatre) in Morrison and is core.

- **Colorado Convention Center:** Bellco Theatre, Mile High and Four Seasons ballrooms and the exhibit hall share one address; marketplaces label all of them "Colorado Convention Center," and still listed 2026 GABF there after the move ([listing](https://gametime.co/colorado-convention-center-tickets/venues/colorado_convention_center)).

- **Ritchie Center (DU):** Magness Arena, Hamilton Gym and small rinks share a building name ([source](https://www.du.edu/du-events/auditoriums-gyms)).
- **DSG Park:** stadium vs 24 surrounding pitches; Rapids 2 room identity unverified.
- **National Western:** Stock Show grounds record vs separately ticketed Coliseum rodeos; new Legacy Building and Livestock Center.
- **Coors Field / Empower Field:** parking sold as separate ticket listings (Coors via Ticketmaster per [guide](https://blog.ticketmaster.com/step-inside-coors-field-denver-co)); suites and club packages may appear as separate products.
- **Red Rocks:** non-concert events (films, fitness, graduations) in the same room ([source](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/?p=336088)) and ~1.1 million free visitors — not concert attendance.
- **Fiddler's Green:** "Englewood" vs "Greenwood Village" in listings.
- **Centennial Stadium:** shares site with the Summit practice facility; capacity changes after 2028.
- **Denver Summit FC:** three home rooms in 2026; never infer room from team name.
- **Bandimere vs Thunder Valley:** both nicknamed "Thunder"; Bandimere is closed.
- **1stBank Center / Broomfield Event Center:** closed; reject any new listing.
- **Folsom Field / BOLDERBoulder:** one event; spectators claim is not a room count.
