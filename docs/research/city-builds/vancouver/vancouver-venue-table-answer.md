# Vancouver venue table — Fan/Friction research answer

**Answer date / as-of:** 2026-10-12 (Monday). All facts are "as of" this date unless a row states otherwise.
**Time zone for all event times:** Pacific Time (PT; PDT in summer, PST in winter).
**Scope of this answer:** public sources only. No app code, flags, formula constants, personal ratings or cost commitments.

**Labels used**

- **Official** = venue, team, league, municipal, university or organizer page.
- **Reported** = news outlet or reputable secondary source (including Wikipedia, which is secondary).
- **Estimated** = a forecast or rough figure ("about", "up to", "expected").
- **Coordinates:** "DMS-sourced" means converted from a published degrees-minutes-seconds value (≈30 m precision). "Approx." means my own map placement, **not source-verified**, precision roughly ±300 m. Verify approximate points before using them for proximity matching.
- **Feed coverage:** I did **not** test any ticketing or schedule feed (Ticketmaster API, SeatGeek, league APIs, etc.) in this pass. Where I list a platform, it is because a source names it as the official seller, not because a feed match was verified. Treat every "feed" cell as *unknown until tested*.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### Recommendation

**Core scope: Metro Vancouver (the regional district) as the working boundary, confirmed as final for friction.** Every 5,000+ fixed setup that draws the Vancouver-branded teams sits inside it: downtown Vancouver (BC Place, Rogers Arena), Hastings Park (Pacific Coliseum, Freedom Mobile Arch), UBC, Burnaby (Swangard), and the Township of Langley (Langley Events Centre, Willoughby Stadium).

Evidence that the Vancouver catchment extends to Langley and Surrey, not just the city proper:

- The Giants kept the "Vancouver" name after moving to Langley, a move described as staying [within Metro Vancouver](https://arenadigest.com/2016/05/03/giants-moving-to-langley-events-centre/).
- Surrey is described as a [Metro Vancouver city](https://www.castanetkamloops.net/news/Metro-Vancouver/622453/Surrey-reaches-agreement-with-Vancouver-Giants-to-anchor-new-10000-seat-arena) in coverage of its planned arena.
- The Massey Theatre in New Westminster describes itself as being at the [geographic centre of the Metro Vancouver area](https://www.masseytheatre.com/?p=1067).
- Swangard, in Burnaby, is described as sitting just outside [Vancouver's official city limits](https://hihostels.ca/en/magazine/spots-to-experience-soccer-culture-in-vancouver-ahead-of-the-world-cup) while hosting three Vancouver-branded teams.

*Gap flagged:* I did not fetch Metro Vancouver's official member-municipality list in this pass. Confirm membership (including UBC/Electoral Area A and Tsawwassen First Nation) from the regional district's site before locking the polygon.

### 5b. Duplicate and matching risks

- **"Rogers" name collision (high risk).** Four different venues carry the Rogers name: Rogers Arena (V02, Vancouver), **Rogers Field at Nat Bailey Stadium** (V08, Vancouver), **Rogers Forum** (E01, Abbotsford, since 2025), and **Rogers Place** (Edmonton, used for Goldeneyes "home" games). Match on full name plus coordinates, never on the "Rogers" token.
- **Team-home ≠ venue-in-boundary.** Goldeneyes Takeover Tour home games (e.g., Edmonton, Dec 27, 2025, attendance 10,264 per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Vancouver_Goldeneyes_season)) and the Whitecaps' Langford match would enter a team feed as Vancouver home games.
- **Hastings Park parent grounds.** Coliseum (V03), Agrodome (V12), Freedom Mobile Arch (V04), Forum, racecourse (V25, closed), Fan Festival (B01) and the PNE Fair (B02) share one address (2901 E Hastings St per [PNE](https://www.pne.ca/?p=10010904)). A generic "PNE" listing cannot be assigned to a room without more data.
- **LEC campus.** Arena (V10), Centre Gym (V16), south gym and fieldhouse, plus adjacent Willoughby Stadium (V09) sometimes labelled "at Langley Events Centre" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver_FC)).
- **UBC campus.** Thunderbird Arena vs Father Bauer Arena (900) vs Protrans Arena in the same building ([UBC](https://sportfacilities.ubc.ca/dmc/)); Thunderbird Stadium is a separate building.
- **QET complex.** QET (2,765) and Vancouver Playhouse (668) adjoin ([Civic Theatres](https://vancouvercivictheatres.com/venues/)).
- **VCC.** West halls, West ballroom and East halls are separate rooms in two buildings; combined-hall figures must not be summed with single-hall figures.
- **BC Place secondary listings.** Suites (14–28 or up to 400) and parking or package listings may appear as separate products for one event. The Sun Run party and Pride Pitstop use the building without being stadium events.
- **Freedom Mobile Arch inside larger events.** Fan Festival and PNE Fair concerts sit inside gated grounds events (B01/B02).
- **Time zone shifts.** EliteProspects lists Goldeneyes games in UTC; e.g., "04/15/2026 02:00 UTC" corresponds to the Tuesday, April 14 home game listed by the [PNE](https://www.pne.ca/?p=10010904) ([EliteProspects](https://www.eliteprospects.com/team/44455/vancouver-goldeneyes/2026-2027)). Evening PT games roll into the next UTC date.
- **Ticketing silos (data-rights note).** BC Place uses Ticketmaster ([FAQ](https://www.bcplace.com/faqs/)); Victoria's arena uses its own Select Your Tickets platform ([promoter deck](https://sofmc.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/202005-SOFMC-Promoter-Deck.pdf)). A single-platform feed will miss events at venues with in-house ticketing.
