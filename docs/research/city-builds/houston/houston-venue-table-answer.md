# Houston venue table — Fan/Friction city research

**Answer dated:** Sunday, October 11, 2026 (all "as of" dates below are source dates; pages were accessed October 11, 2026 unless noted).
**Market time zone:** America/Chicago (US Central). **Data:** public sources only. **Scope of this document:** research findings and proposals; no app code, formula constants, ratings or cost commitments.

**How to read labels.** *Official* = venue, team, league, university, government or organizer page. *Reported* = news, trade press, architect/vendor or Wikipedia. *Estimated* = a source that itself calls the figure an estimate or projection. *Proposal* = my recommendation, not a confirmed or scheduled change. *Unknown* = not found in this pass; nothing has been inferred.

**Biggest changes since a 2025-era model would have been built** (each sourced in the tables):

- NRG Stadium/NRG Park became **Reliant Stadium/Reliant Park** in August 2026, after a spring–summer stint as FIFA "Houston Stadium". Three names inside one year.
- Rice Stadium became **First Community Stadium** on October 3, 2026.
- The **WNBA Houston Comets** return to Toyota Center in 2027; Toyota Center is mid-way through a $180M renovation due fall 2027, ahead of the **2028 Republican National Convention**.
- The **UFL left Houston** after the 2026 season (Gamblers at Shell Energy Stadium).
- **Texas Southern football** left Shell Energy Stadium for 2026 (W.W. Thorne Stadium plus one game at Reliant Stadium).
- The **Houston SaberCats** (MLR) withdrew before 2026.

### 1.2 Uncertain edge venues (decide before shipping)

- **Panther Stadium, Prairie View (Waller County).** It is in the statistical metro and holds [15,000](https://pvpanthers.com/facilities/blackshear-field/1), but the crowd is campus-centered.
- **Large ISD high-school stadiums.** These are public, fixed, 8,000–12,000 seats ([Legacy 12,000; Cy-Fair FCU 11,000; Woodforest Bank 10,000](https://kiiitv.com/article/sports/high-school/hs-football/texas-high-school-football-stadiums-most-expensive/287-616a4f40-ba9a-4f19-98d9-4585838950d3)). They host dozens of games a season, mostly without public ticket feeds.
  - **Proposal:** list them (they clear 5,000), but treat them as manual-entry rooms unless a college or pro tenant moves in.
  - W.W. Thorne Stadium is the exception: it is now Texas Southern's primary 2026 home and must be pre-listed.
- **Sam Houston Race Park (north-west Harris County).** It is active, with a [2026–27 Thoroughbred meet starting Dec 18, 2026](https://www.cnhinews.com/news/article_9f5c7f1d-3457-54ee-b2e0-11a4ca49e48f.html), but its grandstand capacity was not found. Status: unknown size.
- **Galveston Island Convention Center / Grand 1894 Opera House.** Both are listed by the City of Houston's [events index (Galveston Opera House)](https://www.houstontx.gov/events/sports.html). Capacity was not verified, so they are left out of Table A.

### 5.2 Duplicate and matching risks

1. **Alias churn at Reliant Park.** One building carried three public names in 2026 (NRG → Houston Stadium → Reliant). The same applies to the Arena, the Center and the Astrodome. Resale and ticketing listings will lag. Map every alias to one room ID with effective dates.
2. **Grounds versus stadium during the rodeo.** Daily grounds counts ([185,785 peak day in 2026](https://www.rodeohouston.com/?p=344072)) include stadium attendees. Keep one grounds-day record and one stadium-show record, and never add them.
3. **Multi-room buildings.** Reliant Arena (arena + pavilion), Wortham (Brown + Cullen), Hobby Center (Sarofim + Zilkha), White Oak (Lawn + Downstairs + Upstairs), the UH complex, and the Berry Center (stadium + arena + theater). Ticketing platforms may group these under one venue.
4. **Shared tenants.** Dynamo and Dash (Shell Energy); Rockets and Comets (Toyota Center, 2027–). Key events by team plus date plus room.
5. **Suites, parking and packages.** Reliant Stadium [suites](https://www.reliantpark.com/reliant-stadium/), Toyota Center premium-club products, and Pavilion lawn/reserved tiers are sold as separate listings for the same event.
6. **Configuration-dependent rooms.** Smart Financial Centre (1,900–6,400) and the GA floor at 713 Music Hall.
7. **Proximity traps.** Daikin Park, Toyota Center, GRB, Discovery Green and Shell Energy are all within a few downtown/EaDo blocks — [Houston First notes three major sports venues near its convention center](https://www.houstonfirst.com/convention-district-transformation/). A geofence alone cannot pick the room.
8. **Name collisions.** "Rice Stadium" (Houston) vs Rice–Totten Stadium (Mississippi). Houston Gamblers (UFL, 2026) vs the 1980s USFL Gamblers.

### 5.5 Coverage findings for data handling

- **Time zone:** all Houston-market events are America/Chicago (CST/CDT). Venue sites list local times.
- **Naming rights churn** is the main data issue in this market. Three venues changed names between Jan 2025 and Oct 2026 (Daikin, Reliant, First Community), plus FIFA's temporary "Houston Stadium" naming.
- **Ticketing fragmentation:** the rodeo uses AXS; Reliant Park marketing uses Ticketmaster; Smart Financial Centre uses ATG; 713 Music Hall is Live Nation. Publicly displayed ticketing relationships say nothing about room-level identity in feeds.
- **Data rights:** the attendance figures here come from organizer press releases. Reuse terms for HLSR, FIFA host-committee and league data were not reviewed.
