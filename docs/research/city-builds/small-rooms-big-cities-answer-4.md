## How to use this installment

The city tables contain researched additions, sorted by the largest cited audience configuration, including clearly flagged missing rooms at or above the app’s large-event threshold. Conflicting maxima are retained rather than silently reconciled. A lower recommended figure can therefore appear in a row sorted using a disputed larger published figure. The unresolved lists are candidates, not additions.

**Deduplication follows your latest message.** Dallas–Fort Worth’s existing large venues and Houston’s listed large and smaller rooms are excluded from the additions tables. Smart Financial Centre, W.W. Thorne Stadium and Sam Houston Race Park were already present with blank capacities in the original brief; they remain existing records and are addressed under Houston updates.

**Evidence labels:** Official means venue/operator, owner, university, district, league or government material. Reported means a named outside publication, tourism listing or venue database. Estimated is reserved for explicit arithmetic, never an inferred fire-code limit. A source’s rounded number remains rounded. Dates, street numbers, coordinates and Ticketmaster identifiers are not capacity claims.

**Entrance coordinates remain a material gap.** A street address is supplied where verified. A few published decimal facility/building points are explicitly labeled; none is represented as a verified main patron entrance. All other entrance coordinates remain unconfirmed. Do not populate an entrance-coordinate field from those fallback points. Finding a location on a general map does not prove its entrance.

Name history is recorded where a dated change was established. “No different room name found” is a limited search result, not certification that every historical alias has been captured. An institutional rename, building name, interior-room name and seller campus label can describe different levels of the same venue. Blank Ticketmaster cells mean no numeric venue-URL identifier was confirmed; event IDs and Live Nation alphanumeric keys are not substituted.

Capacities apply to the named room and configuration. Reception / banquet / whole-property rental limits are identified when relevant but are not treated as public concert capacities. Ticketed public school and college sports count; ordinary free walk-up park concerts do not establish ticketed use. Free reserved tickets, such as Miller’s covered-seat tickets, can qualify.

The report provides supported additions and an explicit remaining verification queue. It is **not a certified exhaustive census** of every small school gymnasium, racetrack grandstand or private rental hall across either metro. No source supports treating every row as fully ready for automatic import: entrance coordinates, configuration details and some name dates still need verification.
