# Columbus venue table — Fan/Friction research answer

**As of:** Sunday, October 11, 2026 · **Time zone for all dates:** US Eastern (America/New_York)
**Scope:** Columbus, Ohio and its connected central-Ohio event catchment. Cleveland and Cincinnati excluded by instruction.

**How to read the labels**

- **Official** = owner, operator, team, league, university, organizer or government page.
- **Reported** = news outlet, reference work, or third-party guide.
- **Estimated** = pre-event projection or organizer claim with no published counting method.
- **Unknown** = no public figure found in this pass. No values were inferred from capacity.
- Capacity is never used as attendance. Attendance figures appear only where a source published one.

**Verification depth (be aware):** figures were verified at search-result level against the linked pages. Only the Ohio Expo Center Celeste Center page was fetched in full. No page was blocked; none required bypassing access. Decimal coordinates are given only where a public source supplied them; every other venue lists a street address for geocoding instead of an invented point.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### Recommendation

Use the **10-county Columbus Metropolitan Statistical Area** as the outer search boundary, with a **Franklin County core**, and treat venues by function rather than by county line.

**Reasoning.** For census purposes the Columbus metro is ten counties: Franklin, Delaware, Licking, Fairfield, Pickaway, Union, Madison, Perry, Morrow and Hocking, with a 2025 estimated population of [2,242,028](https://www.wosu.org/news/2026-03-26/metro-columbus-gains-21-000-residents-according-to-new-census-numbers) (WOSU, March 26, 2026, citing Census estimates). The three satellite counties named in the brief are large in their own right: Delaware County [242,032](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_statistical_areas), Licking County [185,564](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_statistical_areas), Fairfield County [169,752](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_statistical_areas) (2025 estimates). That makes Delaware, Newark and Lancaster part of the actual metro, not marketing stretch.

| Area | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Franklin County incl. Columbus, Dublin, Upper Arlington, Westerville, Obetz | **Core — include** | Holds every 5,000+ fixed room found except Selby, Mid-Ohio and the Delaware fairgrounds. |
| Delaware (Delaware County) | **Include** | In MSA. Hosts Selby Stadium (9,100) and the Little Brown Jug at the county fairgrounds. |
| Newark / Hebron (Licking County) | **Include** | In MSA. Midland Theatre (1,200) is a pre-list room; National Trail Raceway is a motorsport edge case. |
| Lancaster (Fairfield County) | **Include, but no qualifying fixed room found** | In MSA. This pass found no 1,000+ fixed public room; fairgrounds and high-school stadiums are a gap (see §5). |
| Marysville, London, Circleville, Mount Vernon-adjacent towns | Include in boundary; no qualifying venues found | Searched only lightly. |
| Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course, Lexington (Morrow County) | **Edge case — separate record, not default** | Legally in the MSA (Morrow County), but founded by Mansfield-area businessmen and operates as a regional destination circuit. See note below. |
| Cleveland, Cincinnati, Dayton, Eldora, Athens | **Exclude** | Separate metros or distant destination sites. |

### Uncertain edge venues

- **Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course** — located in [Troy Township, Morrow County](https://Www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mid-Ohio), just outside Lexington; the track was [opened in 1962 by Les Griebling and several Mansfield-area businessmen](https://Www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mid-Ohio). Grandstand seating is [12,000, with observation mounds raising capacity to over 75,000](https://www.scca.com/locations/1990905-mid-ohio-sports-car-course) (Reported, SCCA). Recommendation: keep as a separately flagged destination venue so its race weekends can be logged by hand, but do not let it shape the Columbus metro default. Re-check this call if the user's own logs show regular Columbus-origin attendance.
- **National Trail Raceway, Hebron (Licking County)** — drag strip [first opened in 1964 and purchased by the IHRA](https://speedsport.com/drag-racing/ihra/ihra-completes-purchase-of-national-trail/). Historic NHRA crowds of [more than 40,000](https://www.bestwestern.com/en_US/hotels/destinations/united-states/great-lakes/ohio/things-to-do/sports/speedway.html) are reported only by a hotel travel page; no seating capacity found. Include as an open-grounds edge case (Table B), not a fixed room.
- **Fortress Obetz** — inside Franklin County, but most events are community and high-school; capacity sits above 5,000 (see Table A).
- **Muirfield Village Golf Club (Dublin)** — golf course, not a room. Belongs in Table B.

### 5b. Duplicate and matching risks

- **Arena District proximity.** Nationwide Arena, Huntington Park, KEMBA Live! and ScottsMiracle-Gro Field sit side by side; KEMBA's [rental page](https://promowestlive.com/columbus/venue-rental) describes it as next to Nationwide Arena and Huntington Park. Proximity matching will mis-assign events — require exact room identity.
- **KEMBA Live! complex.** Indoor hall, outdoor amphitheater and plaza are [three independent spaces](https://promowestlive.com/our-venues/kemba-live); A&R Music Bar and The Basement are next door and open as pre-party rooms on the same ticket. Log one event per show, attached to the room actually used.
- **Nationwide Arena + OhioHealth Ice Haus.** Attached practice rink; not a seating configuration.
- **Schott full vs. curtained.** Same building, different capacity class.
- **Ohio Stadium + St. John Arena.** The Skull Session at St. John before every home game is a separate room and crowd from the game.
- **Expo Center grounds vs. rooms.** Fair gate total (Table B) vs. Celeste Center concerts (Table A) vs. Historic Crew Stadium (adjacent, own events). Celeste fair concert tickets include fair admission.
- **Historic Crew Stadium festival vs. stadium.** Sonic Temple uses the bowl and outside stages; parking, camping and suite products are sold separately.
- **ScottsMiracle-Gro Field aliases.** Lower.com Field (2021–2025), "New Crew Stadium" pre-opening ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ScottsMiracle-Gro_Field)), and "Columbus Stadium" for LA28.
- **Historic Crew Stadium aliases.** Columbus Crew Stadium, Mapfre Stadium; operator also refers to the OhioHealth Performance Center on the same site.
- **Name collisions.** "Midland Theatre" (Newark, OH) vs. the [Midland Theatre in Kansas City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midland_Theatre); "Columbus Stadium" (LA28 alias) is generic; "Covelli Center" (OSU) vs. Covelli Centre (Youngstown).
- **Package products.** Mid-Ohio season passes bundle GA, grandstand, paddock and parking; Sonic Temple sells parking/camping passes; Schott and Crew venues sell suites. Exclude non-admission listings.
- **Columbus Fury.** Two April 2026 home dates were flagged as possibly moving to the Schott — check each match's actual room.
- **Arnold Sports Festival.** One festival spanning several GCCC rooms and, historically, other venues (the Celeste Center hosted Arnold events in [2015](https://www.ohioexpocenter.com/p/planevent/buildings-and-floorplans/celeste-center)).
