# Toronto venue table answer — Fan/Friction city research

**Answer dated:** Monday, 12 October 2026. Local time zone for all event dates: Eastern Time (America/Toronto), as shown on organizer listings such as the [MTCC Fan Expo listing](https://www.mtccc.com/event/fan-expo-canada/).

**Labels used in every table**

- **O** = official (venue, team, league, government, university, organizer page)
- **R** = reported (news media, Wikipedia, trade press, ticket resellers, aggregators)
- **E** = estimate or projection published by someone (never computed here)
- **Unknown** = not found in a public source during this pass. Nothing has been inferred from capacity, and no attendance has been converted between measures (daily vs multi-day vs peak).

**Coordinates policy.** Decimal coordinates are given only where a public page states them; otherwise the row gives the address and says "geocode needed". No coordinates were invented.

**Feed policy.** A ticketing or listing match is marked verified only where a public page ties that exact room or event to the seller. Everything else is "unknown — not verified".

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### 1.1 Recommendation (proposal, for your city model)

Use **City of Toronto + Peel + York + Halton + Durham (through Oshawa)** as the "Toronto" city, keep **Hamilton as a separate city**, and treat **Clarington's motorsport park as a destination edge** handled by manual records only.

Reasoning from public sources:

- The Toronto census metropolitan area had [6,202,225 people in 2021](https://www.durham.ca/en/regional-government/resources/Documents/Council/CIP/CIP-2022/CIP-04082022.pdf) (O, Durham Region report on the 2021 Census).
- City of Toronto planners describe the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area as roughly approximated by [three separate CMAs: Toronto, Hamilton and Oshawa](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/92e3-City-Planning-2021-Census-Backgrounder-Population-Dwellings-Backgrounder.pdf) (O). So "GTA" is broader than the Toronto CMA.
- The Oshawa CMA is its own metro and [includes Whitby, Oshawa and Clarington](https://www.durham.ca/en/regional-government/resources/Documents/Council/CIP/CIP-2022/CIP-04082022.pdf) (O). It is inside the GTA/Durham working boundary, and its one 5,000-class room (Tribute Communities Centre) hosts a GTA-branded pro lacrosse team, so including it is reasonable; tag it as an edge.
- The GTA's population is larger than the Toronto CMA partly because the GTA also counts [the Oshawa CMA and Burlington, which belongs to the Hamilton CMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_census_metropolitan_areas_and_agglomerations_in_Canada) (R). No 1,000+ room was found in Burlington, so this does not affect the venue list.
- Hamilton is a distinct metro of [785,184 people (2021 CMA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_census_metropolitan_areas_and_agglomerations_in_Canada) (R) with its own large arena and stadium. Keep it separate even though the "Toronto" Rock play there (see Section 5 matching risks).
- Niagara is outside the GTA working boundary; exclude by default.

### 2.3 Rooms whose 1,000 / 5,000 treatment needs care

- **CAA Centre (A12):** City says 5,000; an arena guide says 4,774 seated. Concert setup 3,800. Recommend: friction only for hockey/basketball setups, and flag as borderline.
- **Varsity Stadium (A13):** published figure is exactly 5,000; no standing-room figure found.
- **Great Canadian theatre (A11):** 5,000 only for general admission; seated layouts are 3,637–4,061. Friction label should depend on the event's layout, not the room.
- **Mississauga arena (A10):** official "5,100-seat" but concert layouts can be as low as 3,000.
- **Scotiabank Arena theatre configuration (A4):** 5,200 — still above 5,000, but a very different event from a full house.
- **Coca-Cola Coliseum concerts (A8):** range down to 4,100 for small end-stage shows.
- **Massey Hall (A18):** standing layout (2,800) uses removable floor seats — do not count it as seated capacity.
- **RBC Amphitheatre (A5) and Rogers Stadium (A1):** a large share is lawn or floor standing.
- **Tribute Communities Centre (A9):** official 6,152 vs 5,180 seated.

### 5.2 Duplicate and matching risks

- **Rogers Stadium vs Rogers Centre.** Different venues 15+ km apart; observers already expect [confusion between the two names](https://toronto.skyrisecities.com/forum/threads/toronto-rogers-stadium-at-downsview-m-s-live-nation.40588/) (R). Feeds may also contain Rogers Arena (Vancouver) and Rogers Place (Edmonton).
- **"Toronto Stadium" = BMO Field** for June–July 2026 only.
- **"Toronto" teams outside Toronto:** Rock (Hamilton), Tempo home games in Montreal and Vancouver, Argonauts "home" games in Hamilton/Regina/Winnipeg, Sceptres neutral-site games. Match on venue, not team name.
- **Exhibition Place** holds BMO Field, Coca-Cola Coliseum (inside the Enercare complex), CNE Bandshell, Queen Elizabeth Theatre and exhibition halls; CNE, Carnival and marathon expo use the same grounds.
- **Ontario Place:** RBC Amphitheatre and RBC Echo Beach are neighbours with the same sponsor prefix.
- **"CAA" names:** CAA Centre (Brampton), CAA Theatre and CAA Ed Mirvish Theatre (Toronto), and a CAA Arena in Belleville ([R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_indoor_arenas_in_Canada)).
- **Stacked/shared buildings:** Elgin + Winter Garden; Meridian Arts Centre Main Stage + George Weston Recital Hall; Massey Hall main + TD Music Hall; Varsity Stadium + Varsity Arena; Sobeys Stadium court + outer courts; Woodbine Racetrack + casino theatre; Mississauga SEC main arena + community rinks/SportsPlex/fields.
- **Suites, parking and packages:** resellers list Coliseum [parking passes](https://www.vividseats.com/cocacola-coliseum-tickets/venue/3048); Scotiabank sells [club seats and multiple suite types](https://exclaim.ca/venues/venue/scotiabank_arena) that can appear as separate listings.
- **Old names still in feeds:** Air Canada Centre, Ricoh Coliseum, Hershey Centre, Paramount Fine Foods Centre, Powerade Centre, Budweiser Stage, Molson Amphitheatre, Sony Centre, Toronto Centre for the Arts, Aviva Centre, Rexall Centre, General Motors Centre, FirstOntario Centre, Tim Hortons Field, York United FC, Canon Theatre, Kingswood Music Theatre (closed).
