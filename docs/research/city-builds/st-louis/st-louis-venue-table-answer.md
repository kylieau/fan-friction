# St. Louis — Fixed Venues, Rooms and Crowd Events (Fan/Friction city research)

**Answer date:** Monday, October 12, 2026 (America/Chicago). All facts are as found on this date unless a different as-of date is shown next to the source.
**Thresholds used (from the brief, not changed):** ≥ 5,000 in a real configuration = friction-eligible; ~1,000–4,999 = pre-listed and muted; < 1,000 = hand-log only. Capacity is never treated as attendance.
**Label key:** **Official** = the venue, team, university, league or government states it. **Reported** = press, a tourism bureau, a contractor or a reference work states it. **Estimated** = a ticketing platform, a third-party listing or a rough figure.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### 1.1 Recommendation

The federal definition is the right outer frame. The St. Louis, MO-IL MSA covers St. Louis city plus St. Louis, St. Charles, Jefferson, Franklin, Lincoln and Warren counties in Missouri (with a slice of Crawford County around Sullivan), and Madison, St. Clair, Monroe, Clinton, Jersey, Macoupin, Bond and Calhoun counties in Illinois ([BLS county list for the MSA](https://www.bls.gov/regions/mountain-plains/data/employmentandunemploymentandwages_stlouis_table.htm)). The 2025 MSA population estimate is [2,814,421](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_statistical_areas). The regional planning agency's own map treats a smaller core, about [4,469 sq mi and 2.58 million people](https://www.ewgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/MSA_2024.pdf), as its planning region inside a [7,865 sq mi](https://www.ewgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/MSA_2024.pdf) MSA.

| Tier | Area | Recommendation | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core (pre-list and friction) | St. Louis city; St. Louis County; St. Charles County; Madison County IL; St. Clair County IL | **Include** | Every confirmed venue with a 5,000+ setup found in this pass sits in these five jurisdictions (Downtown/Midtown/Forest Park; Maryland Heights; St. Charles and O'Fallon; Madison and Edwardsville; Sauget). |
| Core (pre-list, nothing large found) | Jefferson County MO; Monroe County IL | **Include** | Inside the planning core and inside the MSA. No 1,000+ fixed venue was confirmed there in this pass, so treat as "searched lightly, nothing found" rather than "empty". |
| Outer MSA ring | Franklin, Lincoln, Warren (and Sullivan area) MO; Clinton, Jersey, Macoupin, Bond, Calhoun IL | **Include as hand-log scope; pre-list only if a venue is later verified** | They are in the MSA by commuting ties, but this pass found no 1,000+ fixed room there. Fairgrounds and high-school/college venues in these counties need a separate check. |
| Excluded destinations | Columbia MO (Mizzou), Carbondale IL (SIU), Sedalia MO (Missouri State Fair), Springfield IL (Illinois State Fair) | **Exclude** | None of these counties is in the St. Louis MSA list above. Sharing a state or a "St. Louis area" travel label is not enough. |
| CSA edge | Farmington micropolitan area (St. Francois County MO) | **Exclude for now** | It is in the wider combined area, [not the MSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_statistical_areas). |

### 5.2 Duplicate and matching risks

- **Energizer Park:** CITY SC and CITY2 share the building — two event streams, one room.
- **America's Center:** the Dome floor, exhibit halls, ballroom and Ferrara Theatre can host parts of the same convention. Map each listing to its exact room.
- **Hollywood Casino Amphitheatre vs Saint Louis Music Park:** both in Maryland Heights near Riverport Drive/Casino Center Drive; proximity matching will confuse them. "Amphitheatre"/"Amphitheater" spellings differ across listings.
- **Enterprise Center vs Stifel Theatre:** adjacent addresses (1401 Clark vs 1400 Market). Do not merge by block.
- **Chaifetz Arena vs Chaifetz Pavilion:** same building complex, very different sizes.
- **Busch Stadium vs Ballpark Village:** watch parties and BPV events must not inherit stadium capacity.
- **WWTR:** NASCAR, Confluence Music Festival, and parking/camping/hospitality products are often listed separately for one weekend; the drag strip, oval and road course are different sub-venues.
- **Suites, parking passes and packages** (Busch has 61 suites and club seats; Enterprise Center sells suites/boxes) should be filtered before counting events.
- **CarShield Field vs Heritage & Freedom Fest:** same complex.
