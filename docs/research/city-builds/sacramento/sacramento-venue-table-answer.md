# Sacramento venue table — research answer

**As of:** Monday, 12 October 2026 (Pacific Time, America/Los_Angeles; PDT until 1 Nov 2026, then PST).
**Scope of work:** public sources only; venue, team, university, government and organizer pages preferred; news and Wikipedia used where official pages were not found and labeled as such.
**Method note:** Sources were read through search-result extracts, not full-page fetches. No page was blocked, but the Sacramento Bee (paywalled) was not used directly; Bee figures appear only where another outlet republished them. Every number below links to the page it came from.

**Labels used**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Official | Venue, team, league, university, government or organizer page |
| Reported | News outlet, trade press, Wikipedia, or a third party republishing an official statement |
| Estimated | Ticket reseller, aggregator, or predictive service; low confidence |
| Derived | Arithmetic from a published figure; shown only to expose a conflict, never used as a capacity |

Capacity is never used as attendance in this file. Where a crowd figure appears, its measure (per-game, multi-day total, ticket count) is stated.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edges

### Recommendation

**Core scope: the four-county Sacramento–Roseville–Folsom MSA (Sacramento, Placer, Yolo, El Dorado counties), with one named Yuba County edge site.**

Reasoning:

- The federal metro definition groups [El Dorado, Placer, Sacramento and Yolo counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacramento_metropolitan_area) as the Sacramento–Roseville–Folsom MSA, population [2,463,127 (ACS 2024 1-year)](https://censusreporter.org/profiles/31000US40900-sacramento-roseville-folsom-ca-metro-area/). Sutter and Yuba counties form a separate Yuba City MSA that sits inside the larger [Sacramento–Roseville CSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_statistical_areas).
- Every professional and Division I home venue found is inside the four-county MSA: downtown Sacramento (Kings), West Sacramento in Yolo County (Athletics, River Cats), Cal Expo (Republic FC), Sac State, and UC Davis in Yolo County.
- The region's only large amphitheatre is in Yuba County. Live Nation describes Toyota Amphitheatre as [roughly 40 miles north of Sacramento, drawing from greater Sacramento, the San Joaquin Valley, Reno and Lake Tahoe](https://livenationentertainment.com/?p=26244). That is evidence it functions as the Sacramento market's amphitheatre, so it is recommended as an **included edge**, not a core site.
