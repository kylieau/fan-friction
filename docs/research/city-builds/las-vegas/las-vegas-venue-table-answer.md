**Answer date:** Monday, October 12, 2026 (all "as-of" dates below are the publication or retrieval date of the linked page; retrieval for every link was October 12, 2026).
**Market time zone:** Pacific (America/Los_Angeles). See §5 for UTC-shift artifacts in resale feeds.
**Scope of this file:** public sources only. No app code, flags, formula constants, ratings or cost commitments. Proposals are marked **PROPOSAL**; confirmed/scheduled items are marked **CONFIRMED** or **SCHEDULED**.

**Label key:** *official* = venue/team/league/operator/government page; *reported* = news outlet; *secondary* = encyclopedia, aggregator, reseller or guide (use with care); *estimated* = a stated estimate by an organizer or reporter.

**Coordinates:** only two venues had decimal or DMS coordinates in the sources retrieved for this pass (PH Live and Orleans Arena). For every other row the official street address is given instead and coordinates are marked *not captured — geocode from the official address*. I did not supply coordinates from memory because they could not be linked to a source.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

**Recommendation: use the Las Vegas Valley urban area (City of Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, and unincorporated Clark County townships including Paradise/the Strip, Winchester, Enterprise, Spring Valley and Summerlin) as the city boundary.** Every fixed 5,000+ venue found sits inside it, including the farthest-out one, Las Vegas Motor Speedway at [7000 Las Vegas Blvd N](https://concerts.vegas/tickets/nascar/), described as roughly [10 miles north of downtown](https://www.ticketfairy.com/events-in-las-vegas--nevada--us/las-vegas-motor-speedway) (secondary).

Reasoning: the catchment for every tenant team and recurring event in this file is the valley itself. Henderson (Lee's Family Forum), Summerlin (Las Vegas Ballpark), North Las Vegas (Craig Ranch) and the north-valley speedway all draw from the same metro population and share the same visitor base. Clark County as a whole is too large: it contains distant towns that are separate event catchments.

**Event clusters (for display/grouping, not scoring):**
- **Strip / resort corridor (Paradise):** Allegiant Stadium, T-Mobile Arena, Sphere, MGM Grand Garden Arena, Michelob ULTRA Arena, PH Live, Dolby Live, Resorts World Theatre, BleauLive Theater, Las Vegas Festival Grounds, Formula 1 street circuit, future Athletics stadium.
- **UNLV / off-Strip west and south:** Thomas & Mack Center, Orleans Arena, South Point Arena.
- **Downtown Las Vegas:** Downtown Las Vegas Events Center, Cashman Field (closing), Fremont Street events.
- **Outlying valley:** Las Vegas Motor Speedway (north), Craig Ranch Amphitheater (North Las Vegas), Lee's Family Forum (Henderson), Las Vegas Ballpark (Summerlin).

### Double-count / matching flags

- **Allegiant Stadium:** separate suite pages ([suites.allegiantstadium.com](https://www.allegiantstadium.com/)), transportation/parking links and the Wynn Field Club appear beside each event on the official site. Treat as one event; ignore suite/parking listings.

- **T-Mobile Arena vs. Toshiba Plaza:** plaza activations are not arena events.
- **Thomas & Mack vs. Cox Pavilion:** adjacent rooms, separate capacities.
- **NFR vs. DLVEC watch parties vs. Cowboy Christmas (LVCC):** NFR week spawns parallel downtown watch parties ([arrive listing](https://las-vegas-microsite.arrive.com/venues/downtown-las-vegas-events-center-parking)) and a convention-center gift show. These are separate events, not NFR attendance.
- **South Point:** main arena vs. three other competition arenas in the same complex.
- **Las Vegas Ballpark:** Aviators vs. Athletics games on the same field; both real, distinct events.

- **Split ticketing platforms:** Allegiant/Raiders on Ticketmaster ([official](https://www.allegiantstadium.com/)); MGM Grand Garden Arena and NFR on AXS ([AXS](https://www.axs.com/venues/101076)); Orleans Arena exclusive Ticketmaster ([guide](https://venuecoalition.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Orleans-Arena-Production-Guide-July-2024.pdf)); UNLV on its own ticket site. A single-platform feed will miss whole venues.
- **UTC artifacts:** resale feeds show Thomas & Mack and T-Mobile Arena events at "3:30 AM" ([concerts.vegas](https://concerts.vegas/tickets/nascar-cup-series/); [concerts.vegas](https://concerts.vegas/tickets/ucla-bruins/)), which are evening Pacific-time events shifted to UTC; F1 timetables in GMT move the Saturday-night race to Sunday ([RacingNews365](https://racingnews365.com/f1/races/gp-las-vegas)). Normalize to Pacific before date matching.
- **Neutral-site "home" games:** PWHL Las Vegas has two Takeover Tour home games at undetermined neutral sites ([KSNV](https://news3lv.com/sports/pwhl-las-vegas-pro-womens-hockey-team-schedule-first-season-2026-27-sports-nevada-t-mobile-arena)); Athletics home games in West Sacramento. Don't map by team name alone.
- **Attendance definitions:** A's announced attendance excludes team-allotted tickets ([Las Vegas Sun](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2026/jun/14/as-savor-a-sold-out-las-vegas-homestand-as-fans-dr/)); NASCAR publishes none ([NBC Sports](https://nascar.nbcsports.com/2017/03/20/reports-las-vegas-charlotte-speedways-may-cut-seating-capacity/)); festival figures are multi-day totals.
