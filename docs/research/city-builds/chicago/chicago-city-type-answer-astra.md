# Chicago venue arrival modes: evidence and gaps

**Research cutoff: October 7, 2026. Prepared for Fan/Friction.**

## Regional verdict

**Chicago is provisionally an “in between” venue region:** strong rail and neighborhood walking access coexist with large parking campuses and suburban venues; the available public evidence does not establish an attendance-weighted regional car share.

| Classification | Verdict and reason |
|---|---|
| Driving region: most crowds at least 80% car | **Not established.** Suburban access patterns suggest substantial driving, but I found no attendee surveys demonstrating that threshold across most venues. |
| Transit region: most large crowds below 50% car | **Not established region-wide.** Wrigley’s historical estimate supports a non-car majority; comparable measurements are missing for most other buildings. |
| In between | **Best provisional label.** Venue location, event type and shuttle arrangements produce materially different transport environments. This is an evidence-based qualitative judgment, not a measured regional percentage. |

**The principal numerical finding is narrower than the brief hoped for:** a historical Wrigley planning estimate, a before-opening Ryan Field concert projection, and a projection for the future Chicago Fire stadium provide complete splits. Public directions and parking inventories are much more plentiful than current attendee mode surveys. Missing shares below mean **not found**, never zero.

## Definitions and how to use these figures

| Item | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Car | People arriving by private automobile, carpool, taxi, rideshare or hired black car. Percentages describe people, not vehicles. |
| Transit | CTA rail and buses, Metra, Pace, event coaches and shuttles. A walk from a station remains a transit journey. |
| Walk/bike/other | Whole journeys made on foot or bicycle, plus explicitly identified residual modes. Walking from a parking lot does not turn a driver into a walking attendee. |
| Remote parking | A driver who parks remotely and boards a venue shuttle can be counted as transit under a final-leg convention or as car under a principal-trip convention. Both versions are shown where the source permits. Use the latter to estimate road and parking pressure. Do not count the person twice. |
| Official | Published by a public agency, venue or commissioned consultant. This identifies provenance, not whether a figure was observed. |
| Reported | A secondary source or publicly reproduced document. |
| Estimated | A planning assumption, an aggregation calculated here, or an approximate walking time. Each is identified. |
| Walking times | Approximate station-to-venue walks, estimated from location and distance unless expressly attributed to a source. Gates, detours, accessibility needs and crowds can lengthen them. They are not measured event-day travel times. |
| Unknown | No defensible public attendee share located in the sources reviewed. No midpoint or proxy percentage has been invented. |

Parking capacity, parking occupancy, transit availability and agency ridership are supporting evidence; none alone establishes an attendee mode share. A comparable below explains the access setting. **Its percentage should not be copied into another venue’s rating.**

## Observed transit counts: useful evidence, incomplete mode shares

| Public figure / dataset | What it establishes | Why it cannot directly fill the car/transit/walk table |
|---|---|---|
| [CTA L station daily entries](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/cta-ridership-l-station-entries-daily-totals) | Official station-by-day entry totals are available. Candidate stations include Addison, Sox–35th, 35th–Bronzeville–IIT, Damen Green, Illinois Medical District and Roosevelt. | They count **station entries**, frequently the return trip at a venue station. They include non-attendees, lack exact event-time isolation in daily totals, and exclude bus/Metra/walking. Addison has several lines/station records to distinguish. Raw entries divided by announced attendance are not an arrival-mode estimate. |
| [CTA bus daily route totals](https://data.cityofchicago.org/Transportation/CTA-Ridership-Bus-Routes-Daily-Totals-by-Route/jyb9-n7fm) | Official route-day boardings. | Whole-route totals include unrelated passengers; transfers can count a person more than once. A route-level increase is not a unique attendee count. |
| **367 inbound Bears Extra passengers, September 10, 2023**, indexed in [Metra’s September 2023 ridership memo](https://metra.com/sites/default/files/inline-files/September%202023%20Ridership%20Trends%20Memo.pdf) | An official event-service count for a specific train operation. The PDF was indexed but could not be retrieved directly during this research. | Covers only that service, not regular Electric trains, other Metra lines, CTA, Pace or all attendees. It cannot establish Soldier Field’s full transit share. |
| **112,845 park-and-ride ridership over 33 Ravinia events in 2023**, [official Highland Park neighbor-meeting notes](https://cms6.revize.com/revize/highlandparkil/government/boards_and_commissions/docs/2023-11-9%20Notes%20Ravinia%20Festival%20Neighbor%20Meeting%20Notes.pdf) | Substantial use of the parking shuttle program. These are parking-shuttle users, not necessarily Metra riders. |
| **227,700 Metra systemwide one-way trips on Lollapalooza’s Thursday in 2024**, [Metra 2025 budget book](https://assets.metra.com/s3fs-public/2025-03/Brochure_8.5x11_FinalBudgetBook_2025_FINAL_Web5.pdf?VersionId=EkOa2Jt12S3x4ANmHY6i1eGuX_P1Wu.z) | A large system ridership day coinciding with the festival. | Includes ordinary riders across the system and both directions. It is not 227,700 festival arrivals and must not be divided by the festival’s daily crowd. |

**No matched event-day station regression or original attendee survey was conducted for this report.** The daily data are a lead for additional analysis, not an observed complete split already established here.

To estimate a defensible rail share from those datasets, isolate excess ridership against matched non-event weekdays, account for weather/holidays and overlapping events, identify inbound versus outbound movements where possible, remove transfers and round-trip duplication, and use actual attendance for the same event. A remaining car share additionally requires bus, shuttle, walking and cycling evidence. Announced attendance can count sold tickets rather than people who traveled.
