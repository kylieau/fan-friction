Atlanta → Miami → Phoenix. Research cut-off: October 22, 2026. New file; earlier installments are unchanged. Deduplication uses the refreshed rooms-on-file lists supplied for this installment.

**Evidence labels:** **official** = venue, owner, operator, institution or governing body; **reported** = a third-party source; **estimated** = an expressly identified approximation or calculation. These labels apply to audience capacities, not dates, addresses, coordinates or venue IDs. A published official figure is not necessarily a current certified fire-code or staged-show manifest. Each capacity is linked to its source.

One room per row; tables sort by the largest published audience configuration discussed, with uncertain maxima identified. Proposed or unverified movable expansions are not used for sorting; “over” figures sort by their stated lower bound. Fixed chairs, floor admission, reception and whole-property totals are not interchangeable or automatically additive. About 1,000 uses the previous installment’s approximate 950 lower boundary. Amaturo Theater and Abdo New River Room are explicit smaller-room exceptions requested by the original brief’s unmatched-feed list. Existing records are corrected below, never added again under a new alias.

**Verification gaps:** unverified main-entrance decimal points are marked in each row. A postal address is not an entrance coordinate. Empty numeric Ticketmaster-ID cells mean no numeric venue URL ID was verified, not absence of ticketing. Full dated naming histories and all setup variations remain incomplete where stated. The unconfirmed lists are screening queues, not certified additions; this inventory does not claim that every school stadium or subdivisible exhibition hall has been certified.
