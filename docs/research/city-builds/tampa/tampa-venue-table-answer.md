## 0. How to read this file

- **Table A** = fixed buildings/rooms (one row per room). **Table A-2** = access, schedule/feed coverage and double-count flags for the same rows (keyed by ID).
- **Table A-E** = edge-county venues kept out of the recommended metro scope.
- **Table B** = open-site and dated crowd events (not rooms).
- Sections 1–5 at the end answer the five required closing items.
- "Proposal" means not yet approved/scheduled; "Scheduled/approved" means a public body or owner has committed a date or financing.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

**Recommendation (sourced reasoning):**

- **Metro membership = the four-county Tampa–St. Petersburg–Clearwater MSA** (Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco, Hernando), per [HUD](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/periodicals/USHMC/reg/TampaStPetersburgClearwater-FL-HMP-March24.pdf) and Florida's [EDR MSA table](https://edr.state.fl.us/Content/population-demographics/data/MSA-2025Estimate.pdf) (2025 estimate [3,403,788](https://edr.state.fl.us/Content/population-demographics/data/MSA-2025Estimate.pdf)). This matches the federal statistical definition and keeps Plant City and Oldsmar inside.
- **Practical venue catchment = Hillsborough + Pinellas.** Every active 5,000+ fixed setup I verified sits in these two counties. I found **no** verified 5,000+ fixed room in Pasco or Hernando (search was not exhaustive there — re-check county fairgrounds and any motorsport tracks).
- **Exclude Polk and Sarasota/Manatee from metro friction.** They form separate MSAs (Lakeland–Winter Haven; North Port–Sarasota–Bradenton) per the [regional competitiveness report](https://stateoftheregion.com/our-region/). Broader "Tampa Bay region" marketing definitions (7–8 counties) are a label, not a metro.
- **Spring training:** keep Yankees (A07), Phillies (A11) and Blue Jays (A12) as in-metro rooms; treat Tigers (E02), Pirates (E03), Orioles (E04), Rays' Port Charlotte site (E05) and other Grapefruit League parks as **Florida-wide spring-travel**, not metro membership.

**Uncertain edges:** E01 RP Funding Center (8,178, ~35 mi east on I-4) and SUN 'n FUN — both plausibly draw Tampa residents but are outside the MSA; keep as separate "edge" records if you want them pre-listed but muted.
