# Indianapolis venue research for Fan/Friction

**Answer date:** 2026-10-12. Every fact below is "as of" this date unless a different date is shown. Research used public web sources only. Each figure links directly to the page that supports it.

**Labels used in the tables**

- **Official**: from the venue, team, university, league, or government owner.
- **Reported**: from news media or a reference site.
- **Estimated**: the source itself calls the figure an estimate or an expectation.
- **Unknown**: no public source was found. Nothing was inferred.

**Thresholds in this answer:** the tables mark which rooms clear about 1,000 (pre-list) and about 5,000 (friction). The thresholds are your existing model's; this answer does not tune them.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### Recommendation

**Core scope:** Marion County, including the enclave town of Speedway, plus the connected counties that actually contain qualifying fixed venues: Hamilton County (Noblesville, Fishers, Carmel, Westfield) and Hendricks County (Brownsburg).

**Reasoning:**

- The federal metro is the [11-county Indianapolis–Carmel–Greenwood MSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_metropolitan_area). It was renamed from Indianapolis-Carmel-Anderson in the [July 2023 OMB delineations](https://incontext.indiana.edu/2023/nov-dec/article1.asp).
- Every 5,000+ fixed venue found inside that MSA sits in Marion, Hamilton, or Hendricks County. The other MSA counties can stay in the search area, but this research found no qualifying fixed venue there. That is a coverage finding, not proof that none exist (see section 5).
- Speedway, home of Indianapolis Motor Speedway, is an [enclave suburb inside Indianapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Brickyard_400). Treat it as core, not as an edge case.

**Bloomington (Monroe County) and West Lafayette (Tippecanoe County):** assess separately, outside the core. Recommend a separate "edge catalog" rather than merging them in.

- Neither county appears among the metro and micro areas that make up the [Indianapolis–Carmel–Muncie Combined Statistical Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_metropolitan_area). That CSA lists Indianapolis, Muncie, Columbus, Kokomo, New Castle, Seymour, Crawfordsville, Greencastle, Peru and Greensburg.
- They share the state, not the commuting metro. That matches your rule against including distant destinations just because they share a state or marketing label.
- **Exception to keep:** Indiana University baseball has played [one game a year at Victory Field since 2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victory_Field). That game is an in-scope event at an in-scope venue.

## 5. Conflicts, duplicate risks, inaccessible sources, and changes through 2028

### Conflicting sources (preserved, not averaged)

- **Lucas Oil Stadium:** 67,000 (official site) versus 63,000 (IBJ 2012 and current Wikipedia). The observed 2023 attendance of 67,842 suggests standing room and suites push actual crowds past either seat figure.
- **Gainbridge Fieldhouse:** 17,274 versus 17,923 versus 20,000.
- **IMS grandstands:** about 240,000–275,000. A Front Office Sports page dated 2026 says the ["event is not expecting a full sellout of its 240,000 reserved grandstand seats"](https://frontofficesports.com/article/indy-500-on-pace-for-best-attendance-in-years/). That contradicts the confirmed 2026 sellout, so the page content appears to be older than its date; it was not relied on.
- **Corteva Coliseum:** 6,800 versus 8,200 versus 6,500.
- **Carroll Stadium:** 12,111 versus 10,000.
- **Everwise Amphitheater:** 6,000 (official) versus 3,000 (Songkick).
- **Hilbert Circle Theatre:** 1,660 versus 1,781.
- **State Fair total:** 800,461 versus 800,641.
- **Morris Arena:** 4,000 versus 4,200 versus 4,500.

None of these conflicts changes the 5,000 classification **except Everwise** (seats versus lawn).

### Duplicate and matching risks

- **"Lucas Oil" name collision:** Lucas Oil Stadium (V02), Lucas Oil Indianapolis Raceway Park (V03), and the Lucas Oil 500 Festival Parade ([title partner from 2026](https://youarecurrent.com/2026/02/05/lucas-oil-named-500-festival-sponsor/)).
- **Multi-room buildings and grounds:**
  - Old National Centre: V16 and V18 are separate rooms.
  - Butler campus: V08, V12 and V17 are separate.
  - IU Indianapolis Sports District: V07, V15 and V21 are separate.
  - The State Fairgrounds hold the coliseum, the Free Stage and the fair itself.
  - IMS holds the oval, road course, Snake Pit and camping.
- **Rename chains feeds may still use:**
  - Riverview Health Arena: the old names "Noblesville Event Center" and "The Arena at Innovation Mile."
  - Corteva Coliseum: "Indiana Farmers Coliseum."
  - Everwise Amphitheater: "TCU Amphitheater."
  - Grand Park: one source calls it ["Droplight Grand Park Sports Campus"](https://roundtable.io/sports/nfl/colts/news/colts-release-entire-training-camp-schedule-including-joint-practices). That name is unverified; check before adding it as an alias.
- **City-label mismatch:** Brownsburg's track is listed as "Indianapolis" on ticket resale sites.
- **Listings that can double-count one event:** suites, parking, camping, pit passes, party decks, and lawn versus pavilion tickets.
