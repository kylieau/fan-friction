**Reading the evidence.** **Official** means a venue, owner, operator, university or governing body's published figure; **reported** means a third-party account; **estimated** means an explicitly identified approximation or calculation. Official is not a guarantee of a current fire-code certificate. Audience capacities carry these labels; dates, addresses, coordinates and Ticketmaster identifiers are not capacity numbers. Sources are linked beside the relevant claim.

Tables contain researched additions, largest published room configuration first; incompatible setups are not added together. An unresolved conflict remains visible. “About 1,000” includes named rooms with a verified total at least approximately 950; smaller rooms are recorded below. A ≥5,000 flag identifies an omission from the refreshed file. Some rows are provisional because the precise current layout or operating programme still needs confirmation. Do not import a provisional or historical value as a certified current limit.

**Verification limits:** main-entrance decimal points have not been verified in this installment. The coordinate column records that gap and any useful entrance guidance; a building point is expressly distinguished from an entrance. Empty Ticketmaster cells mean no numeric venue URL ID was established, not that the room has no ticket feed. Generic name-history wording means no alternative was found, not proof that every alias since 2016 has been recovered. Unconfirmed rooms below remain outside the addition tables. This is a researched inventory with an explicit unresolved queue, not a claim that every school stadium or subdivisible exhibition room has been certified.
