## Reading the results

- Coordinates are WGS84 decimal degrees, latitude first. Each numeric coordinate is explicitly labelled **reported** or **estimated**.
- **Reported** means the linked public map record or venue page supplies that coordinate. OpenStreetMap/Nominatim feature points and ArcGIS named-place pins are representative locations; they are not automatically front doors. The point-type column identifies the physical object represented.
- **Estimated** means I calculated a point from the identified OpenStreetMap building, grandstand or playing-field outline. Each estimated row states its method. These are map anchors, not surveyed entrances.
- Addresses are **reported** venue/school addresses. A campus or shared-property address is explicitly labelled where applicable; it is not the basis for substituting a campus-centre coordinate.
- A shared building point does **not** make two rooms aliases. In particular, Mana’s White and Black Rooms and Broward’s Amaturo and Abdo rooms remain separate venue records.
- **Not found** means a sufficiently specific point was not established in this research, not that the room does not exist. Closure, relocation, repurposing and stale-name issues are stated separately. Current names already supplied in the request are distinguished from their historical aliases.
- Final sources are public venue, school, municipal, event-operator and map records. No Wikipedia page is used as a final source. Some direct page loads failed; affected rows state the failure and any public indexed evidence used.
