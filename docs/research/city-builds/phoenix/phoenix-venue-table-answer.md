# Phoenix venue table — research answer for Fan/Friction

**As of:** Sunday 11 October 2026. Local time zone for every date below is America/Phoenix, which event listings show as UTC−7 ([PredictHQ listing metadata](https://www.predicthq.com/major-events/top-events/barrett-jackson-collector-car-auction)). Arizona does not move its clocks in March/November, so feeds that assume a US daylight-saving shift will be off by one hour for roughly half the year; check feed offsets.

**Scope of this pass.** Public sources only. Every number links to the page it came from. Where a figure could not be sourced it is written as *unknown*, never estimated. Capacity is never used as attendance. Proposals are separated from confirmed or scheduled changes (Section 5).

**Gaps in this pass, stated up front.** Decimal coordinates were not captured from any source; each row gives the sourced street address or cross-street so it can be geocoded (source precision = street address or intersection). Ticketmaster/AXS/feed matches were not verified at room level; where a ticketing listing was observed it is noted as "listing observed", not "verified match". Several 1,000–5,000 rooms are named as candidates without capacities because they were not verified.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

**Recommendation (proposal):** Use **all of Maricopa County** as the core scope, **plus tribal-community venues with Valley street addresses** (Salt River Pima-Maricopa Indian Community and Gila River Indian Community), and treat **Pinal County venues as named edge cases**, not as a blanket inclusion. Tucson and distant destination sites stay outside.

Reasoning from sources:

- The Cactus League, Arizona Fall League and all 2026–27 big-league venues sit inside the Maricopa County cities you listed. MLB describes the ten spring parks as spread "across the greater Phoenix area" ([MLB.com, 24 Aug 2026](https://www.mlb.com/news/explore-arizona-fall-league-stadiums)); the farthest-west park, Surprise Stadium, is described as roughly 40 minutes from downtown Phoenix ([MLB Spring Training ballparks](https://www.mlb.com/spring-training/ballparks)).
- Two important venues are on tribal land rather than in a city's jurisdiction: Salt River Fields (built with the Salt River Pima-Maricopa Indian Community, [MLB.com](https://www.mlb.com/news/explore-arizona-fall-league-stadiums)) and Firebird Motorsports Park (Gila River Indian Community, Chandler mailing address, [NHRA](https://nhra.com/news/2024/it-looks-long-future-firebird-motorsports-park-repaves-its-dragstrip)). Phoenix Rising also played on Gila River land in 2021–22 ([Phoenix Rising FC](https://www.phxrisingfc.com/news/phoenix-city-council-unanimously-approves-lease-for-phoenix-rising-fc-to-relocate-its-stadium-to-38th-street-and-washington)). A strict city or county filter could drop these; include them explicitly.
- Name traps: "Talking Stick Resort Amphitheatre" is *not* on tribal land despite the sponsor ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talking_Stick_Resort_Amphitheatre)); "Phoenix Raceway" is in Avondale; the "Phoenix Open" is in Scottsdale.
- I did not fetch an official metro-area (MSA) definition in this pass; if you want the boundary tied to a statistical definition, re-check the OMB/Census Phoenix metro delineation before locking it.

### 5b. Duplicate and matching risks

- **"Talking Stick" collision:** Talking Stick Resort Amphitheatre (current), Talking Stick Resort *Arena* (Mortgage Matchup Center's 2015–2020 name), and Salt River Fields *at Talking Stick* are three different venues ([KTAR](https://ktar.com/arizona-news/phoenixs-ak-chin-pavilion-renamed-as-talking-stick-resort-amphitheatre)). Match on address, not name.
- **Mortgage Matchup Center has six names since 2015**, including the interim "PHX Arena" used twice (2020 and 2025) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortgage_Matchup_Center)). Historic logs under Footprint Center / PHX Arena must map to one venue ID.
- **"Desert Diamond"** = arena (Glendale) and the sponsor's casinos nearby ([Sports Travel](https://www.sportstravelmagazine.com/glendale-arena-rebranded-as-desert-diamond-casino-arena)).
- **"Gila River"** = former arena name and the Indian Community that hosts Firebird.
- **Shared spring parks:** five parks host two clubs each; one game = one event even though both clubs list it. Split-squad days put the same club in two parks on one date ([Rangers](https://www.mlb.com/rangers/press-release/press-release-texas-rangers-announce-2027-spring-training-schedule)).
- **Suite/parking listings:** Mortgage Matchup suite packages bundle garage passes ([TM](https://blog.ticketmaster.com/step-inside-mortgage-matchup-center-phoenix-az/)); State Farm, Chase and the Raceway also sell parking separately — suppress parking-only and suite-only listings.
- **Parent/child event listings:** WM Phoenix Open parent + seven daily AXS events ([AXS](https://www.axs.com/sg/venues/128299)); Barrett-Jackson daily resale listings; State Fair concert tickets that include fair entry.
- **Same-grounds parents:** Fairgrounds (State Fair, County Fair, Coliseum); WestWorld (Barrett-Jackson, horse shows); TPC Scottsdale (Phoenix Open, its concert stage).
- **Resale-first listings:** TickPick and similar sites list 2027 events before organizers confirm (Barrett-Jackson, NHRA). Treat as *unconfirmed* until organizer or league confirms. These marketplaces are commercial data sources; reuse terms were not reviewed.
