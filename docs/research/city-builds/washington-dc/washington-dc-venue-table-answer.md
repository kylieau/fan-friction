# Washington, DC — Venue Table Research Answer (Fan/Friction)

**Answer date:** 12 October 2026. **Time zone for all event times:** US Eastern (ET).
**Scope of this pass:** public web sources retrieved on 12 Oct 2026. Every numeric figure below links to the page it came from. Labels: **official** (venue, team, university, league, government, organizer), **reported** (news, trade or reference sites), **estimated** (a source's own estimate). Where sources disagree, both figures are kept.

> **Reading rule.** Capacity is not attendance. Where attendance appears, it is in its own column and named by measure (average, single-game, weekend total, participants). Nothing here converts one into the other.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### Recommended scope

| Zone | Recommendation | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| District of Columbia | **Core** | Contains Capital One Arena, Nationals Park, Audi Field, CareFirst Arena, the National Mall and the RFK redevelopment site. |
| Prince George's County, MD | **Core** | Holds the Commanders' current dated home ([Northwest Stadium, Landover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwest_Stadium)), [SECU Stadium and Xfinity Center in College Park](https://umterps.com/sports/2018/6/14/secu-stadium.aspx), [Prince George's Stadium in Bowie](https://www.milb.com/chesapeake/community/stadium-info/) and the [MGM National Harbor theater in Oxon Hill](https://scenoplus.com/en/projects/detail/the-theater-at-mgm-national-harbor/). |
| Montgomery County, MD | **Core** | Strathmore (North Bethesda) and the Fillmore (Silver Spring) are DC-market rooms. No 5,000+ fixed room was confirmed in this pass. |
| Arlington County, VA | **Core (event geography)** | No 5,000+ fixed room confirmed, but the [Marine Corps Marathon starts and finishes in Arlington](https://www.marinemarathon.com/event/marine-corps-marathon/weekend-events). |
| City of Alexandria, VA | **Core (no qualifying room found)** | The proposed Potomac Yard arena for the Wizards/Capitals did not proceed, and the teams stayed in DC under the [Capital One Arena deal](https://frontofficesports.com/article/mystics-capitalize-on-wnba-surge-move-4-games-to-larger-arenas/). No 1,000+ fixed room was verified in this pass. |
| Fairfax County / City of Fairfax, VA | **Core** | [EagleBank Arena (George Mason)](https://www.eaglebankarena.com/arena-info/about-eaglebank-arena) and [Wolf Trap's Filene Center (Vienna)](https://www.wolftrap.org/about/venues/filene-center.aspx). |
| Loudoun County, VA | **Include, edge** | [Segra Field, Leesburg](https://EN.wikipedia.com/wiki/Segra_Field) sits exactly at the 5,000 threshold. |
| Prince William County, VA | **Include, edge (recommended addition)** | Not on the starting list, but [Jiffy Lube Live in Bristow](https://www.visitpwc.com/listing/jiffy-lube-live/325/) is described by its county tourism office as about [36 miles from DC](https://www.visitpwc.com/listing/jiffy-lube-live/325/) and is the region's largest amphitheater. Leaving it out would drop the DC market's biggest concert lawn. |
| Howard County, MD | **Edge, shared DC/Baltimore catchment** | [Merriweather Post Pavilion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merriweather_Post_Pavilion) draws [about 90% of attendees from outside Howard County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merriweather_Post_Pavilion). Destination DC lists it as a [DC-area venue](https://mp.washington.org/find-dc-listings/merriweather-post-pavilion), but that is a marketing listing. Assign it to exactly one city model (DC *or* Baltimore) so its events are not counted twice. |
| Anne Arundel County (Annapolis), MD | **Edge, recommend exclude from DC core** | [Navy–Marine Corps Memorial Stadium](https://stadiumdb.com/stadiums/usa/navy_marine_corps_stadium) is 5,000+ but sits on the Annapolis/Baltimore side. Keep it as an edge record rather than including it automatically because it shares a state. |
| Baltimore City / County | **Separate candidate, not in scope** | The Mystics' 2025 games at [CFG Bank Arena in Baltimore](https://frontofficesports.com/article/mystics-capitalize-on-wnba-surge-move-4-games-to-larger-arenas/) are DC-team events in another market. Record them under Baltimore if that model exists. |

**Not verified in this pass:** the federal (OMB) metro-area delineation file. The recommendation above rests on venue location and audience evidence, not on an official MSA list. Re-check against the OMB delineation before locking the boundary.

### Uncertain edge venues (summary)

- **Merriweather Post Pavilion:** shared catchment; risk of double-assignment to DC and Baltimore.
- **Jiffy Lube Live:** outside the starting county list; recommended for inclusion.
- **Navy–Marine Corps Memorial Stadium:** Annapolis; recommended as an edge record.
- **Segra Field and the Charles E. Smith Center:** both sit exactly at 5,000 (see §2b).
