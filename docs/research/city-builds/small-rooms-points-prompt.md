# Research brief: map points for 137 small rooms

Status: open. Written Oct 23, 2026 by Claude Code, at Kylie's request (Oct 23, 2026: "send me the short research for small-room points"). For any research model that can browse.

## What this is for
Fan/Friction is a personal log of live events. An earlier research series listed these rooms (concert halls, theaters, college gyms and fields, mostly 1,000 to 5,000 seats) but gave no map point, and an OpenStreetMap search could not place them reliably. The app needs one point per room to put it on the map and to match ticket listings to it.

## The question
For **each room in the table**, give:
1. **A decimal coordinate pair** (latitude, longitude) for the room itself: the building, stadium or the room's own entrance, not a campus or city centre. Say what the point represents (main entrance, building outline, field) and where it came from (OpenStreetMap feature, the venue's own map, a university campus map; link it).
2. **The street address** the venue or school publishes, with a link.
3. If the room **is the same as another room in the table or a better-known name** (a gym inside a named arena building, a hall inside a complex), say so.
4. If the room **is closed, renamed or not a public room**, say so with a source, and give no point.

Keep the table's order and one row per room. Columns: city, room, latitude, longitude, point type, address, source, note. Label every figure **reported** (a map or listing gives it) or **estimated** (your own placement, with the method). Do not guess a point from a town name; say "not found" instead.
