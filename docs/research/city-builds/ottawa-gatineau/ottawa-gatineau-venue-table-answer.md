# Ottawa–Gatineau venue and crowd-event research — Fan/Friction city model

**Answer date:** Monday, October 12, 2026 (all "current" statements are as of this date unless a row says otherwise)
**Time zone:** both sides of the river (Ontario and Quebec) run on Eastern Time, so one time zone covers the whole recommended scope.
**Method:** public web sources only. Venue, team, league, government, university and organizer pages were preferred; trade press and Wikipedia were used where no primary page surfaced, and each such figure is labelled *reported* (secondary) rather than *official*. Nothing here is app code, a formula constant, a personal rating or a cost commitment.

**Labels used throughout**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Official** | Published by the venue, owner, team, league, government or organizer |
| **Reported** | Published by press, an aggregator or Wikipedia; not confirmed against a primary page in this pass |
| **Estimated** | Explicitly described by the source as an estimate, average or "about" |
| **Confirmed** | A change that has happened or been formally announced by the responsible party |
| **Scheduled** | Approved and in progress with a published timeline |
| **Proposal** | Announced intent or plan without a committed date, or a statement by a consultant/third party |

Thresholds applied (from the brief, not tuned): rooms around **1,000+** are pre-listed; only setups of **5,000+** feed friction; rooms under about 5,000 may be listed but muted. **Capacity is never used as attendance**, and attendance is given only where a source publishes a count.

## 1. Recommended boundary (summary first; detail in section 5)

**Recommendation: treat the City of Ottawa and the Ville de Gatineau together as one event metro**, with every room assigned to its municipality, and keep surrounding municipalities as named edge cases rather than automatic members.

- **Core scope — City of Ottawa** (includes Kanata, Orléans, Nepean and the rural wards such as Carp). Kanata has been inside the City of Ottawa since the 2001 amalgamation, which is why the Canadian Tire Centre is an Ottawa venue despite sitting about [26.2 km from downtown](https://www.si.com/onsi/breakaway/news-feed-page/ottawa-senators-buy-land-new-arena) ([amalgamation history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Tire_Centre)).
- **Core scope — Ville de Gatineau** (includes Hull, Aylmer, Gatineau sector, Buckingham, Masson-Angers).
- **Why the cross-river grouping holds for events, not just by label:** in 2021, [26.8% of Gatineau residents in the labour force worked across the river in Ontario, versus 2.8% of Ottawa workers going the other way](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/o1/en/plus/7803-tale-two-cities-one-national-capital-region-ottawa-gatineau-glance) (Statistics Canada). In 2019 about [51,000 interprovincial bridge trips were made in the morning rush hour](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/news/2025/01/an-interprovincial-link-expected-for-over-100-years-in-the-national-capital-region.html) (PSPC/NCC). Event operators themselves sell into one market: the NLL described the Black Bears' market as the ["Ottawa-Gatineau region"](https://www.oursportscentral.com/services/releases/nll-issues-statement-on-the-ottawa-black-bears/n-6379764), the Senators' owners describe LeBreton as an event centre for ["our Ottawa-Gatineau community"](https://ncc-ccn.gc.ca/news/joint-statement-from-the-national-capital-commission-and-capital-sports-development-inc), and Canada Day and Winterlude are run as single festivals across [official sites in both cities](https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/news/2026/05/in-the-mood-to-celebrateunveiling-the-program-for-canada-day-2026.html).
- **The flow is asymmetric** (mostly Gatineau → Ottawa). That supports one pool for Ottawa's big rooms, but it does *not* prove Ottawa residents travel to Gatineau's mid-size rooms at the same rate. This is a reason to keep Gatineau rooms muted by size (they are all under 5,000), not a reason to exclude them.
- **Statistics Canada reference frame:** the Ottawa–Gatineau CMA had [1,488,307 people in 2021, of whom 353,293 were in the Quebec portion](https://ottawa.ca/en/living-ottawa/statistics-and-demographics/2021-census); the 2021 CMA is reported to include [Arnprior and Carleton Place](https://citypopulation.de/en/canada/metroottawa/505000104__ct_0001_04/) (reported). The CMA is a useful outer limit for catchment, but it is wider than where the fixed event venues actually are: every 5,000+ room found in this pass is inside the City of Ottawa.

**Edge cases kept separate (not automatically included):** Luskville Dragway (Municipality of Pontiac, QC), Calabogie Motorsports Park (Renfrew County, ON — a distant destination, excluded), and anything in Kingston, Cornwall, Montreal or Mont-Tremblant (outside the metro; share only a province or marketing label). See section 5.

### 9b. Duplicate and matching risks

- **Same address, different rooms:** A1 and A5 both use 1015 Bank Street; A6 will be on the same site. Match on room name, never on address or geofence alone. Aggregators already mislabel arena games as stadium events ([613today](https://613today.ca/events/2026-05-12-blackjacks-vs-surge)).
- **Name collisions:** "Rogers Centre" (Toronto) vs Rogers Centre Ottawa; "Slush Puppie Place" (Kingston) vs Centre Slush Puppie (Gatineau).
- **Old names still live in listings:** EY Centre → Cohere Centre; RCGT Park → Ottawa Stadium; Shaw Centre → Rogers Centre Ottawa.
- **Bilingual pairs:** Centre Canadian Tire / Canadian Tire Centre; Stade Place TD / TD Place Stadium; Winterlude / Bal de Neige; FMG / Gatineau Hot Air Balloon Festival. Gatineau sources are often French-only.
- **Add-ons and sub-spaces:** Ticketmaster "Add-Ons" (parking, packages) on venue 401513; TD Place club/patio rentals; CTC suites and restaurants; casino bars and the Hard Rock Cafe stage; festival stages inside Bluesfest.
- **Team moves:** Charge events before Dec 2026 belong to A5 (or A2 for specific games); from 2026-27 to A2. Black Bears: no events after the 2025-26 season.
