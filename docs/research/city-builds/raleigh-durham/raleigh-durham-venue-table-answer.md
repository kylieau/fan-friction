**Answer date:** Monday, October 12, 2026 (America/New_York). All "as-of" dates below are the publication or announcement date of the linked source unless stated.

**Scope reminder (from the brief):** pre-list ~1,000+; only 5,000+ setups feed friction; capacity ≠ attendance. This file fits the existing city model and does not propose formula changes.

**Status labels used:** **Confirmed** = in effect now per a source; **Scheduled** = dated change with construction/contract in place; **Proposal** = under study or unfunded. **Official** = owner/team/university/government figure; **Reported** = media or listing figure; **Estimated** = organizer or media estimate.

**Coordinates:** only given where a fetched source published them. Everything else is marked *unknown — geocode from the sourced address*. No coordinates were filled from memory.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### 1.1 Recommendation

Use **one app metro, "Raleigh–Durham (Triangle)"**, whose core is **Wake, Durham and Orange counties** (Raleigh, Durham, Chapel Hill, Cary and their suburbs), plus the remaining counties of the two federal MSAs (Johnston, Franklin, Chatham, Person) as in-scope but currently venue-light.

Reasoning, with sources:

- Federal statistics split the area into two MSAs but join them in one combined area. The 2023 delineation puts Franklin, Johnston and Wake in the [Raleigh-Cary MSA](https://www.osbm.nc.gov/facts-figures/geography/core-based-statistical-area-reference-maps/2023-core-based-statistical-area-maps) and Chatham, Durham, Orange and Person in the [Durham-Chapel Hill MSA](https://www.commerce.nc.gov/news/the-lead-feed/north-carolinas-changing-msas); both sit inside the [Raleigh-Durham-Cary CSA](https://www.osbm.nc.gov/facts-figures/geography/core-based-statistical-area-reference-maps/2023-core-based-statistical-area-maps) together with the Anderson Creek (Harnett), Henderson (Vance) and Sanford (Lee) micropolitan areas.
- The CSA's 2025 population estimate is [2,484,238](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_statistical_areas), with [1,595,720](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_statistical_areas) in the Raleigh-Cary MSA and [625,485](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_statistical_areas) in the Durham-Chapel Hill MSA (secondary compilation of Census estimates).
- Event catchment behaves as one market: Durham is roughly [20 miles](https://www.andrewclem.com/Baseball/DurhamBullsAthleticPark.html) from Raleigh; when the Mudcats franchise tried to move into Raleigh, the Durham Bulls [blocked it with their territorial rights](https://www.baseball-reference.com/bullpen/Carolina_Mudcats), i.e., organized baseball treats Raleigh as inside Durham's territory; Raleigh-based Shaw University plays football in Durham at [Durham County Memorial Stadium](https://www.durhamncsports.com/facilities/durham-county-memorial-stadium); and the region's NWSL team plays in Cary.
- **Do not split Raleigh and Durham into separate app metros.** The federal MSA split is a commuting-threshold artifact, not an event-market boundary; the three university programs, the NHL team and minor-league baseball all draw across Wake/Durham/Orange.

### 1.3 Uncertain edge venues (in-boundary but need care)

- **Nomaco Park (formerly Five County Stadium), Zebulon** — inside Wake County (county owns [85%](https://www.wake.gov/news/wake-county-zebulon-approve-new-operator-five-county-stadium)), so in scope, but the tenant changed from affiliated MiLB to summer-collegiate and its current capacity was not verified this pass.
- **Durham County Memorial Stadium** — hosts a Raleigh school's football (Shaw) in Durham; in scope.
- **First Horizon Stadium at WakeMed Soccer Park, Cary** — in scope; the men's tenant is on hiatus and the women's tenant may move to a proposed Raleigh stadium (Proposal, §6).
