## Notes for whoever turns this into app data (build rules from the brief)

- Every venue ID and team ID must be unique across all cities. `giants`, `jets`, `rangers`, `kings` and `cardinals` are already taken, so add a city prefix (e.g. `por-…`). Portland needs this in particular: four ESPN teams share the abbreviation **POR** (Blazers, Fire, Timbers, Thorns; see §3). Use league plus team, never the abbreviation alone, as the key.
- Don't make a separate per-city opponents file. Opponent draws go into `expectedDrawIndex` like every other city.
- Never guess a capacity. A missing or range-only figure stays blank and goes on the city gaps list.
- Car shares: wire measured ones; wire sourced estimates marked estimated; leave the rest on the city default. (No car-share work was done here; the city-type answer was not provided.)
- **Feed attendance zeros:** ESPN's MLS feed returns `0` for some 2026 Timbers home matches ([ESPN feed](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/soccer/usa.1/teams/9723/schedule?season=2026)). Treat `0` as "not reported", never as a crowd of zero. This is the Tampa Bay problem in a smaller form.
