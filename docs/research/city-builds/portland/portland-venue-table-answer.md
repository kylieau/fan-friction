# Portland venue table — Fan/Friction research answer

**As of:** 2026-10-12 (Monday). All times America/Los_Angeles (Oregon and Washington are both Pacific). Every figure links to the page it came from; items I could not source are marked **unknown** or **not verified**.

**Labels used**
- **official** = venue owner/operator, team, league, government or university page. **reported** = news outlet, encyclopedia or aggregator. **estimated** = a source's own approximation ("about", "nearly").
- **Coordinate precision:** `GP` = Google Places point retrieved 2026‑10‑12 for the named place (building/property centroid; no static public URL, so re-verify against a public source before storing — see data-rights note in §5). `WP` = Wikipedia infobox (decimal places as published).
- Capacity is never attendance. Attendance figures appear only in the "observed attendance" column or in Table B, with their count type.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

**Recommendation: keep the four-county core — Multnomah, Washington, Clackamas (Oregon) and Clark (Washington) — as the Portland event metro, and treat everything outside it as separate edge records.**

Reasoning, with sources:
- The Oregon side of the regional government covers Portland plus surrounding cities in three counties ([Metro testimony, 2023](https://apps.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2023R1/Downloads/PublicTestimonyDocument/80184); [Metro Expo factsheet, 2024](https://www.oregonmetro.gov/sites/default/files/2024/04/23/expo-future-project-factsheet-20240423.pdf)). Those are Multnomah, Washington and Clackamas.
- Clark County must be included because the metro's largest outdoor concert room sits there: the Ridgefield amphitheater is promoted as roughly 20 minutes north of Portland ([Visit Vancouver WA](https://www.visitvancouverwa.com/listing/cascades-amphitheater/2201/)), and the Clark County Fair runs free C-TRAN shuttles ([Clark County Fair](https://www.clarkcofair.com/hours-directions)).
- Washington County is essential: Hillsboro hosts the new Hops ballpark, Hillsboro Stadium (Portland State football) and Hillsboro Ballpark (Portland Cascade softball) on one complex ([Hillsboro Stadium, WP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillsboro_Stadium); [KGW](https://kgw.com/article/sports/hillsboro-hops-announce-new-ballpark/283-61fc8cdd-ba16-4d65-be6e-e7085a91806c)).
- East Multnomah (Troutdale) is inside the core: Edgefield's lawn is described as about 15 miles east of Portland ([Travel Portland](https://www.travelportland.com/events/edgefield-concerts-on-the-lawn/)).

**Outer counties (assessed separately, not included):**
- **Columbia, Yamhill, Skamania and other outer counties:** no fixed venue at or near 5,000 surfaced in this pass. This was not an exhaustive county-by-county sweep, so treat as "none found", not "none exist". Re-check before locking scope.
- **Salem (Marion County):** the Oregon State Fair and its L.B. Day Amphitheatre concert series run at the Oregon State Fairgrounds in Salem ([Oregon Expo press release, 2026‑07‑07](https://oregonexpo.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Oregon-State-Fair-2026-Entertainment-Press-Release_FINAL.pdf)). Salem is outside Metro's three counties; recommend a **separate Salem record**, not Portland friction.
- **Eugene and distant destination amphitheaters** (e.g., the Gorge in central Washington, Bend): exclude. They share a state label but are separate trips and markets. No capacities are given here because none were verified in this pass.

### 5b. Duplicate and matching risks

1. **Theater of the Clouds = Moda Center.** Same building; listings may show "Theater of the Clouds at Moda Center" as a separate venue ([Rose Quarter](https://www.rosequarter.com/about-us/our-venues)). Map to R2 with a configuration flag.
2. **Rose Quarter shared ticketing account.** Moda Center and VMC share one Ticketmaster account manager ([Rose Quarter](https://www.rosequarter.com/about-us/our-venues)). Match on room name, not account.
3. **Old vs. new Hillsboro ballparks.** The old park was renamed Hillsboro Ballpark in March 2024, and the new park is generically "Hillsboro Hops Ballpark" ([WP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillsboro_Ballpark); [WP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillsboro_Hops_Ballpark)). Google Places' "Hillsboro Ballpark" entry (4450 NE Century Blvd) shows reviews describing the *new* park, while the same street address is used for the old ballpark and for Hillsboro Stadium in PSU's event page ([PSU](https://www.pdx.edu/events/portland-state-football-2026-home-opener)). **Proximity/address matching is ambiguous here** — match by tenant (Hops → R7; Cascade → R10; PSU football → R5).
4. **AXS mis-listing.** AXS shows "Utah Talons at Portland Cascade" under Pickles‑Walker Stadium ([AXS](https://www.axs.com/category/cities/5746545/portland-or?page=2)), but the Cascade's official home is Hillsboro Ballpark ([MLB/AUSL](https://www.mlb.com/press-release/press-release-ausl-2026-host-cities-unveiled)). Do not trust aggregator room identity.
5. **Edgefield room grouping.** Songkick lists "McMenamins Historic Edgefield Manor" with capacity 225 while hosting lawn concerts ([Songkick](https://tickets.songkick.com/venues/664-mcmenamins-historic-edgefield-manor)). Lawn shows must map to R6.
6. **Cascades Amphitheater vs. Clark County Fairgrounds** — adjacent addresses (17200 vs. 17402 NE Delfel Rd). Fair grandstand shows are not amphitheater shows.
7. **Waterfront Park (G4)** hosts Rose Festival CityFair, the Blues Festival and the Marathon start/finish. Same ground, different events — never merge by location and date window alone.
8. **IndyCar title-sponsor rename within 2026** (BITNILE.COM → OnlyBulls) — one event, two names ([Race Portland Apr](https://raceportland.com/news/latest-news/541-tickets-go-on-sale-tomorrow-for-2026-indycar-race-weekend-at-portland-international-raceway); [Race Portland Aug](https://raceportland.com/news/latest-news/550-indycar-racing-returns-to-the-pacific-northwest-this-weekend-at-portland-international-raceway)).

### 5c. Time-zone, naming and data-rights coverage findings

- **Time zone:** every venue here is America/Los_Angeles. Several feeds publish in other zones: ESPN lists a Thorns home match at 3:00 AM on May 21 ([ESPN](https://africa.espn.com/football/match/_/gameId/401853915/bay-fc-portland-thorns-fc)) and Basketball-Reference states times in Eastern ([B-Ref](https://www.basketball-reference.com/gleague/boxscores/202601270RIP.html)). Normalize before matching by date — a late-evening Pacific game can land on the next calendar date.
- **Naming:** Washington-side venues are often marketed as "Portland" (Cascades Amphitheater, "20 minutes north of Portland"), and "Vancouver" is ambiguous between Washington and British Columbia.
- **Data rights:** coordinates marked `GP` came from Google Places; Google's terms generally restrict storing that data outside Google products. Treat them as a verification aid and replace with public coordinates (venue, county GIS or Wikipedia) before storing. Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA; capacity numbers are facts but attribution is advisable.
