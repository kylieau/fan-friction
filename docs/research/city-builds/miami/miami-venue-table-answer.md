# Miami–South Florida venue table — Fan/Friction city research

**Answer date:** 2026-10-12 (all "as of" dates below are this retrieval date unless a source date is given).
**Time zone for all event dates:** US Eastern (ET); official schedules quote ET kickoffs, e.g. [World Cup Miami schedule](https://theworldcupguide.com/2026-world-cup-miami-guide/).
**Scope of this file:** public sources only; no app code, no formula constants, no personal ratings, no cost commitments. Proposals are labelled **PROPOSAL**; everything else is a sourced finding or explicitly marked *unknown / not verified*.

**Label key.** *Official* = venue, team, league, university, government or organizer page/press release. *Reported* = news, Wikipedia, ticketing or trade listing. *Estimated* = a source's own estimate or expectation. Wikipedia is used as *reported* where official pages could not be fetched; treat those numbers as needing a re-check.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edges

### Findings

- The official metro (MSA) is the three-county Miami–Fort Lauderdale–West Palm Beach area: Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/regions/southeast/news-release/2023/areaemployment_miami_20231101.htm)).
- BLS also splits that MSA into three metropolitan divisions it describes as separately identifiable employment centers, one per county; Palm Beach is its own division ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/regions/southeast/news-release/2023/areaemployment_miami_20231101.htm)).
- Division populations: Miami-Dade 2,802,029; Broward 2,013,317; Palm Beach 1,575,726 ([Florida statistical areas, reported](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_statistical_areas)).
- Every major-league home venue verified in this pass (Dolphins, Heat, Marlins, Panthers, Inter Miami) is in Miami-Dade or Broward (see Section 3).
- Palm Beach's verified 5,000+ rooms are a different event mix: a college stadium (FAU), a Live Nation amphitheater, and two MLB spring-training parks used by out-of-market clubs plus the Marlins ([Palm Beaches spring training](https://www.thepalmbeaches.com/spring-training)).
- Venue municipality is not the market label: Hard Rock Stadium, the "Miami" World Cup venue, is in Miami Gardens, about 15 miles north of the City of Miami ([Miami New Times](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/news/fifa-world-cup-2026-in-miami-what-to-know-40553973/)).

### PROPOSAL — scope

1. **Core city scope = Miami-Dade + Broward.** This matches where every verified pro home venue sits and keeps Palm Beach, which BLS treats as a separate employment center, out of the core.
2. **Palm Beach = separate "edge" tier (Section 2, Table E), not auto-included.** If one Palm Beach venue is promoted later, FAU's stadium in Boca Raton is the strongest candidate (college football, bowl game, southern end of the county); West Palm Beach and Jupiter venues are better as a separate edge.
3. Do not add Orlando/Tampa or other Florida destinations because of the state label. Nothing in this research supports them as part of the Miami event catchment.

### Rooms around the 1,000 / 5,000 thresholds that need care

- **A10 FPL Solar Amphitheater:** sources range 1,646 → 8,500. Only the city/CVB 8,500 full-venue figure puts it above 5,000, and much of that is standing lawn ([Vendry](https://www.thevendry.co/venue/224087/fpl-solar-amphitheater-at-bayfront-park-miami-fl/space/146276)). Hold for manual decision.
- **A15 Ocean Bank Convocation Center:** listed at exactly 5,000 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean_Bank_Convocation_Center)) while 2024–25 crowds were 560–1,535 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_FIU_Panthers_men%27s_basketball_team)). Capacity, not attendance, governs the label — but confirm the official figure.
- **A16 Grandstand at Hard Rock Stadium:** exactly 5,000, mostly GA ([Miami Open](https://www.miamiopen.com/faq-items/how-many-seats-does-the-grandstand-court-have/)).
- **A17 James L. Knight Center:** 4,500–5,000 conflict; also scheduled for replacement.
- **A07 Kaseya Waterfront Theater** (3,000–5,800) and **A05 Amerant theatre setup** (3,000) and **A12 Hard Rock Live theatre mode** (2,500): sub-5,000 *setups* inside 5,000+ rooms. Do not create separate rooms; the event's actual setup matters, and capacity must not be inferred from the larger room.
- **A13 Beyond Bancard Field** (7,000) and **A11 Watsco** (7,972) are clearly above 5,000 by capacity even though observed crowds are often lower.
- **Sub-5,000 pre-list rooms:** A18–A21 (2,200–3,230). Amaturo Theater (584–590) is below the ~1,000 pre-list line ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broward_Center_for_the_Performing_Arts)).
