**As of:** Monday, October 12, 2026 · **Local time zone:** America/Chicago ([cfbtrack venue record](https://www.cfbtrack.com/venues/3493-caesars-superdome)) · **Scope:** public sources only

**Labels used:** **OFF** = official (venue/team/league/government/organizer page); **REP** = reported (news, third-party guide, encyclopedia); **EST** = estimate by a named body; **UNK** = not found. "Capacity" never means attendance; attendance appears only in its own column or section.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

**Recommendation (proposal, not a confirmed fact):** keep the working core as **Orleans Parish + Jefferson Parish (east bank and West Bank)**. Treat **St. Tammany** as a separate, optional ring and treat **Hammond/Tangipahoa and Baton Rouge** as out of scope.

Reasoning, with sources:

- The federal metro definition changed in 2023: OMB removed St. Tammany from the New Orleans–Metairie MSA and created a separate Slidell–Mandeville–Covington MSA ([CSA summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans%E2%80%93Metairie%E2%80%93Slidell_combined_statistical_area); [Data Center analysis](https://www.datacenterresearch.org/reports_analysis/on-st-tammanys-split-from-the-new-orleans-metro/); [BLS parish mapping](https://www.bls.gov/OES/current/la_counties.htm)). The remaining MSA is seven parishes ([Data Center](https://www.datacenterresearch.org/reports_analysis/on-st-tammanys-split-from-the-new-orleans-metro/)).
- Every 5,000+ fixed setup found in this pass sits in Orleans or Jefferson (Table A). Jefferson adds the Shrine on Airline (Metairie), Pontchartrain Center (Kenner), Alario Center (Westwego) and TPC Louisiana / NOLA Motorsports Park (Avondale).
- St. Tammany's two largest venues are the Castine Center (events "for up to 2,500 people", [Northshore CVB](https://www.louisiananorthshore.com/meetings/facilities/)) and the Northshore Harbor Center (up to 2,000 theater style, [Convention South](https://conventionsouth.com/?p=71536)). Neither reaches 5,000, so excluding St. Tammany does not lose any friction-eligible room. It could be added later as "listed/muted" rooms only.
- Hammond (Southeastern Louisiana University) has two 5,000+ rooms — Strawberry Stadium at 7,408 seats ([Lion's Roar, citing lionsports.net](https://lionsroarnews.com/31494/opinion-why-slu-football-needs-more-fan-support-for-next-season/opinion/)) and the University Center at about 7,500 ([Stadium Journey](https://www.stadiumjourney.com/stadiums/university-center-s601)) — but Tangipahoa is its own Hammond MSA ([NORPC table note](https://www.norpc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RPCRaceEthnicityACS5Yr2019thru2023_Percent.pdf)). Sharing a state or a "Northshore" label is not a reason to include them.

### Duplicate and matching risks

- **Superdome complex:** Superdome, Champions Square and Smoothie King Center share grounds and parking. Saints pregame parties at Champions Square, parking listings and suite/club packages can appear as separate "events" on ticketing platforms.
- **Fair Grounds:** Jazz Fest occupies the racetrack grounds; do not also log grandstand or "Fair Grounds Race Course" listings for the same days.
- **City Park:** Tad Gormley, Pan American Stadium and Bayou Oaks are distinct; proximity matching will mis-assign events.
- **Morial Convention Center:** New Orleans Theater (fixed) vs exhibit halls (temporary) vs Essence day program.
- **Renamed school:** UNO → LSU New Orleans (Jul 1 2026) may cause team-name mismatches in feeds.
- **Parking-only listings:** NOLA Motorsports Park parking for the Zurich Classic.
- **Two-event days:** Bayou Classic Battle of the Bands (Friday) and game (Saturday) are separate ticketed Superdome events.

### Inaccessible or unfetched sources

- The Caesars Superdome home page could not be fetched directly in this session (tool restriction, not a site block); its official PDF press kit was read instead. No current post-renovation fact sheet was located.
- Official team schedule pages (Saints, Pelicans, Tulane, LSUNO) were not fetched; schedule coverage above relies on league releases and third-party listings.
- No ticketing API or feed was queried, so every "feed coverage" cell is unverified.
