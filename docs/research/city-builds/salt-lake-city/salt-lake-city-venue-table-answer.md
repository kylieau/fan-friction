**Answer date:** Monday, October 12, 2026 · **Local time zone:** America/Denver (Mountain; [cfbtrack venue record](https://www.cfbtrack.com/venues/587-rice-eccles-stadium)) · **Scope of this pass:** public web sources only; no ticketing-API or feed access was used.

**How to read this file**

- *Official* = venue, team, university, league or government source. *Reported* = news outlet or encyclopedia. *Estimated/predicted* = third-party forecast (e.g. PredictHQ) or an explicitly approximate figure. Every number is linked to the page that supports it.
- Capacity is never attendance. Where a crowd figure is published it is shown separately and labelled.
- "Feed coverage" means verified coverage in a ticketing/listing feed with exact room identity. **No feed match was verified in this pass**, so every feed cell reads *unknown* unless stated otherwise. Official schedule pages that were seen are listed.
- Coordinates: only one venue had coordinates in a fetched source (Rice-Eccles). All others are marked *geocode needed* with the sourced address, rather than supplying unsourced numbers.
- **Confirmed** = has happened. **Scheduled** = officially announced with a date. **Proposal** = plan, bid or study with no committed date.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### Recommendation

**Core scope: Salt Lake County.** Every 5,000+ fixed room found in this pass that has a current major tenant sits inside Salt Lake County (Salt Lake City, Sandy, West Valley City, South Jordan, Herriman, Taylorsville). This matches the federal metro definition: the Salt Lake City MSA's boundaries [include Salt Lake County and Tooele County](https://edcutah.org/recent-news/research-minute-updates-to-utahs-core-based-statistical-areas) under the 2023 delineation.

**Include as a flagged edge: Tooele County (one venue).** Tooele County is inside the same MSA, so West Desert (the former Utah Motorsports Campus) qualifies on metro definition. It is a motorsport venue with irregular spectator events, so treat it as *in scope but event-by-event*.

**Do not merge — keep as separate edge markets:**

| Area | Federal grouping | Why separate | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Davis County (Farmington, Layton) | Part of the [Ogden MSA](https://edcutah.org/recent-news/research-minute-updates-to-utahs-core-based-statistical-areas) with Weber and Morgan counties | Different MSA; no 5,000+ fixed room verified in this pass | Exclude unless a specific event shows Salt Lake catchment |
| Ogden (Weber State) | Ogden MSA | Separate MSA, own college tenant, own concert series (Ogden Twilight, [noted alongside SLC Twilight](https://www.ksl.com:443/article/51477966/heres-who-will-perform-at-salt-lake-citys-2026-twilight-concert-series)) | Separate edge list (Section 3) |
| Provo/Orem (BYU, UVU) | [Provo–Orem–Lehi MSA](https://edcutah.org/recent-news/research-minute-updates-to-utahs-core-based-statistical-areas) | Separate MSA; BYU has its own 62,000-seat stadium | Separate edge list; but see the BYU-at-Delta-Center matching note |
| Park City (Summit County) | Not in the SLC MSA; part of the wider CSA | Sundance has left (moved to Boulder from 2027) | Exclude; historical only |

**Reasoning.** The Census combined statistical area ties these MSAs together because a CSA is formed when the [employment interchange rate between adjacent areas is at least 15%](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho_statistical_areas). That is commuting evidence, not event-travel evidence. The [Salt Lake City–Provo–Orem CSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_statistical_areas) is about 2.9 million people, but the brief asks not to merge the Wasatch Front without event-travel evidence, and this pass found none that would justify pulling Ogden or Provo venues into the Salt Lake friction pool. Two small pieces of cross-market evidence exist and are logged below: LOVB Salt Lake has listed a BYU (Provo) venue among its venues, and BYU basketball has played at the Delta Center. Neither is enough to move a boundary.

### 6e. Data-rights and coverage notes

- All times are Mountain Time (America/Denver), with daylight saving.
- Several key crowd figures (State Fair, parade, FanX) come from commercial forecasters or pre-event expectations, not organizers' counts. These are flagged and should not be stored as attendance.
- Church-run venues (Conference Center, Tabernacle) use free ticketing outside mainstream platforms; feed coverage is likely absent and would need manual entry.
