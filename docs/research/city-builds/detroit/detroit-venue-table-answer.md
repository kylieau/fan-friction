## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### Recommendation (proposal — not a change to the existing city model)

| Tier | Area | Basis | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core** | Six-county Detroit–Warren–Dearborn MSA: Wayne, Oakland, Macomb, Livingston, St. Clair, Lapeer | OMB definition as summarized on [Metro Detroit (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro_Detroit); 2023 OMB delineation table on [Michigan statistical areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_statistical_areas) | Pre-list all 1,000+ rooms; 5,000+ setups feed friction |
| **Edge: Ann Arbor** | Washtenaw County (Ann Arbor MSA), inside the Detroit–Warren–Ann Arbor CSA but outside the Detroit MSA | Same OMB sources; Ann Arbor is described as roughly 43 road miles from downtown Detroit and a separately developed university city ([Metro Detroit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro_Detroit)) | Keep as a separately labeled scope. Its three 5,000+ rooms (Michigan Stadium, Crisler Center, Yost) draw a statewide/regional crowd, so they belong in the catchment, but tagging them "Ann Arbor edge" avoids implying they are downtown Detroit rooms |
| **Edge: Windsor (cross-border)** | Windsor, Ontario | Directly across the Detroit River; a third crossing, the Gordie Howe International Bridge, opened to traffic July 27, 2026 ([WDBA](https://gordiehoweinternationalbridge.com/celebrating-the-gordie-howe-international-bridge-july-24-2026/)) | Keep as a separately labeled scope. Border inspection, CAD pricing and Canadian ticket storefronts make it a different access/coverage case even though it is physically closer than Ann Arbor |
| **Excluded unless requested** | Distant Michigan destinations sharing only a state label (e.g., Michigan International Speedway, East Lansing) | Outside the MSA; not part of the Detroit event footprint researched here | Not assessed this pass ❓ |

**Why not use the CSA wholesale?** The nine-county CSA also pulls in Genesee (Flint), Monroe and Lenawee ([Metro Detroit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro_Detroit)); no 5,000+ room in those counties surfaced in this pass, and adding them would mostly add distance without adding friction-feeding rooms. Re-check if the model needs Flint.

### Rooms around the 1,000 / 5,000 thresholds that need care

- **Fox Theatre (A15):** 5,048 fixed seats crosses 5,000; the 5,174 figure depends on removable pit seats ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Theatre_(Detroit))). Treat the fixed figure as the base and the pit as an alternate setup; do not merge.
- **Colosseum at Caesars Windsor (A16):** sources say "up to" or "about" 5,000 with a retractable floor ([TCC](https://theatrecc.com/mies_portfolio/caesars-windsor-entertainment-centre/)). Classification depends on whether exactly 5,000 counts as "5,000+"; flat-floor GA setups should not be read as seated capacity.
- **Masonic Temple (A17):** 4,404 theatre vs 8,266 building ([Crain's](https://prod.crainsdetroit.com/node/25946)) — the building figure appears to combine rooms; keep the theatre as the room. Conflict preserved.
- **WFCU Centre (A13):** above 5,000 for hockey and end-stage concerts, but its hybrid (4,500) and theatre (3,000) setups fall below ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WFCU_Centre)). Classification depends on configuration.
- **Amphitheatres (A5, A10, A11):** totals include lawn GA. Pavilion-only seating at Meadow Brook (2,819) and Freedom Hill (4,200) is below 5,000; lawn is standing/blanket space, not fixed seats.
- **Wayne State Fieldhouse (A18):** ~3,000 — pre-list, muted, no friction label.
- **Ford Field (A2):** 65,000 is the football/overall figure; concert and basketball configurations are unpublished on the official page, so the 2027 Final Four should not be assigned 65,000 without a configuration source.
