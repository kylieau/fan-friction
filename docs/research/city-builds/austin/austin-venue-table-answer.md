# Austin venue and crowd-event research — Fan/Friction city model

**Answer dated:** Sunday, October 11, 2026 (US Central Time, CDT). All statuses are "as of" this date unless a row says otherwise.
**Scope:** Austin–Round Rock–San Marcos working area, with Killeen/Temple, College Station and San Antonio assessed separately.
**Method:** Public web sources only. Each number links to the page it came from. Labels: **official** = venue/team/university/government/organizer page; **reported** = news or reference site; **third-party** = ticketing, travel or aggregator site (lowest confidence). "Unknown" means I did not find a public figure, not that none exists.

Important limits of this pass, stated up front:

- **Coordinates:** I only report decimal coordinates where a source published them. Most rows show an official street address instead and are marked "geocode from address." I did not invent coordinates.
- **Feed coverage:** I verified ticketing listings only where I actually saw a venue-specific page. Everything else is "unknown."
- **High-school stadiums:** Several ISD stadiums in Travis/Williamson/Hays counties likely exceed 5,000 seats. I did not enumerate them; this is the largest known gap (see section 5).

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### Recommendation

Use the **Austin–Round Rock–San Marcos MSA** as the boundary, which means adding **Bastrop and Caldwell counties** to the Travis/Williamson/Hays working area. The metro is defined under that name and had a [2020 census population of 2,352,426](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Austin); the same source lists Bastrop and Lockhart (Caldwell County) among its cities. *Verify the five-county list against the current OMB delineation before locking it; I did not fetch the OMB bulletin.*

Reasoning for the two additions:

- **Bastrop County** sits on COTA's lodging and traffic shed. The county tourism office markets itself as the overnight base for both the [2026 F1 weekend](https://explorebastropcounty.com/calendar-event/formula-1-united-states-grand-prix-2026/) and the [2026 MotoGP weekend](https://explorebastropcounty.com/calendar-event/motogp-red-bull-grand-prix-of-the-americas-2026/). That is direct catchment evidence for the metro's largest event site. No qualifying fixed venue was found in Bastrop County itself.
- **Caldwell County** is included for metro consistency only; no qualifying venue was found there.

### Separate markets — do not merge

| Area | Recommendation | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| San Antonio | **Keep separate.** Treat Spurs games played in Austin as Austin events at Moody Center, not as a reason to merge. | The Spurs' Austin games are a deliberate secondary-market play: the team runs [an Austin office and wants to be "Austin's professional basketball team"](https://www.insidehook.com/culture-texas/san-antonio-spurs-austin/amp). Their home building remains [Frost Bank Center](https://www.tpr.org/news/2026-08-13/nba-western-conference-champion-spurs-release-2026-2027-schedule). |
| Killeen/Temple (Bell County) | **Keep separate.** | H-E-B Center in Cedar Park markets directly to [Temple and Killeen audiences](https://www.hebcenter.com/arena-info/about-the-arena/sporting-events-temple), which shows audience flow *into* Austin's northern suburbs. That supports counting Bell County fans at Austin venues, not adding Bell County venues to Austin. |
| College Station | **Keep separate.** | No audience/travel evidence found linking Texas A&M venues to the Austin event market. |

### Uncertain edge venues

| Venue | Issue | Treatment |
|---|---|---|

| New Braunfels / Comal County | Commonly grouped with San Antonio, not Austin. Not researched. | Exclude by default. |

## 3. Threshold rooms that need careful treatment

- **Moody Amphitheater (5,000):** Exactly at the friction line and mostly lawn. Classifying it as 5,000+ or under depends on whether the model's threshold is "≥5,000" or ">5,000" — an existing-model rule, not something to tune here.
- **Palmer Events Center:** Theatre setup of 5,172 is a third-party figure for a flat exhibit floor; standing figures for each hall are lower. Recommend listing it but leaving it out of friction unless a specific event publishes a crowd above 5,000.
- **Bold Stadium (~5,000):** At the line and possibly inactive.
- **Moody Center:** Its basketball setup (~10,000–10,763) and concert setup (15,000–16,223) are different rooms in effect. Never use the concert figure for a UT game.
- **Dell Diamond:** 11,631 includes berm/lawn; fixed seats are 8,722. Both are above 5,000, so the classification is safe; the number used for any per-seat logic is not.
- **Luedecke Arena:** 6,400 vs 8,400 does not change classification (both 5,000+), but it changes any capacity-ratio math.
- **ACL Live:** 2,750 concerts vs 800 TV tapings.
- **Near 1,000:** McCombs Field (1,250), Parmer Field (1,100), and three Texas State facilities listed at 1,000.

### Duplicate and matching risks

- **COTA:** The F1 weekend total, daily sessions, support races, concerts in the amphitheater, hospitality suites and parking all share one crowd. Log one event record for the weekend; the amphitheater gets its own events only on non-race days.
- **UT campus cluster:** DKR, Moody Center and Myers Stadium sit side by side ([KXAN](https://www.kxan.com/sports/moody-center-to-provide-different-experience-for-basketball-fans/amp/)). Platforms may group them as "UT Austin." Match by exact room.
- **H-E-B Center:** Listed on both Ticketmaster and AXS. The Ticketmaster page is labeled "All Events and Add-Ons," and AXS lists parking as separate events. Filter parking and add-on listings out.
- **Stubb's:** Waller Creek Amphitheater (~2,200) and the indoor stage (~300) are separate rooms under one brand. A [fan guide](https://ava.ead.domalberto.edu.br/stubbs-waller-creek-amphitheatre-austin) warns buyers to check which stage a listing is for.
- **Palmer Events Center:** Halls 1 and 2 can be booked separately or together.
- **SXSW and Rodeo Austin:** The umbrella totals include many room-level events. Keep them as separate records.
- **Spurs in Austin:** Match to Moody Center, not to Frost Bank Center.
- **Proximity alone is ambiguous** at COTA (amphitheater vs. grounds vs. Bold Stadium) and on UT campus.
