# Kansas City venue table — research answer

**Answer date:** Monday, October 12, 2026 (all "as of" dates below are publication dates of the linked sources; "now" means October 12, 2026).
**Market:** Kansas City, Missouri–Kansas (KCMO + KCK and suburbs). Lawrence, KS and Columbia, MO are reported separately in Appendix A and are **not** recommended for this city record.
**Time zone:** America/Chicago, Central Time ([cfbtrack venue record](https://www.cfbtrack.com/venues/3486-children-s-mercy-park)).
**Method:** public web sources only; venue, team, league, government and organizer pages preferred; local press (KCTV, KSHB, KCUR, FOX4) used for dated changes; aggregators (Wikipedia, StadiumDB, Stadium Journey, KC Today, ticket resellers) used where no primary page was retrieved and labeled as such. Every number links to the page it came from. Where I could not retrieve a source, the cell says **unknown** rather than estimating.

**Labels used in capacity cells**
- **official** = venue/team/operator/government page
- **reported** = press or reference aggregator citing the venue
- **estimated** = crowd estimate, not a count
- **conflict** = sources disagree; both values kept

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### Recommendation
Use a **bi-state metro boundary** covering the Missouri side (Kansas City MO, Independence, North Kansas City, Jackson/Clay/Platte counties) and the Kansas side (Wyandotte County/KCK, Bonner Springs, Johnson County incl. Olathe and Overland Park). Do **not** stop at the state line, because the 5,000+ venue base is split across it and is shifting west:

- The Kansas-side Village West cluster already holds four 5,000+ setups — Sporting Park, Kansas Speedway, Legends Field and Azura Amphitheater — all reached off I-70/I-435 ([Stadium Journey, Legends Field](https://www.stadiumjourney.com/stadiums/communityamerica-ballpark-s239); [Stadium Journey, Sporting Park](https://www.stadiumjourney.com/stadiums/childrens-mercy-park-sporting-kc); [Azura CU](https://www.azuracu.com/about/azura-amphitheater/)). Legends Field is about [11 miles west of downtown KCMO](https://www.stadiumjourney.com/stadiums/communityamerica-ballpark-s239); Sporting Park about [14 miles](https://stadiumguide.com/?p=5112).
- The Chiefs' scheduled 2031 domed stadium is near [126th Street and State Avenue in Wyandotte County](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/10/08/wyandotte-county-passes-measure-opting-out-parts-chiefs-stadium-funding/) — StadiumDB describes the move as only about [24 miles](https://stadiumdb.com/designs/usa/chiefs_stadium) east-to-west suburbs within the same metro.
- The American Royal's new campus is at [118th and State Avenue, KCK](https://ingrams.com/article/american-royal-wyandotte-campus-community-improvement-district-passes/), while its old site (Hale Arena, West Bottoms) is in KCMO — the same organizer straddles both sides during 2026–27.
- Event catchment evidence: KC2026 reported that over half of FIFA Fan Festival registrations came from within a [45-mile radius](https://fox4kc.com/sports/2026-world-cup/fans-can-expect-strong-security-presence-at-arrowhead-stadium-fan-fest/), and that [52% of attendees through June 16](https://fox4kc.com/sports/2026-world-cup/fan-fest-draws-fans-from-178-countries-as-kansas-citys-world-cup-momentum-grows/) came from Kansas and Missouri combined — the crowd base is bi-state.

I did **not** retrieve the Census Bureau's official MSA county list in this pass; if the app's boundary must match a statistical definition, fetch it before locking the polygon.

### Lawrence and Columbia — keep separate
- **Lawrence (KU):** a college town with its own venues (Allen Fieldhouse, David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium) and its own schedule feeds. KU did play its 2024 home football season at Arrowhead and Children's Mercy Park while the Booth was rebuilt ([KCTV](https://www.kctv5.com/2024/07/11/ku-announces-new-attendance-capacity-following-renovations-allen-fieldhouse)), so record those games under the KC venues, but keep Lawrence itself as a separate market. See Appendix A.
- **Columbia (Mizzou):** separate market, much farther away; Mizzou playing in KC is occasional and should be logged by event. See Appendix A.

### 5b. Duplicate / matching risks
- **Truman Sports Complex:** Arrowhead and Kauffman share lots and an entrance road; parking passes and suite products are commonly listed as separate "events" on ticket platforms — filter out parking/suite listings so one game isn't logged two or three times.
- **Arrowhead aliases:** "GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium" (2021–Aug 2026), "Arrowhead Stadium" (now), and "Kansas City Stadium" (FIFA, June–July 2026) are **one room** ([AP via NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/chiefs-drop-geha-field-return-arrowhead-stadium)).
- **Sporting Park / Children's Mercy Park:** one room; a further rename is expected ([KSHB](https://www.kshb.com/sports/sporting-kc/childrens-mercy-wont-renew-stadium-naming-rights-partnership-with-sporting-kc)).
- **Village West cluster (KCK):** Sporting Park, Legends Field, Kansas Speedway (with Hollywood Casino) are close together — proximity matching is ambiguous; match on exact room name.
- **126th & State Ave:** Azura's VIP lot, the Renaissance Festival entrance area and the future Chiefs stadium site all reference this intersection ([Azura CU](https://www.azuracu.com/about/azura-amphitheater/); [KCTV](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/02/06/wyandotte-county-approves-star-bonds-chiefs-stadium/)).
- **Municipal Auditorium complex (301 W 13th St):** Arena, Music Hall, Little Theatre and Exhibition Hall share an address with the Convention Center ([KC Convention](https://kcconvention.com/venue/municipal-arena/)) — platforms may group them; the Music Hall (2,363) must never inherit the Arena's 10,721.
- **Starlight:** main stage vs Cohen Community Stage; **Kauffman Center:** Muriel Kauffman Theatre vs Helzberg Hall; **Uptown:** main room vs Encore Room; **Midland:** main theater vs private-event spaces.
- **Crown Center / Washington Square Park / Union Station / WWI Memorial:** hosts parade rallies, the 2023 Draft, the 2026 Fan Fest, Irish Fest and Boulevardia — and becomes the Royals ballpark district ([HPPR/KCUR](https://www.hppr.org/2026-04-22/kansas-city-royals-stadium-location-crown-center)). Keep each as a dated event, not one "venue."
- **West Bottoms:** Hy-Vee Arena (ex-Kemper) and Hale Arena are adjacent but separate rooms; American Royal now also uses its KCK campus.
- **T-Mobile Center vs KC Live!:** across the street from each other; watch parties at KC Live! are not arena events.
- **Mavericks playoff relocation:** games moved to Independence Community Ice with no fans ([KCTV](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/05/01/mavericks-say-conflicts-arena-could-cause-playoff-games-be-played-without-fans/)) — feeds may still show Cable Dahmer Arena.
