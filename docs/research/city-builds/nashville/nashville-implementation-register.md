## Judgment calls

- **City type `sprawl`.** The research recommends it. The contrary case for `hub` is the unmeasured downtown walk share. Car-share estimates (Nissan 0.88, GEODIS 0.90, Superspeedway 0.97, and the festival estimates) stay in the research.
- **Display name** is Nashville. Short code is `BNA`, the airport code, same pattern as Tampa's `TPA`.
- **Map center** `[-86.78, 36.16]`, zoom 10.6, so downtown, Vanderbilt, and the fairgrounds share the first frame. The Opry, Murfreesboro, Lebanon, and Thompson's Station sit off that frame.
- **Zone names** are set on the anchor rooms: Downtown (Nissan), Fairgrounds (GEODIS), Vanderbilt, MTSU, Lebanon, Thompson's Station, Music Valley, Tennessee State, Lipscomb. A room within 1.25 miles of the anchor joins that zone. Curb Event Center may fall in the Vanderbilt zone on that rule.
- **GEODIS roof** is stored as open. The research describes an open bowl under a canopy.
- **Olympic alias** "Nashville Stadium" for 2028 was not added. A name with no end date would keep showing after the Games.
- **LP Field** (the name before 2016) was not added. The research alias window starts in 2016.
- **Coordinates.** Most rooms are OpenStreetMap points. FirstBank Amphitheater and Curb Event Center are the Wikipedia points from the research. The Truth is the street address. GEODIS is the OSM stadium point (501 Benton Ave), not the research street address (625 Smith Ave). The Superspeedway OSM point is tagged Rutherford County; the research places the track in Lebanon, Wilson County. Polk Theater and Andrew Jackson Hall share one building point.
