# Nashville venue and crowd-event research — Fan/Friction city model

**As of:** Monday, October 12, 2026. **Time zone for all dates/times:** US Central (America/Chicago).
**Scope of this answer:** public data only; no app code, flags, constants, ratings or cost commitments. Proposals are marked separately from confirmed or scheduled changes.

**Labels used throughout**

- **Official** = venue, team, league, university, organizer or government page.
- **Reported** = media, architect/engineer, encyclopedia or third-party guide.
- **Estimated** = organizer or agency crowd estimate (not a ticket count).
- **Unknown** = not found or not verifiable in this pass. Nothing has been converted from capacity to attendance, or from multi-day totals to daily/peak counts.
- **Threshold flag:** `≥5k` = friction-eligible setup exists; `1k–5k` = list and mute, no friction label; `straddles` = treatment depends on configuration.

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### Recommendation

**Core scope: the Nashville-Davidson–Murfreesboro–Franklin MSA.** The official metro is [Davidson plus 13 other counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville_metropolitan_area), with Murfreesboro and Franklin as named principal cities, so MTSU (Murfreesboro) and FirstBank Amphitheater (Franklin/Thompson's Station) belong in core scope rather than as edge cases. The MSA had an estimated [2,197,416 residents in 2025, inside a 2,452,592-person CSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_statistical_areas). Event catchment supports this: Nashville SC runs [fan buses from Antioch and Murfreesboro](https://stadiumjourney.com/stadiums/geodis-park-nashville-sc), and Nashville Superspeedway in Lebanon sits [about 30 miles southeast of Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Cracker_Barrel_400).

**Clarksville: include as a separately tagged edge scope, not core.** Montgomery County is the core of the [Clarksville, TN-KY MSA, which is listed with no combined statistical area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_statistical_areas), i.e. statistically separate from Nashville. It is still worth listing because the Nashville-branded Kats moved there ([home games at F&M Bank Arena from 2026](https://www.nashvillepost.com/sports/football/nashville-kats-moving-to-clarksville-beginning-in-2026/article_55b54ef2-5c21-41c8-9546-bf6f3a78772f.html)) and the arena is [operated by the Nashville Predators' venue company](https://letsgopeay.com/facilities/?id=1025). Tag rows `Clarksville (separate MSA)` so city-level aggregation can include or exclude them deliberately.

**Manchester: event record only (Table B), not a fixed-venue market.** Coffee County forms the [Tullahoma–Manchester micropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_statistical_areas), not the Nashville MSA. Its only threshold-relevant asset is Great Stage Park (Bonnaroo), an open farm site without a meaningful fixed-room capacity. Whether that micropolitan area sits inside the Nashville CSA could not be confirmed from the captured table excerpt; re-check against the current OMB delineation before relying on CSA membership.

**Do not auto-include** other Tennessee destinations because they share a state or "Middle Tennessee" label.

### Duplicate and matching risks

- **Two "Nissan Stadium" buildings** with the same official name across the Jan 3, 2027 → 2027 transition. Use date ranges or distinct venue IDs; never merge.
- **The Fairgrounds Nashville** is a parent grounds containing GEODIS Park, the Fairgrounds Speedway and expo buildings. A "Fairgrounds Nashville" listing could be any of them.
- **"Tennessee State Fairgrounds" lead is ambiguous:** the state fair now runs at Lebanon's [James E. Ward Agricultural Center](https://www.wsmv.com/2026/02/13/wilson-county-releases-dates-theme-2026-tennessee-state-fair/), not the Nashville fairgrounds it used [until 2019](https://stadiumdb.com/constructions/usa/geodis_park).
- **Olympic alias:** GEODIS Park will appear as "Nashville Stadium" in LA28 feeds.
- **Team name vs venue city:** Nashville Kats play in Clarksville.
- **Temporary home:** TSU's Aug 29, 2026 home game was at FirstBank Stadium; some feeds still showed Nissan.
- **Brand vs room:** Opry shows at the Ryman; CMA Fest spans Nissan, Ascend, Fan Fair X and street stages; TPAC groups four rooms; Cannery Hall groups three.
- **Sub-rooms inside large venues:** Vinyl Room at The Truth; Robert's and Bluebird East inside the new stadium; Ford Ice Center inside the F&M Bank Arena complex.
- **Non-event listings:** PSLs, suites, parking, camping, payment plans, four-night passes and lounge upgrades (Nissan, Superspeedway, Ryman) can look like events.
- **Measure confusion:** hotel rooms sold, tickets sold, organizer crowd estimates and 10-day fair totals are all distinct from peak attendance.

### Coverage notes

- **Time zone:** all venues are in US Central time.

- **Event calendars:** most dates come from organizer, team or university pages; no single public feed was verified to cover every room.
- **Data rights:** figures are from public pages; attendance for most rooms is unpublished, and crowd estimates for open-site events are organizer estimates, not counts.
