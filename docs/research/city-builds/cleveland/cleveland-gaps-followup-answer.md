## Build rules (notes for whoever turns this into app data)

- **Unique IDs across all cities.** Every venue ID and team ID must be unique across all cities. `giants`, `jets`, `rangers`, `kings` and `cardinals` are already taken, so add a city prefix. For Cleveland, prefix every team and venue ID, for example `cle-browns` or `cle-c01`. A prefix is needed here because feeds reuse short codes: ESPN gives the abbreviation `CLE` to the Browns, Guardians, Cavaliers and Cleveland State, and the `5` ID to three different teams (see §3).
- **No per-city opponents file.** Don't make a separate opponents file for Cleveland. Opponent draws go into `expectedDrawIndex`, as in every other city.
- **Never guess a capacity.** A missing or range-only figure stays blank and goes on the city gaps list. That covers MGM Northfield Center Stage and the current official House of Blues Music Hall figure (§6).
- **Car shares.** Wire measured ones. Wire sourced estimates marked as estimated. Leave the rest on the city default. Car shares live in the city-type answer, which was not part of this pass.
