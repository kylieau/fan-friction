**As of:** Sunday, 11 October 2026 (America/New_York, Eastern Time)

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### What the official metro is

The federal Boston–Cambridge–Newton, MA-NH Metropolitan Statistical Area is made up of [Essex, Middlesex, Norfolk, Plymouth and Suffolk counties in Massachusetts plus Rockingham and Strafford counties in New Hampshire](https://www.bls.gov/regions/northeast/news-release/areaemployment_boston.htm). It had an estimated [5,034,221 residents in 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_statistical_areas), inside a wider Boston–Worcester–Providence combined area of [8,521,063](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_statistical_areas). Bristol County, Massachusetts (which contains Mansfield) is grouped with [Providence–Warwick, RI-MA](https://www.santanderbank.com/footprint-by-county), and Worcester County is its [own MSA](https://www.santanderbank.com/footprint-by-county). Mansfield was listed inside the old [Boston PMSA in the 1990s OMB definitions](https://clintonwhitehouse4.archives.gov/OMB/bulletins/95-04att-2.html), so its regrouping is a statistical change, not a change in where its crowds come from.

### Recommended final scope (fits the existing city model)

| Zone | Municipalities / sites | Treatment | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core | Boston (all neighborhoods incl. Allston, Brighton, Fenway, Seaport, Jamaica Plain/Franklin Park), Cambridge, Chestnut Hill (Newton/Boston border) | In scope | Suffolk and Middlesex counties, inside the MSA. |
| Corridor | Foxborough (Gillette Stadium) | In scope | Norfolk County, inside the MSA; about [22 miles from Boston](https://www.thestadiumbusiness.com/2025/05/22/boston-legacy-to-spend-first-season-at-gillette-stadium/); home of every Boston-branded outdoor pro tenant that needs a large stadium. |
| Corridor exception | Mansfield (Xfinity Center) | In scope, flagged | Statistically Providence MSA today, but sits next to Foxborough, about [30 miles from Boston](https://thrillzing.com/music-venues/xfinity-center-mansfield/), and was in the Boston PMSA under earlier definitions. Keep it so the Foxborough/Mansfield corridor stays whole. |
| In-MSA edge | Lowell (Tsongas Center), Topsfield (fairgrounds) | In scope, flagged as edge | Middlesex and Essex counties are in the MSA. Tsongas was the primary home of a Boston pro team (Fleet) through spring 2026. |
| Out-of-metro home venue | Pawtucket, RI (Centreville Bank Stadium) | Separate record, not Boston friction | Temporary home of Boston Legacy FC in 2026–2027 and a 2026 Boston Cannons home weekend. Log the team's events, but tag the venue as outside the Boston boundary. |
| Excluded edge | Providence (Amica Mutual Pavilion), Worcester (DCU Center, Polar Park), Manchester and Loudon, NH (incl. New Hampshire Motor Speedway) | Excluded | Separate MSAs or non-MSA NH counties; distinct teams and catchments. NH Motor Speedway is in Loudon (Merrimack County), not Rockingham/Strafford. |

**Uncertain edge venues needing a decision by the app owner:** Xfinity Center (statistically outside, practically inside); Tsongas Center (inside the MSA but about 25+ miles out, losing its pro tenant); Centreville Bank Stadium (outside, but a Boston team's actual home through 2027).

### Duplicate and matching risks

The Alumni Stadium–Conte Forum pair shares suites and concourse, so suite or parking listings for one can surface under the other. Harvard Athletic Complex listings (Songkick lists it as one "venue") can stand for Harvard Stadium, Jordan Field or festival grounds. Lansdowne Street has three different rooms (Fenway Park, MGM Music Hall, House of Blues) within one block. Boston Landing has two rooms in one building. Gillette appeared as "Boston Stadium" in FIFA-sourced feeds from June 13 to July 9, 2026. Boston Legacy FC and Boston Cannons "home" games in Pawtucket will carry Boston team names but are outside the Boston boundary. The Matthews Arena site will reappear as a new venue with a new name in 2028.
