# Philadelphia — Fan/Friction venue and crowd-event research

**Answer date:** Sunday, October 11, 2026 (all "as of" dates below are the source's publication/update date; re-check dates are in Section 5).
**Time zone for all events:** US Eastern (ET, observes daylight saving).
**Method note:** Figures come from public search-result extracts of the linked pages. No page was full-fetched in this pass, and some Inquirer/PBJ pages may be paywalled. Where a number is quoted from a secondary or aggregator page, it is labelled *reported*; venue/team/university/government/organizer pages are labelled *official*. Capacity is never used as attendance.

Label key: **O** = official (owner/operator/team/university/government/organizer) · **R** = reported (press, encyclopedic, ticketing blog) · **E** = estimate/expectation (pre-event projection, not a count).

## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### Evidence for the metro definition
- The federal MSA is Philadelphia–Camden–Wilmington, PA-NJ-DE-MD: Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Montgomery and Philadelphia (PA); Burlington, Camden, Gloucester and Salem (NJ); New Castle (DE); Cecil (MD). It has four metropolitan divisions; Camden division = Burlington, Camden, Gloucester; Philadelphia division = Delaware + Philadelphia counties ([BLS, Aug 2024](https://www.bls.gov/regions/mid-atlantic/news-release/2024/areaemployment_philadelphia_20240828.htm)).
- New Castle, Salem and Cecil are coded to the Wilmington division in a Maryland planning list ([MD Planning, 2010-era list](https://mdp.state.md.us/MSDC/Documents/Census/Cen2010/metroareamap/List1.pdf)). *Inference (not stated in the BLS extract):* the fourth division is Bucks–Chester–Montgomery.
- MSA 2025 estimate 6,329,118; the wider CSA ("Philadelphia-Reading-Camden") is 7,493,171 ([NJ statistical areas, citing OMB July 2023 delineations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey_statistical_areas)). Reading/Berks is CSA-only, not MSA.

### Recommendation (proposal)
| Tier | Area | Treatment | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core | Philadelphia, Delaware, Montgomery, Bucks, Chester (PA); Camden, Burlington, Gloucester (NJ) | Include | Philadelphia + Camden divisions + PA suburban division; contains every major-team home and the Chester (Subaru Park), Camden waterfront, Villanova, Bensalem (Parx) and Newtown Square (Aronimink) leads. |
| Edge (flagged) | New Castle County, DE (Wilmington, Newark) | Include as a separate, flagged tier | Inside the MSA but its own metro division with its own teams (Blue Hens, Blue Rocks). Keep visible but tag so it can be muted without redesign. |
| In MSA, no qualifying venue found | Salem (NJ), Cecil (MD) | Include in boundary; nothing to list | No 1,000+ fixed venue surfaced in this pass. Re-check before finalising. |
| Exclude | Mercer County NJ (Trenton/Princeton), Atlantic City, Berks (Reading), Lehigh Valley, Poconos, Dover/Kent DE | Exclude | Not among the MSA's counties per BLS. Shared state or "Philly region" marketing labels are not grounds for inclusion. Atlantic City stays out: no evidence found that it belongs to the Philadelphia event catchment. |

### Duplicate / matching risks
- **"Philadelphia Stadium"** names two different venues: Subaru Park in CONCACAF play and Lincoln Financial Field during the 2026 World Cup. Match on venue ID/address, never the display name.
- **Mann campus:** TD Pavilion, Skyline Stage, Downstage (300) and festival mode share one ticketing parent. Festival-day listings should map to one grounds event.
- **Freedom Mortgage Pavilion:** summer amphitheatre and winter enclosed theatre are one building, two configurations.
- **Fillmore / Foundry** and **Marian Anderson Hall / Perelman Theater:** same building, separate rooms.
- **Parking, suite, VIP and package listings** (e.g., Ticketmaster "parking" events at the Sports Complex, ballpark parking pages on ticket marketplaces) can mimic events — filter by event type.
- **Sports Complex:** Linc, Citizens Bank Park, Xfinity Mobile Arena and the planned arena are adjacent; proximity matching will mis-assign. Same issue for Parx (casino Xcite Center vs racetrack).
- **Penn Relays:** Franklin Field plus Mondschein Throws Complex — one event.
- **Broad Street Run:** run (street course) and expo (Convention Center) are separate events.
