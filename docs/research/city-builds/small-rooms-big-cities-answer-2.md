## Reading and importing the records

- **Official** means published by the venue, owner, operator, university, municipality, sponsor or primary ticketing partner. It does not guarantee a sellable count for every production. **Reported** means a secondary publication or directory. **Estimated** means a calculation or geographic approximation made in this research. An official source's own approximate quantity is labeled official and approximate.
- Labels apply to audience quantities and coordinates. Dates, street addresses, document identifiers and Ticketmaster IDs are identifiers rather than audience estimates.
- **Coordinate precision is part of the data.** Estimated street-range points, official routing points and reported building references must not be imported into a field labeled verified main entrance. No number of decimal places establishes door accuracy. An unconfirmed coordinate means evidence was insufficient, not that a room lacks a location.
- The working lower cutoff is approximately one thousand. Near-line rooms are listed separately. A sub-threshold seated layout can qualify where a documented standing or lawn configuration reaches the target range. Capacities over the upper bound are flagged as missing larger venues.
- The supplied app inventories are exclusion lists: existing rooms are not repeated. Separate rooms in the same building are retained separately; sponsor changes, spelling variants and court dedications alone do not create new physical rooms.
- Provisional paid-public-program evidence, future reopening, closure and unresolved whole-house limits are explicit qualifications. **The main tables are research records, not an unfiltered active-venue import list.**
- Tables run largest first by the preferred current published public-event configuration, or a clearly provisional inventory where no reliable maximum exists. Historical, dinner, cocktail and combined-property totals do not silently determine a room's concert capacity. For unresolved totals, order is indicative rather than a certification of a maximum.
- Unknown setups are not inferred from floor area, a fire-occupancy claim, a directory's identical seated/standing fields, or historical attendance. Unknown change dates do not mean that no change occurred. Temporary pandemic restrictions are not treated as permanent room inventories.
- Ticketmaster IDs appear only when a venue URL containing the ID was found. Blank means not verified; AXS and Live Nation identifiers are not substituted. Unless an alias is documented, no Ticketmaster name equivalence is asserted.
- Public sources only. No purchases, accounts, sign-ups or venue correspondence were used. Failed pages are listed; an indexed extract is distinguished from a fully read page.

### What could not be confirmed

**Entrance coordinates.** Street-address estimates are supplied only where a usable public Census match survived the address check. They are labeled estimated address references, not verified doors. Campus mailing addresses, multilevel shopping/entertainment complexes, resort addresses and fairground-wide gates cannot locate a particular room's main entrance. Most campus and outdoor-room door coordinates remain explicitly unconfirmed. The published AVALON location is an official routing reference, not a certified door.

The geocoder returned the wrong directional street for Alex Theatre and Pasadena Civic, and an Ocean Avenue match for the Terrace Theater's Ocean Boulevard address. Those were rejected. Campus-road points at LMU and CSUDH were also rejected for door use. The Fred Kavli and Oxnard street matches were rejected after building-location comparison; Oxnard retains a separately sourced reported building reference, not a door. The Royce Hall, Cerritos and Segerstrom Hall / Concert Hall address queries did not return usable matches. No false precision is asserted for these failures.

**Public ticketing and room scope.** Some table rows are provisional: Palace Theatre, Yost and Ambassador need current recurring qualifying public-program evidence; several college outdoor venues need routine paid-admission verification. Private rental availability does not establish public ticketing. Fox Pomona's maximum covers single or combined rooms and cannot yet be assigned confidently to Grand Hall alone. Park/fair admission bundled with a concert or rodeo ticket is distinguished from a free walk-up event.

**Operating status.** Warner Grand is retained for historical logs while its late-2026 reopening remains unconfirmed. Burbank's restoration page and its August 2026 cancellation notice describe different status snapshots for Starlight Bowl; neither alone proves today's operating calendar.
