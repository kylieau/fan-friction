This file contains 66 researched room records, a near-threshold exclusion list, unresolved candidates, source failures, and a continuation checklist. **It is not a completed New York census or a completed multi-city answer.** Los Angeles and the remaining cities have not yet been researched in this installment.

## How to use this installment

The largest immediate additions include the Beacon, Terminal 5, Brooklyn Paramount, Brooklyn Steel, the Capitol, the Paramount in Huntington, multiple Lincoln Center halls, suburban theaters, and college arenas. Some public concert rooms publish only a rounded maximum; others publish different inventories for different layouts. The table preserves those distinctions.

- **Official** means published by the venue, university, owner, operator, or naming sponsor. It does not mean independently counted or guaranteed sellable on every night.
- **Reported** means a secondary publication or directory. **Estimated** means a calculation or approximation made in this research; an official source's own approximate number remains labeled official and approximate.
- Labels apply to audience quantities and geographic coordinates. Dates, street numbers, document identifiers, and Ticketmaster identifiers are identifiers, not estimates of attendance.
- **Coordinates are an unfinished mandatory field.** The supplied decimal pairs are labeled **reported venue reference points**, with a source for each. They have **not** been confirmed as the building's main entrance and must not be imported as verified entrance coordinates. No entrance coordinate was fabricated. A blank/unconfirmed cell does not imply the room lacks a location.
- **H0** means no post-2016 rename was found in the sources reviewed. It is **not** a certification that every name since 2016 has been exhaustively checked. Names that predate 2016 are mentioned only to prevent mistaken duplicates.
- A missing historical capacity pair means the change was not established, not that capacity stayed constant. Pandemic occupancy restrictions are not treated as permanent room configurations.
- Ticketmaster IDs are included only where a venue URL was actually found. Blank means not found/verified in this pass, not that Ticketmaster never lists the room. IDs from Live Nation's different URL scheme are not substituted.
- Rows are sorted by the **preferred current public-event capacity**, largest first; greater-than and approximate counts keep their uncertainty. A conflicting historical, cocktail, whole-building, or unspecified marketing maximum does not determine the row order.
- College campus relationships describe physical co-location, not shared ownership alone. “No larger venue established” is a research limitation, not a verified absence.
- Public sources only. No purchases, accounts, sign-ups, correspondence, or contact with venues were used.

### Unconfirmed details and decisions still open

**Entrance coordinates:** Every row still needs a front-door check. The reported venue points are useful for distinguishing buildings, but some are auditorium centers or campus/building references. This is particularly material at Pier 17, the Stone Pony Summer Stage, Lincoln Center, university campuses, and multilevel clubs. The Tilles Wikipedia title redirects to LIU Post; its campus coordinate was deliberately not reused for the concert hall.

**Names and histories:** H0 rows still need a systematic historical-name audit. Known incomplete transitions include the Paramount's TD Bank / Northwell suffix, Irving Plaza's Verizon suffix, Wellmont's Theatre / Theater spelling, URSB Carteret's sponsorship date, and the Great Hall / Brooklyn Hall / Brooklyn Pacha branding. Island Federal's current university page and sponsor naming need reconciliation. These are unresolved fields, not assumed permanent names.

**Public configurations:** No unknown standing capacity was inferred from floor area, fire occupancy, cocktail reception capacity, or a seat map. No seated capacity was inferred by subtracting a GA floor. The maximum published for the Paramount in Huntington does not establish each of its layout limits. The Monarch's retrieved official statement does not explicitly assign its maximum to a standing layout. Melrose remains provisional because its numerical setup evidence is a directory.

**Temporary closure:** The Apollo's current official rental page says its historic theater is closed for renovation and expects a late-2026 reopening. The future reopening is not treated as an accomplished event. The post-renovation seat inventory remains unknown.

**Yogi Berra Stadium:** University ownership and capacity are confirmed, and a Ticketmaster venue record exists, but recurring paid public events at that stadium in 2026 were not established. The Jackals' historical tenancy alone cannot establish current paid-event frequency.

**Threshold handling for Fan/Friction:** A near-five-thousand approximation is not evidence that a particular event reaches the app's larger-event threshold. Yogi Berra's approximate total and FDU's unspecified campus-marketing maximum need particular care. Venue size also does not establish actual event attendance. The app's own event eligibility rule remains the rule supplied in the brief.
