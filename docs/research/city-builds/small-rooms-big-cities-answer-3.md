## Reading the evidence

- **Official** means published by the room, operator, school, municipality, team or regulator. An official historical document is still historical; its age is identified when material. **Reported** means an external publication, designer’s project account, tourism directory or other cited source. No attendance capacity has been invented or inferred from floor area.
- Capacity quantities carry their status in each cell. Addresses, years, source dates and Ticketmaster venue IDs are identifiers or dates, not capacity estimates. An approximation published by an operator is labeled official and retains its qualifying language.
- The table is sorted by the selected maximum for the current room, with provisional/historical selections explicitly marked. It does not sort by attendance records, whole-building buyouts, or superseded capacities. Closely grouped approximations are not precise rankings.
- **Coordinates are a material unresolved field. No main-entrance coordinate has been certified in this batch.** Where independently retrieved, a cited **reported building point** is supplied as a clearly separated fallback. It is not an entrance pin and must not be silently imported as one. Otherwise the coordinate cell says “Not confirmed.” Do not geocode a whole campus to its central building and treat that as a room entrance.
- A blank Ticketmaster cell means no numeric venue-page ID was confirmed. It does not mean Ticketmaster has no listings. Linked numeric IDs were read from Ticketmaster venue URLs; they are not event IDs or AXS IDs. Ticketmaster’s resale/partner listings do not establish primary ticketing rights.
- “No different name found” means no post-2016 alias was established in the reviewed material, not proof that every sponsorship contract has been located. Where dates are only known to the year, exact days remain unconfirmed.
- Fixed-seat theater rows show the documented seated configuration. An absent standing figure means no public standing setup was confirmed, not that standing events are legally impossible. Reception, dinner, whole-building and ticketed-concert limits are kept distinct.
- The near-line choices are disclosed: dedicated venues with sourced maxima around the lower limit are retained down to a **reported capacity of 950**. Smaller borderline rooms are listed separately, rather than rounded up.
- Larger venues missing from the supplied app list are explicitly flagged. Campus notes describe physical relationships; sharing an operator alone does not make separate buildings the same room. “No larger connected venue identified” is a research finding, not a cadastral certification.
