# Milwaukee venue and event evidence for Fan/Friction

**Research cut-off: October 11, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles).** Historical evidence starts in 2016 unless a clearly labelled older figure explains a present-day conflict. Future announcements are checked through 2028. This is an evidence inventory for review, not a guarantee that every room, private rental or school venue has been captured.

Use **one shared Milwaukee geography for the sports and concert briefs**: the four-county Milwaukee–Waukesha metropolitan area as the core, with separately identified Racine/Kenosha corridor candidates. County inclusion identifies the search/listing scope; it does not mean every pair of events shares an access road. The recommendation and its limits are developed in section 1 at the end.

Anything remains manually loggable. A supported setup around **1,000** qualifies a public room for pre-listing; **observed or defensibly estimated event attendance of at least 5,000** is the separate friction threshold. A 12,000-capacity arena hosting a 2,000-person college game does not supply a 12,000 crowd. A festival's multi-day total does not supply a daily crowd. Unknown attendance remains unknown and must not be silently replaced with capacity.

## Reading the evidence

- **Capacity:** official = venue/team/league/public-authority publication; reported = secondary publication; estimated = explicit derivation with basis. Numbers are people/places unless called seats. Lawn/standing/accessible positions are distinguished from fixed chairs.
- **Attendance:** announced = organizer/team declaration; reported = published third-party account; estimated = a labelled estimate, including promotional expectations. Neither a sellout label nor square footage supplies an observed headcount. Series averages are context; dates still need their own evidence.
- **As of:** every table row was checked on **2026-10-11**; source dates, seasons and known effective dates are stated separately. An undated current webpage is evidence retrieved on that date, not proof of a 2026 renovation. “No dated change established” means the search did not establish one.
- **Coordinates:** latitude, longitude in that order. OSM features were checked using public OpenStreetMap/Nominatim name/address results and matched to locality. Five decimals are display precision (roughly a metre), **not surveyed positional accuracy**. Polygon points are mapped centroids/representative points, not entrances. A parent-building or campus point is expressly labelled when an interior room could not be independently pinned. Unknown exact room points remain visible; this report does not fabricate them or import same-name locations in other states.
- **Setups:** only sourced configurations appear. For every A row, **unlisted sport, end-stage, in-the-round, seated or standing capacities are unknown**, not equal to the row's maximum. A concert “360° sight lines” label is retained verbatim as a layout definition and is not automatically an in-the-round stage.
- **Feeds:** public calendar/ticket pages were verified where stated. A visible page is not proof of API completeness, polling access or a complete historical feed. IDs are included only when present in an observed source URL. “Unknown” means unverified coverage, not verified absence. No private API or paywall was bypassed.

## 1. Boundary recommendation and disputed edge venues

**Recommend the four-county Milwaukee–Waukesha metropolitan core for both briefs**, while preserving room-level points and event-specific travel evidence within it. This is the result of the search, not a rule that county lines always decide inclusion. The [July 2023 OMB metropolitan delineation](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/OMB-Bulletin-23-01.pdf) lists Milwaukee, Ozaukee, Washington and Waukesha in the Milwaukee–Waukesha MSA; metropolitan delineations use commuting integration. The [SEWRPC seven-county region](https://www.sewrpc.org/About-Us/Our-Region) is a broader planning area that also includes Racine, Kenosha and Walworth, useful for transportation context but insufficient to put every destination in one Milwaukee event envelope.

Audience catchment reaches farther than the recommended core. [VISIT Milwaukee’s 2022 visitor-origin report](https://www.visitmilwaukee.org/press-releases/post/milwaukee-sets-tourism-record-in-2022/) identifies Chicago and Madison among major visitor origins. That supports regional audience overlap; it does not establish that two simultaneous events in those cities share Milwaukee access roads. Keep **Madison, Green Bay and Chicago outside** this brief. Keep distant destination sites outside absent concrete event travel evidence; fame and nominal capacity do not justify enlarging the boundary.
