### Evidence conventions

- **Official capacity** means a school/league/operator publishes a seating setup; it is not attendance. The [WIAA basketball seating inventory](https://www.wiaawi.org/portals/0/pdf/sports/basketball_boys/bk_gyms.pdf) is dated **February 27, 2025**. All school figures below use that date. No seating change is inferred from a sponsor name, new floor or donation.
- **Announced attendance** is an organizer claim. **Reported attendance** is published reporting; when reporting quotes an organizer, that origin is stated. **Estimated attendance** includes organizer forecasts and approximate retrospective claims. Undisclosed counting methods remain unknown.
- A daily count is a whole-day measure, not peak simultaneous occupancy. A weekend total is not a daily count. Repeat entry, unique people, admissions, competitors, finishers and tickets sold are separate measures. No totals are divided into invented daily crowds.
- Room recommendations mean “eligible room identity for listings research.” Event-level evidence is still needed where the listing threshold is applied to actual/expected attendance. Unknown attendance stays unknown; no gym is recommended for friction from seats alone.
- **Unknown** is a researched unresolved candidate, not an assertion of closure or ineligibility. **Keep excluded** is used for an established exclusion, cancellation, inactive program or a mismatched setup—not merely a failed search.
- School coordinates below are **carried forward from the earlier Codex OSM-linked campus inventory**, not newly surveyed gym coordinates. Each is expressly a campus/building point; no interior-room point was established. Where the original lookup failed, no coordinate is invented. This limitation also applies to the three school-room recommendations.
- Public schedules are distinguished from a working app feed. Current official pages frequently link Bound or Arbiter Live, but a dynamic calendar shell is not proof of a complete schedule feed. The existing register verifies no school-gym adapter. No new adapter or provider ID is guessed.
