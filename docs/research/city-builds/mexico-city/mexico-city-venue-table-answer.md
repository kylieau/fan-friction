# Mexico City venue table — Fan/Friction city research

**As of:** Sunday 11 October 2026 (research pass dated this day; all "current" statements mean as of this date).
**Scope:** Mexico City (CDMX) plus connected State of Mexico municipalities as a working search area; Toluca, Puebla and Querétaro assessed separately as edge cases.
**Method note:** Figures come from public web pages found in Spanish and English searches on this date. Most venue figures come from press, government tourism pages, operator statements quoted in press, or reference sites; I did **not** directly fetch most official venue/league pages in this pass (see §5), so "official" below means "attributed to the operator/league/organizer by the linked page," not "read on the operator's own site." Labels used: **official** (operator/league/government/organizer figure), **reported** (press without clear operator attribution), **estimated** (approximate/marketing figure), **unknown** (not found).

### Recommendation (PROPOSAL — not a confirmed external change)

1. **Core boundary:** CDMX's 16 alcaldías plus the 59 State of Mexico municipalities of the ZMVM (and Tizayuca), i.e. the long-standing 76-unit definition. Every 5,000+ fixed venue found in this pass sits inside CDMX itself (Coyoacán, Tlalpan/Santa Úrsula, Iztacalco/Magdalena Mixhuca, Benito Juárez, Azcapotzalco, Cuauhtémoc, Miguel Hidalgo, Gustavo A. Madero).
2. **Do not auto-expand** to the August 2026 84-unit ZMVM. The seven new Hidalgo municipalities and Huitzilac were added for metropolitan planning; that is an administrative label, not evidence of a shared event catchment. Treat any qualifying venue there as an edge case to review individually.
3. **Toluca, Puebla, Querétaro:** keep as **separate cities**, not part of Mexico City. Exception handling needed: Cruz Azul (a Mexico City club) used Puebla's Estadio Cuauhtémoc as its registered home for Clausura 2026 (see §3). Record those games against the Puebla venue, linked to the team, rather than pulling Puebla into the Mexico City boundary.
