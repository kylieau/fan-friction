**Answer date:** Monday, October 12, 2026. **Local time zone:** Central Time (America/Chicago). **Window checked:** changes through 2028.

**Labels used throughout:** **O** = official (venue, team, league, government, university, organizer). **R** = reported (news, trade press, Wikipedia, directories). **E** = estimate or projection (organizer round numbers, expected crowds). ## 1. Recommended boundary and uncertain edge venues

### Recommended scope

Use **Bexar County as the core**, plus **named venues in Comal County (New Braunfels/Canyon Lake) and the Selma/I-35 corridor**, and treat Guadalupe (Seguin) and Kendall (Boerne) as *searched, nothing qualifying found* rather than excluded.

Reasoning:

- The federal metro definition is the **San Antonio–New Braunfels MSA**, which the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics lists as Atascosa, Bandera, Bexar, Comal, Guadalupe, Kendall, Medina and Wilson counties ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/regions/southwest/data/employmentandunemploymentandwages_sanantonio_table.htm)). The City of Austin's own MSA map places Hays County (San Marcos) in the Austin–Round Rock MSA, not San Antonio's ([City of Austin map](https://www.austintexas.gov/sites/default/files/files/Planning/Demographics/Austin_SA_MSA_2017.pdf)). That gives a defensible statistical line between the two covered cities.
- Every 5,000+ room found in this pass sits in Bexar County or in Selma/New Braunfels along I-35, which matches the event catchment rather than the full eight-county MSA.
- Atascosa, Bandera, Medina and Wilson counties are in the MSA but were **not searched** in this pass. Recommend leaving them out of default scope and adding venues by hand only if a specific room surfaces.

### 2.3 Rooms around the 1,000 / 5,000 thresholds that need care

- **Cowboys Dancehall (5,772)** and **R&J Music Pavilion (5,000 / 5,200)** clear 5,000 only on a city or fire-code figure; both are club/festival sites where most capacity is likely standing. Recommend treating as 5,000+ only if the model accepts standing capacity for friction; otherwise list and mute. Neither has a sourced seated split.
- **Whitewater (5,600)** is above 5,000 but in a sale process — keep, but re-check before 2027.
- **Sunken Garden Theater (4,800)** sits just under 5,000; a festival setup could exceed it but no source supports a higher figure.
- **Floore's outdoor (4,000)** vs. **indoor (800)**: same address, two rooms; the indoor room is below 1,000.
- **Benson Stadium (6,000)**: capacity qualifies; typical attendance reported just under 2,500 ([R](https://stadiumjourney.com/stadiums/gayle-and-tom-benson-stadium-s2472)). This is the clearest capacity-is-not-attendance case.
- **Alamodome UTSA setup (about 34,984)** vs. full stadium (64,000+): both above 5,000, but do not apply the 64,000 figure to UTSA games.
- **Freeman Coliseum (9,500–11,700 depending on setup)**: the rodeo setup (9,500) differs from concerts (11,700). Above 5,000 in every listed configuration.
- **Real Life Amphitheater**: only 6,000–8,000 are seats; the remaining 12,000–14,000 is lawn.
- **Boeing Center (3,100 standing)** and **The Espee (3,175 GA)**: both standing-dominated and below 5,000.
- **New Missions ballpark (7,500 max, 4,500 fixed)** will cross 5,000 only on the maximum figure — relevant from 2029.

### 5.2 Duplicate and matching risks

- **Freeman grounds parcel (A2, A3, Expo Halls, rodeo fairgrounds):** rodeo performances, concerts, expo-hall shopping and carnival all occur at once in February. Two addresses are in use (1 and 723 Frost Bank Center Dr). Match room events by building name, not by address or proximity.
- **Ticketmaster parking products:** Frost Bank Center parking is sold on Ticketmaster ([R](https://blog.ticketmaster.com/step-inside-frost-bank-center-san-antonio-tx/)); filter parking, suite, club and package listings so they do not create phantom events.
- **Alamodome parcel (A1, A23, Lots B/C):** Fiesta Carnival (Lot C), Alamo Bowl Tailgate Town (Lot B), and Illusions Theater events can share the venue name in listings.
- **Alamo Bowl game vs Fan Zone:** the game is an A1 room event; the Fan Zone is a B10 event at The Espee.
- **Henry B. González Convention Center vs Lila Cockrell Theatre:** listings may name the convention center; the ticketed room is the theatre.
- **Floore's indoor vs outdoor; Real Life seats vs lawn; Tobin flat-floor vs seated:** each is a separate configuration, not an additive total.
- **Spurs Austin games:** Ticketmaster labels them as Spurs home games at Moody Center ATX ([Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.ca/san-antonio-spurs-billets/artist/806012?page=1)); assign to Austin's room and do not duplicate in San Antonio.
- **Rodeo as a "venue":** some listing sites treat the San Antonio Stock Show & Rodeo as a venue name ([example](https://concerts.consequence.net/venues/san-antonio-stock-show-rodeo-tickets)); map to A2 for arena performances.
- **NCAA volleyball sessions and the LOVB opener** are separate events in the same week.
