# Baltimore — venue table answer (Fan/Friction city build)

**Answer date:** Tuesday 13 October 2026. **Time zone for all events:** America/New_York (ET).
**Method:** public web sources only, searched 13 Oct 2026. Every number below links to the page it came from. Labels used: **official** (venue, team, league, university, government or organizer page), **reported** (news, encyclopedia, trade or league-member page), **estimated** (the source itself calls it an estimate or approximation). "Gap" means no acceptable source was found; the search tried is stated. Nothing here is an invented figure.

**Two standing cautions.** Capacity is not attendance; the two are kept in separate columns. Several pages carry figures that differ by a few seats; those are shown side by side, and only conflicts that move a room across the 1,000 or 5,000 lines are called out as classification-changing.

### Duplicate and double-count flags

- **CFG Bank Arena add-on listings:** Ticketmaster sells "Fearless Club at CFG Bank Arena" and "The NINETEEN 62 at CFG Bank Arena" as separate events on the same night as the main show ([Ticketmaster venue page](https://ticketmaster.com/cfg-bank-arena-boletos-baltimore/venue/172041)). These are premium packages, not events — suppress.

## 10. Build notes (for whoever turns this into app data)

These are proposals and rules, not confirmed external facts.

- **IDs must be unique across all cities.** `giants`, `jets`, `rangers`, `kings` and `cardinals` are already taken. This answer could not see other cities' ID files, so prefix every Baltimore ID (proposal: `bal-orioles`, `bal-ravens`, `bal-blast`, `bal-camden-yards`, `bal-mt-bank-stadium`, `bal-cfg-bank-arena`). Navy needs a prefix too (`bal-navy`) to avoid any generic `navy` collision.
- **Opponents:** no separate per-city opponents file; opponent draws go into `expectedDrawIndex` like every other city.
- **Capacities:** never guess. Blank and on the city gaps list: Meyerhoff, Harbor Ballroom, Laurel Park building, fairgrounds buildings, Camden Yards 2026 effective, M&T post-renovation, UMBC arena basketball (conflict), all concert setups for the two stadiums.
- **Car shares:** no measured or sourced estimated mode-share figures were found for any Baltimore venue. Leave all on the city default.
- **Time zone:** America/New_York; no international naming or data-rights issues for this market.
- **Tampa Bay precedent applied:** no regional rename is warranted; code stays BAL.
