# Proposal: Compare, rebuilt around your nights

Oct 6, 2026. Item 6 of the reordered plan (`docs/big-picture-plan-oct6.md`). Nothing here is built. Kylie approves, then it is built in slices, each shown on the local site. The Oct 4 review locked the frame: compare nights and personal stats, never fanbases, never people.

## Where Compare lives
Favorites took Compare's tab on Oct 5. Compare does not get a tab back. It lives in two places people already are:
1. **You → Stats** grows into "your year" (section 1 below). It is already the stats screen; the new lines slot in.
2. **"Compare with…" on any night** (a date page or an event page you attended) opens a side-by-side (section 2). The unlinked `/compare` stub becomes that side-by-side page, reached only from a night.

No new tab, no new chrome. The Oct 3 rule holds: rehome, don't delete.

## 1. Your year (You → Stats)
Above today's counts (Events, Venues, Heaviest, Hours at games), one block, this calendar year with a flip to all time:
- **"4 Heavy nights, 1 Cooked."** Your nights by friction word, as a short bar.
- **Heaviest and quietest night**, each a tappable row to that night.
- **Your usual**: the median read of your nights ("Your usual night is Mild, 3.2"). This is the yardstick section 2 uses.
- **Most-seen team, most-visited venue** (already counted; shown as a line, not a list).
- Streaks and badges stay out (locked Oct 3).

## 2. Two nights side by side
From a night you attended, **Compare with…** opens a picker of your other nights (search, newest first, Famous nights at the bottom as the demo). Pick one and the page shows the two in columns:

| | Fri, Oct 25, 2024 | Tue, Apr 1, 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Read | 9.2 Cooked | 4.5 Mild |
| Crowd fight | 9.2 | 4.3 |
| Conditions | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| Gridlock | 4.1 | 2.0 |
| What else was on | World Series G1, Lakers, USC, East LA Classic, Gilmour, ELO | Kings vs. Jets |
| Your night | USC vs. Rutgers · 63,404 announced · 3h 42m | Dodgers vs. Braves · 50,182 · 2h 58m |
| Weather | 62° feels-like | 68° |

Under the columns, one line against your usual: "Both above your usual Mild." The share card at the bottom becomes a two-night card (text and link, as today).

Famous nights can be the second column, which is the Oct 4 review's "against a famous night." Someone else's night can be the second column later, once logs are shared; it is not in this proposal.

## 3. Dates for the same tour or homestand (forward-looking)
On an upcoming event page with others of its kind within 30 days (the same artist's other LA dates, the same homestand), a small strip: "Also on Fri 7.1 · Sat 4.2 · Sun 2.8." It needs concerts to be interesting, so it ships after Ticketmaster. Listed here so the design is whole.

## What this does not do
No ranking of people. No fanbase comparison. No "worst night" lists. No new numbers: everything shown is a read or a fact the app already computes. Nothing changes the formula.

## Slices
1. Your year block in Stats (half a day).
2. Compare with… picker and the side-by-side page (a day).
3. Same-tour strip, after Ticketmaster.
