# Build 6 mockup pack: manifest

Built Oct 17, 2026, 7:15 AM PT (read-only audit of `/workspace/ff-claims/mosaic-build6/`; no source file was changed). Every image is a JPEG copy of Mock Mosaic's board, 1,600 to 2,400 px wide, at most 1.5 MB each (tall boards are cut into `_partNofM` files, top to bottom). Text on every board is readable at full size.

**How to use.** The files in this folder are the boards that still apply to build 6 (current, or current with the deviations listed). `_superseded-do-not-send/` holds boards that contradict a decision; do not attach them to Claude, they are there so we know what they show. The notes text wins wherever a board and the notes differ.

**Read first: decided after the boards were drawn.** These decisions came after the boards below were rendered; boards that show the old look say so in their row.
1. Chips, Local Competition bars, Stubs rows, Following cards: NO ring, NO tag, NO friction color (principle; the final boards already show it for chips and bars).
2. Local Competition bars are GOLD (`crowdBar #E3A600`), no violet, no ring, no outline; quiet-day line is "No other big events that day."; no read tile.
3. All four friction pills use ONE violet tint (`#E6DDF8` / `#3F2A78`), Unopposed included; the event page's tag row DROPS the pill; the Crowd card shows pill plus a 16 px ring to the LEFT of the pill; the "Up against" card keeps its pill with a ring; the share card keeps its pill.
4. No "~" on compact surfaces, in rival rows or on the share card; "~" only on the event page's full figure next to "Estimated" and in the map-key line.
5. Avatar stays on the You tab ONLY (not every tab); Settings opens from it as a side panel of dropdowns.
6. Team badges: sport icon top-LEFT; college code carries sport letters ("UCLA MBB", on a clash); NO W mark; NO corner mark; pro same-sport pairs are an OPEN question (options A to D in notes 9a). Run labels are on HOLD.
7. Stubs and Following are one timeline with MONTH bands and a today line (no day bands, no Recent/Upcoming split).
