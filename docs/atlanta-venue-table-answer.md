# Atlanta Venues 5,000+

As of October 7, 2026.

## How to read this

The list covers every venue inside the City of Atlanta and the 11-county core that holds about 5,000 or more in at least one setup. Each venue gets one row, largest first, sorted by its biggest regular setup. I kept the boundary as briefed. I found no reason to move it, since Sanford Stadium and the Macon, Columbus and Chattanooga venues are all 60+ miles out.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **(O) Official** | Published by the venue, its owner, the team, the league or the event organizer |
| **(R) Reported** | Press or a secondary reference (Wikipedia, ticketing guides, architects); not confirmed on an owner page |
| **(E) Estimated** | My figure, with the reasoning given; load it only until something better turns up |

**Which figure to load:** the **bold** number in each capacity cell is the one I'd put in the app. Standing-room and festival figures are listed separately from seated ones.

**Coordinates** are my own map read of each building to 3 decimals (about 100 m). Treat them as approximate and check them before loading. Where I wasn't confident, I wrote "not confirmed."

**The "Constrained site?" column** is my judgment unless a source is cited.

## Venues, largest first

Six things in the brief are out of date:
- Atlanta Motor Speedway is now **EchoPark Speedway** (June 2025).
- Coolray Field is now **Gwinnett Field** (Jan 2026).
- Chastain is now **Synovus Bank Amphitheater** (Mar 2025).
- Lakewood has dropped Cellairis and is **Lakewood Amphitheatre** again.
- **Shaky Knees moved to Piedmont Park** in 2025.
- State Farm Arena's 18,118 was the 2013–14 figure.
