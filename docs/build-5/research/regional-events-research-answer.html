Scoring and crowd estimation

Model event-generated vehicle trips by origin, mode, occupancy, route, and arrival/departure hour. Include metro residents, through-travelers, airport users and visitors staying in metro hotels. Only the in-footprint portion contributes to the metro score. Shared freeway or airport use is a mechanism to investigate, not proof of significant delay. An attendee camping outside the metro generates long-distance travel on arrival and departure days, not automatically on each festival day.

Use past daily gate counts first; otherwise use a median of comparable editions and redistribute a cumulative visit total across actual open days using historical day shares. A multi-day pass holder attending three days is three person-days. Never divide unique visitors or passes sold by festival days. Do not add included concerts, rodeos or air displays to the parent event's gate attendance. Record source year, count definition, estimate method and uncertainty.

Source priority

  1. Daily scans and operational reports: strongest attendance and route evidence, but limited public access.
  2. Official organizer results and university statistics: strong historical counts; marketing estimates and capacity need distinct labels.
  3. Official schedules, fair associations and sanctioning bodies: strong discovery coverage within their categories; generally weak attendance coverage. IAFE, NASCAR, PRCA.
  4. Pollstar: useful reported ticket sales and music history, not complete daily headcounts. Reporting policies.
  5. Tourism agencies and attributed local reporting: useful cross-checks; retain attribution and original definitions.
  6. General listings, lineups and social posts: discovery leads; confirm dates, venue and attendance upstream.

Recommended operating rules

  • Discover events likely above 5,000 attendees on at least one day inside the metro or within a 120-minute baseline drive of a designated core.
  • Allow farther exceptions when documented resident travel, airport use, shared corridors or historic delay establish relevance.
  • Show outside-footprint events in “Further out” and an optional map layer; map visibility never automatically adds score.
  • Score estimated incremental in-metro road delay by corridor and hour, including travel before opening and after closing.
  • Default to comparable past daily attendance; distinguish visits, unique visitors, passes, capacity and concurrent crowd.
  • Preserve sources, dates, definitions, confidence and deduplication; calibrate against actual traffic.
Method: published evidence plus explicitly labeled calculations and product recommendations. A recurring event's next edition must be verified before ingestion.
