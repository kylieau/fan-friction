  surfaced most of the sample I checked, and AXS also lists Goldenvoice co-promoted DICE
  shows like the PAWSA example. Trial JamBase as a normalized multi-seller feed before
  committing.

        Biggest misses are not on any one alternative seller. Insomniac megafestivals sell on
        Front Gate, arena shows on AXS, pop-ups on DICE, and LA Phil Hollywood Bowl shows
        on the Bowl's own box office.
        Your example is catchable. PAWSA (Oct 17) has an AXS listing whose buy button hands
        off to DICE, and Framework's site shows a waitlist, which signals a sell-out.
        Pop-up capacity is the hard part. Permits and city contracts are the most reliable
        source; promoter press releases are the usual one in practice; aggregator capacity
        fields are often wrong.
        Outside LA: AXS holds up in Las Vegas (MGM/AEG arenas). In Chicago the big crowds
        sell through festival sites, so AXS adds little there.

  3. Recommendation
  Start with the SeatGeek API plus an AXS crawl: SeatGeek is the one official, free events
  API, and AXS holds the arena shows and the Goldenvoice co-promoted DICE pop-ups.

    1. SeatGeek API (catch-all). Query by city and date window each night. Resale listings
        appear for pop-ups (Hugel was there) and for sold-out shows, which are exactly the big
        ones.
    2. AXS venue and artist pages. Crawl the LA venue pages (Crypto.com Arena, Shrine,
        Greek, Peacock) and Goldenvoice listings. Check the AXS terms of use first, since
        there is no public API.

    3. Trial JamBase before building more scrapers. If its claimed AXS, DICE and Front Gate
        coverage holds for this sample, it replaces steps 2 and 4.
    4. Small promoter watch list for the highest-impact pop-ups: Framework (REST API),
        Insomniac and Goldenvoice. These also give the best capacity statements.

  Resale as catch-all: partial. It catches sold-out and high-demand shows across sellers,
  but misses free events, non-transferable tickets (the Bowl's $1 seats cannot be resold, per
  NBC LA), and shows that sell slowly. Resale pages also sometimes use vague venue
  names, so match on date and coordinates, not venue strings.

  Next step: run SeatGeek and JamBase against the 25-event table above and count hits
  before choosing.


  4. Pop-up sizing
  Permits and city contracts give the most trustworthy numbers, but for LA pop-ups the
  number you can actually get is usually a promoter press release; use it, discounted, and
  flag the source.

  Proposed sizing rule
  The percentages below are my proposal, not sourced; calibrate them against permit or
  police counts as you collect them.

    1. Start from the best capacity available, in the reliability order above. For multi-day
       events, use the per-day figure.

    2. Default expected crowd = 80% of stated capacity.
    3. Raise to 95% if the seller shows sold out or a waitlist (PAWSA's waitlist on Framework's
        site is this signal), or if resale prices sit above face value.
    4. No capacity found: use the same venue's or brand's best past result (Reframe lot:
       9,000).
    5. No history either: estimate from usable area. Clark County's outdoor-assembly guide
       uses 7 sq ft per person (Clark County); mark the result "estimated".
    6. Still nothing: keep the event on the map as "unsized, likely 5,000+" if it is a lot, park or
        beach show from a promoter with a 5,000+ track record; drop it otherwise.

  Caveats and unverified items
        Not a full census. The LA table is what searches surfaced; Peacock Theater, Shrine
        Auditorium, Greek Theatre (split between AXS and Ticketmaster) and Intuit Dome
        shows were not checked show by show.
        Seller platforms marked "festival site" (Ocean Way, Just Like Heaven, ARC,
        Lollapalooza) were not traced to the underlying ticketing company.
        No capacity found for PAWSA's Rio Vista Lot, California Plaza, Sun Soaked, Just Like
        Heaven, Head in the Clouds or ARC.
        API access and terms for AXS, Front Gate, StubHub and Vivid Seats were not
        confirmed; read each terms page before automated collection.
        Free shows are invisible to every seller here; none were sized in this pass.
        The sizing percentages (80% / 95%) are a proposal to calibrate, not a measured rate.
