## Normal day vs. event night (model)
No park publishes daily or nightly attendance. ## Claude's check (Oct 6, 2026)
- Knott's own page confirms a separate ticket and the Sept 17–Oct 31 window. Universal's and SeaWorld's calendars load by script, so their night-by-night dates rest on fan and travel sites (which agree).
- The useful split is **close-and-reopen** (Universal, Knott's, Disney California Adventure, SeaWorld: a new evening crowd with an evening arrival and a late exit) versus **continuous** (Six Flags Magic Mountain, Wild Waves, Great Adventure: same day crowd, same ticket). The continuous ones fit Kylie's "ordinary park day" rule and stay out.
- The gap: no crowd size is published for any of them, and the formula needs a size for an event to count toward friction.

## Kylie's call (Oct 6, 2026)
**Option A.** The four close-and-reopen events (Halloween Horror Nights, Knott's Scary Farm, Oogie Boogie Bash, Howl-O-Scream) go in the catalog as loggable special events but do **not** feed friction until a real crowd figure exists (a published capacity or a reported nightly number). Same treatment as rooms under the floor. Continuous Fright Fest-style nights (Six Flags, Wild Waves) stay out, like ordinary park days.
