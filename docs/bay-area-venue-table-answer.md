# Bay Area venues of 5,000 or more — capacity by setup

**Researched and written: October 7, 2026.** Every figure is "as of Oct 2026" unless a different date is shown next to it.

**Labels used on every number**

- **(official)**: the venue, team, league, university or organizer says so on its own page or in its own release.
- **(reported)**: news outlets, Wikipedia, ticketing sites or other secondary sources.
- **(estimated)**: my own derivation, with the method shown.

Coordinates marked **≈** are approximate (good to roughly 100 m) and were not checked against a source in this pass. Unmarked coordinates come from the linked source.

**Boundary used:** as briefed. That means San Francisco; Alameda and Contra Costa counties; San Mateo County plus Stanford and Mountain View; and Santa Clara County. Note that Stanford and Mountain View are in **Santa Clara County**, not San Mateo, and the county column below says so. I would not move the line. Sacramento, Napa and Sonoma stay out, and nothing in Marin or Solano reaches 5,000. The one addition worth making inside the line is the **Alameda County Fairgrounds** in Pleasanton, which the brief didn't name but which is in Alameda County.

**One duplicate in the brief:** "Provident Credit Union Event Center" and "the Event Center at San José State" are the same building. The sponsor name was added in 2019. It gets one row below.

## 3. What I could not confirm

1. **Oracle Park:** 41,265 vs 41,915. Neither came from the Giants directly, and I found no source for 41,331. Check the 2026 Giants media guide.
2. **Memorial Stadium:** the pre-2012 capacity, and whether the tarping began in 2022 or 2024.
3. **Bill Graham** all-seated capacity (~7,000 in the brief).
4. **Levi's** concert capacity, and whether 75,000 has actually been sold since 2014. It was not used for the World Cup.
5. **Chase Center** concert figure (19,500) beyond secondary sources.
6. **SAP Center** concert figure: 19,190 vs 18,500.

## 5. Moscone Center and the San Jose McEnery Convention Center

**My answer: don't treat either as a venue row. Log their big events as dated crowd events in section 4.**

- **Moscone** (≈37.7841, −122.4010; SoMa, SF) has no seating capacity, and convention attendance is not one simultaneous crowd. **Dreamforce** draws **40,000+ in person** (reported) across three buildings over three days, with badge-holders cycling in and out. That is big enough to matter on SoMa roads and BART, and it is less than 1.5 km from Oracle Park and Chase Center. Log it as a dated event at ~40,000/day (estimated: the reported in-person total, assumed present on each day as a worst case). Its evening concert ("Dreamfest") should be logged at whatever building hosts it. **GDC** is not confirmed for 2026.
- **San Jose McEnery Convention Center** (≈37.3297, −121.8889): **FanimeCon** drew ~28,000 (reported, estimated by the organizer, 2024) over four days on Memorial Day weekend. That is under 10,000 at any one time by any reasonable spread, so it's a minor event. It does sit in the same downtown cluster as SAP Center, which matters if a Sharks playoff game or big concert lands that weekend. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FanimeCon)

## 7. Shared-site pairs for the crowd read

These buildings share roads or transit closely enough that two events on one night should be read as **one crowd**, not two:

- **Coliseum + Oakland Arena:** same lots, same I-880 exits, same BART station.
- **Oracle Park + Chase Center:** ~1 km apart, same Muni T line and Third St.
- **Memorial Stadium + Greek Theatre:** ~300 m apart on the same hillside streets.
- **Stanford Stadium + Maples + Frost:** one campus cluster.
- **Bill Graham + Civic Center Plaza:** same block.
- **SAP Center + SJ Convention Center + Discovery Meadow:** downtown San Jose cluster.
- **CEFCU Stadium + Provident CU Event Center:** ~1.6 km apart, both SJSU.
- **Kezar + Golden Gate Park festival meadows:** same park.

The reverse holds too. Levi's, Shoreline, the Coliseum and Oracle Park are 30–45 miles apart on different freeways, which is the distance flaw the one-metro boundary is meant to expose.
