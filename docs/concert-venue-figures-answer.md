# Answer: published concert crowds per venue (four covered cities)

Answers the research brief "Published concert crowds per venue (four covered cities)" (drafted Oct 7, 2026). Researched October 2026 from free web sources only; no paywalled pages were read (Billboard's own chart pages returned a paywall and were not used directly).

**Labels:** **announced** = venue/operator/public agency said it · **reported** = named outlet or Billboard Boxscore/Pollstar as quoted in free coverage · **estimated** = my arithmetic, shown.

**Bottom line:** Only six rooms have both a published attendance figure and a published show count for the same period (MSG, Kia Forum, Barclays, Prudential, Intuit Dome, YouTube Theater). Each of those comes from Billboard's chart year (Oct 1–Sep 30), not a calendar year, and counts every ticketed non-team event Billboard received a report for. That means concerts plus comedy, family shows and boxing, and never pro team games. No venue in the four areas publishes a concerts-only, calendar-year "X shows, Y fans" release that I could find. San Diego and Seattle have **no** room with both numbers.

### How to read Billboard and Pollstar figures
- **Billboard year-end "Top Venues"** cover Nov 1–Oct 31 for 2022, Nov 1, 2022–Sep 30, 2023 for 2023, and Oct 1–Sep 30 for 2024 and 2025. They rank by gross and list attendance and shows only for engagements that were reported to Boxscore. Unreported shows are missing, so the per-show figure is the average of *reported* shows.
- **Pollstar year-end** covers roughly mid-Nov to mid-Nov and counts tickets from reported shows. Free articles often give tickets but not show counts.
- Attendance in Billboard's free tables is rounded (1.1M, 962K). The ± ranges below come from that rounding.

## 2. New York Comic Con

**Organizer wording (RX/ReedPop press release, Oct 2025):**
> "New York Comic Con (NYCC) concluded another successful event, as more than 250,000 attendees made their way through the Javits Center from October 9 through October 12"

[RX Global release](https://rxglobal.com/new-york-comic-con-wraps-2025-show-over-250000-attendance-weekend-celebrity-appearances-comic-icons)

**NYCC press kit, "2025 Milestones":** "250,000 fans attended" and "570+ panels and events over 4 days". [NYCC press kit](https://www.newyorkcomiccon.com/en-us/industry/press/press-kit.html)

**What kind of total it is:** The organizer doesn't say. The 2025 wording never says unique attendees, badges or per-day. The evidence leans toward **tickets/badge-days (person-days)**, not unique people:
- In 2006, Reed explicitly reported "unique attendees," and ICv2 noted that other shows count turnstile entries instead. [ICv2](https://icv2.com/articles/comics/view/8738/new-york-comic-con-announces-2007-dates) That method statement has not been repeated since.
- In 2015, ReedPop's own figure was given as "169,000 tickets sold." [The Beat](https://www.comicsbeat.com/nycc-15-biggest-ever-with-167000-tickets-sold)
- Of the 2018 "250,000" figure, The Licensing Letter wrote, citing Rob Salkowitz at ICv2: "The organizer is actually counting ticket sales, and many fans bought more than one ticket… daily convention badges." [The Licensing Letter](https://www.thelicensingletter.com/?p=1946) This is reported commentary, not an organizer statement.
- A 2025 attendee review: "it's unclear whether this is a turnstile or unique attendee count." [flannel.ninja](https://www.flannel.ninja/articles/new_york_comic_con_2025)

**Daily cap:** None published. The same Licensing Letter piece reports, secondhand from Salkowitz, that "the total capacity of the Javits Center is supposed to be 70,000 heads." That is not an organizer or Javits figure. In 2021 ReedPop said it was "working closely with each convention center to determine how many people are permitted in the building every day and at a given time" but gave no number. [Shelf Awareness](https://www.shelf-awareness.com/theshelf/2021-04-23/n.y._comic_con%C2%A0plans_october_show_with_very_limited_attendance.html)

If you treat 250,000 as person-days over 4 days, that's 62,500 a day (estimated). This only holds under that assumption.

## 4. Caveats

1. **Billboard's "shows" mix in non-concerts.** Billboard counts every reported ticketed engagement except team home games: comedy, family shows, boxing/UFC, award shows. A press release about Moody Center noted Billboard counted 94 shows against the venue's 140 ticketed events, so the coverage is also incomplete. [CultureMap](https://austin.culturemap.com/news/entertainment/moody-center-highest-grossing-2025/)
2. **Chart years are not calendar years.** Billboard YE 2025 = Oct 1, 2024–Sep 30, 2025. Pollstar ≈ mid-Nov to mid-Nov. Don't mix them in one average without noting it.
3. **The two Kia Forum sources disagree.** Billboard's 10,890 includes smaller setups and comedy runs. Pollstar's 14,549 covers 47 reported 2024 shows. Same room, two trades, about 34% apart.
4. **Rounding.** Billboard's free tables round to two or three significant figures; ranges are shown above.
5. **Season totals.** The Hollywood Bowl and Rady Shell totals are season totals with "over"/"nearly" wording. Per-show numbers are only upper bounds or can't be computed. Bowl totals include $1 and free tickets.
6. **Seattle Center's Climate Pledge figures** are total "visitors" including NHL/WNBA games. They are useless for concert averages.
7. **MetLife's Pollstar total** includes soccer.
8. **Radio City's mid-year total** is dominated by 191 Christmas Spectacular performances.
9. **Gorge's 88,000 for two Illenium nights** is far above the room's commonly cited single-day capacity. It may count multi-day or camping passes. Treat it as unverified.
10. **Renames and rebuilds since 2022:** Microsoft Theater → Peacock Theater. Honda Center's $1.1B renovation is phased, began fall 2024, runs to 2027, and the venue stays open; capacity effect not published. [OCBJ](https://www.ocbj.com/tourism/honda-center-set-for-1-1b-overhaul/) Intuit Dome opened Aug 15, 2024, and the Clippers left Crypto.com Arena that year. Pechanga Arena's lease moved to Midway Rising in Aug 2024 (Legends operating).
11. **Market totals are not venue figures.** Pollstar's 2025 Concert Market Rankings (Nov 16, 2023–Nov 13, 2024) give NY 9,623,396 tickets / 3,723 shows, LA 4,719,798 / 1,146 and Seattle-Tacoma 2,777,182 / 1,130. That works out to roughly 2,580 / 4,120 / 2,460 per show (est.), across all venue sizes. These are listed for context only and are not a rung-3 value. [Pollstar CMR chart PDF](https://data.pollstar.com/Chart/2025/08/122324_cmr.chart_1042.pdf)
