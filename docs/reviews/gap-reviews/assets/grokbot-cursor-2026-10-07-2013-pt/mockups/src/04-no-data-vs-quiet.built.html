04 · No data must not look like a quiet night

Sep 23 to Oct 2, Oct 5, Oct 30 and Nov 3 all show "— QUIET" in the strip and blank cells on the calendar. Oct 5 is "Quiet" in LA, NY and Seattle at once, which points to missing data, not calm nights. Open one of those dates and the page says "NO READ YET · Nothing big on file." That's three messages for one state. Data-viz practice: never draw a missing value the way you draw zero or low. WCAG 1.4.1: don't rely on color alone, so use a pattern and a word.

Stripes are the usual chart pattern for "no data" and can't be confused with any color on the scale. One line names the whole gap, so ten striped days read as "data hole," not "dead week." Calendar cells stay wordless stripes to cut clutter, and the legend sits right under the calendar (that's a calendar legend, not a map legend, so it doesn't break the map-key lock). example Same value mapping as Option 1.
