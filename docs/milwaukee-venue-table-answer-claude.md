# Milwaukee — venue and crowd evidence for Fan/Friction

**Research date (as-of for every row unless a different date is shown): 2026-10-11.**
Evidence for review only. No app code, hand ratings or formula constants are proposed.

## Conventions

- **Capacity labels:** *official* = published by the venue, team, university or operator; *reported* = news outlet or reference work; *estimated* = derived, or from a low-quality source.
- **Attendance labels:** *announced* = team/organizer release; *reported* = news or reference work; *estimated* = includes organizer pre-event expectations.
- **Coordinates:** four buildings carry coordinates published on the linked Wikipedia page (precision noted). Every value prefixed `~` is an approximate placement from the sourced street address, likely within ±200–400 m, **not verified against OpenStreetMap** (OSM could not be queried from this research environment). Treat `~` values as geocoding placeholders, not evidence.
- **Thresholds from the brief:** pre-list ≈1,000+; friction feed ≥5,000. Capacity is never used as attendance; crowd evidence is reported separately.
- **Table layout:** Table A is split into four linked sub-tables (A1–A4) that share one row ID and the same largest-first order, so each distinct room keeps exactly one row in each sub-table without an unreadable 12-column table. Rows sort by the largest supported setup; unknown-capacity rooms and the closed building sit at the end.
- **"Unknown"** means no public evidence was found, or the source was inaccessible (named in closing note 4).

### How to avoid counting the same audience twice

1. **Room inside an open site on festival days:** use the event-level crowd (E1, E4); treat the room's ticketed audience as a component unless the organizer reports it separately and on separate days (E2 is the one documented case).
2. **State Fair Main Stage** tickets include fair admission, so Main Stage crowds are always inside E4.
3. **Season aggregates** (S1 1.1M; S6 1.9M) include the events listed beneath them — never add them to event rows.
4. **Multi-day totals** are left as totals. Only the IndyCar per-day figure is usable as a daily crowd, because the promoter supplied it. Summerfest's "daily average" is a derived mean.
5. **Rave complex rooms** (A11, A18, A21) can host simultaneous shows; each is its own crowd, but aggregator feeds collapse them to one venue name.
6. **Deer District cluster** (A05, A09, A06, A10, A22 within blocks): separate crowds that share approaches, garages and transit.

## Closing notes

### 1. Boundary recommendation and disputed edge venues

- **Core (recommended): Milwaukee, Waukesha, Ozaukee and Washington counties.** This is exactly the OMB-defined Milwaukee–Waukesha MSA ([Wikipedia: Milwaukee metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milwaukee_metropolitan_area)), whose delineation is built on commuting ties; 2025 population 1,575,010. Every friction-scale venue found (A01, A02, A04–A07) sits in Milwaukee County; Ozaukee (A15) and the Waukesha/Washington suburbs contribute listing-scale rooms only.
- **Racine County — proposed edge (listings).** It belongs to the wider Milwaukee–Racine–Waukesha CSA, which also includes Dodge, Jefferson and Walworth ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milwaukee_metropolitan_area)); CSAs join metro areas with an employment-interchange rate of at least 15% ([Wikipedia: WI statistical areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisconsin_statistical_areas)). Shared roads with Milwaukee crowds are mainly the I-94 corridor. No 5,000+ Racine venue was identified (Racine was not searched exhaustively).
- **Kenosha County — edge, list-only.** Kenosha became its own MSA in the 2023 delineation ([WI DOR 2025](https://www.revenue.wi.gov/DORReports/2025-03-wi-msa-outlook.pdf)) and the cited table lists it outside the Milwaukee CSA ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisconsin_statistical_areas)). It is inside the seven-county SEWRPC planning region ([SEWRPC map](https://www.sewrpc.org/Files/About-Us/MPO-Role-and-Responsibilities/Metropolitan-Planning-Area-Boundary-Map-2020.pdf)) and the M7 economic group ([Kenosha County](https://wi-kenoshacounty.civicplus.com/273/Labor-Market-Information)). Kenosha leads (Kenosha Kingfish ballpark, Bristol Renaissance Faire, Carthage College) were **not verified** in this research.
- **Walworth County / Alpine Valley (A03) — disputed edge.** CSA membership and attendees from Racine and Madison show **regional audience overlap**, but the site does not share downtown or I-94-west approaches with core venues. Recommend listing it as an edge venue with its own separate road context rather than enlarging the core.
- **Exclude:** Madison, Green Bay, Chicago, and Road America at Elkhart Lake (about 60 miles north per [AP](https://www.castanetkamloops.net/news/Auto-Racing/448665/IndyCars-return-marks-major-comeback-for-120-year-old-Milwaukee-Mile)) despite overlapping IndyCar audiences. Summerfest's 48% out-of-state ticket buyers show audience catchment far beyond any commuting boundary; that is overlap of audiences, not crowds sharing roads, and does not justify widening the area.
