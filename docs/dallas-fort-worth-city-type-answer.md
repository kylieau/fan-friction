# Dallas–Fort Worth: how crowds reach the large venues

*Research for Fan/Friction. Compiled 7 October 2026. Every number below has a label: **Official** (published by an agency, team or venue), **Reported** (news coverage quoting officials), or **Estimated** (my own derivation, with the method shown). Where I estimate, I show the inputs so you can redo the math.*

---

## 1. The short answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Region type | **Driving region.** It is LA-like or more so. No large venue I could measure clears 20% transit, even on the busiest transit day of the year. |
| Why | Arlington, which hosts the three biggest buildings, has no fixed-route transit at all and the stadiums are 6–8 miles from the nearest rail stop ([Gigafact](https://gigafact.org/?p=205), [NBC DFW](https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/sports/soccer/world-cup-fans-transit-dallas-fort-worth-arlington/4033718/)). The suburban venues (Frisco, Allen, Denton, Fort Worth west side, the Speedway) are built around surface lots. DART rail reaches only American Airlines Center, Fair Park, SMU and Irving's Music Factory. |
| Where it breaks | **Fair Park during the State Fair** (≈13% transit, ≈18% on Red River Showdown day) and **American Airlines Center** (≈8%). Everything else is 90%+ car. |
| Your expectations | Confirmed with two corrections: AAC's DART/TRE share is real but **high single digits, not a large minority**; and the AAC result has an expiry date, since the Mavericks and Stars both plan to leave for car-oriented sites when the lease ends in 2031 ([NBC Sports/AP](https://www.nbcsports.com/nhl/news/nhls-dallas-stars-plan-move-to-suburbs-in-5-years-with-nbas-mavs-also-leaving-downtown-then)). |

**AAC method (Estimated):** DART reports Victory Station averaged **1,038 boardings per weekday in FY22**, averaged across event and non-event days ([Wikipedia, citing the DART Reference Book](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victory_station)). I assumed ≈300 non-event commuter boardings and events on roughly half of weekdays. That gives ≈1,500 extra boardings per event night, and almost every fan riding home boards once at Victory. 1,500 ÷ ~19,000 ≈ **8%**. FY22 was a post-COVID low year, so the current number could be somewhat higher, which is why the range runs to 12%. The 2026 station table (DART Reference Book v17) would sharpen this; I couldn't open it.

**Venues where I found no published figure:** every venue except Fair Park (fair days) and AT&T Stadium (World Cup / Super Bowl / Final Four) relies on an estimate. I found **no** published mode split, intercept survey or event-day station count for AAC, Globe Life Field, Dickies Arena, Toyota Stadium, any campus venue, the Speedway or Lone Star Park. The nearest comparables I used: Fair Park non-fair events are scaled from fair days; SMU and the Music Factory are scaled from AAC (rail beside or near the venue, but less service); Globe Life and Choctaw are scaled from AT&T's non-World Cup baseline.

## 5. The Arlington double date (the Gridlock case)

| Item | What the record shows | Label | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| How often | Rare by schedule design. In 2025 the only same-night overlap was 22 August (Cowboys–Falcons preseason with Rangers–Guardians). | City plan | Arlington PD staggers lot openings (Cowboys lots 10 a.m., Rangers 11:30 a.m.) and brings in officers from outside agencies. | Clearance time | The only published figure: post-game closures and reversed lanes stay in place **1–2 hours after the final whistle** for a single Cowboys game. I found **no published clearance time or vehicle counts for a double date**. | Reported | [Dallas Morning News](https://www.dallasnews.com/news/transportation/article/at-t-stadium-traffic-22406488.php) |
| Concert + Rangers precedent | Taylor Swift's 2023 AT&T Stadium shows overlapped Rangers opening weekend; police predicted the weekend days would be worst and restricted street parking with towing. | Reported | [Dallas Morning News](https://www.dallasnews.com/news/2023/03/28/going-a-taylor-swift-concert-or-texas-rangers-game-in-arlington-dont-try-to-park-here/) |
| Demand vs. supply | Estimated: ~80,000+ Cowboys plus ~35,000–40,000 Rangers ≈ 115,000–120,000 people. At 2.5–3 people per car that is ≈40,000–48,000 vehicles against ≈24,000 official spaces, so roughly half park in private lots, at Six Flags, or come by rideshare. Transit share ≈0%. | Estimated | spaces: [AT&T Stadium](https://www.attstadium.com/parking) |
| Studies you suggested | The 2004 stadium election materials, the 2016 Globe Life Field agreements and the entertainment-district master plan did not surface with mode-split or clearance numbers in my searches. I also found no NCTCOG special-event traffic study for the district beyond the World Cup transit plan. | Not found | — |

**Read for the model:** a double date is the region's worst case because it combines ~0% transit with a fixed official parking supply that covers only about half the demand. Staggered start times are the only mitigation in the record. If the starts were simultaneous (as on 22 Aug 2025), egress from both buildings would land on I-30 and SH-360 together with no rail relief.
