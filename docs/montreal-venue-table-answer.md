# Montreal venues of about 5,000+ — capacity table for Fan/Friction

Research date: **October 7, 2026**. Boundary: island of Montreal, Laval, Longueuil and the South Shore (Brossard, Saint-Lambert, Boucherville).

**Labels used on every number**

- **[O] Official**: published by the venue, team, league, event organizer, the City, or Tourisme Montréal's venue spec sheets (which come from the venues).
- **[R] Reported**: media, Wikipedia, ticketing or third-party sites.
- **[E] Estimated**: my own arithmetic, with the working shown.

**Sort key.** Rows are sorted by the number I recommend the app use as the event size (the "Use" figure in each row). For open sites and the tennis grounds that is people on site per day, not seats.

**Headline corrections to the brief**

- The Bell Centre's hockey capacity is now **20,962**, not 21,105. It changed for the 2025–26 season.
- The Victoire's main home has been **Place Bell since 2024–25**. The Verdun Auditorium was the main home only in 2023–24 and now hosts training camp and preseason.
- Stade Uniprix became Stade IGA in **2018**, not 2019.
- Parc Jean-Drapeau has more than one road link (the Concorde Bridge and the Jacques-Cartier Bridge ramp), but only **one Métro station** serves both islands. The transit bottleneck is real.
- The archive file `docs/archive/research/past-dates/san-diego-montreal.md` was not attached, so I could not read it. See the check under section 2.

## 4. The Palais des congrès

**Treat it as a conditional event site, not a standing venue.** Most days it holds conventions that do not put a public crowd on the streets. A handful of public shows do:

| Event | Per-day figure | Peak pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Comiccon (July; 3 days) | ≈20,700/day [E from 62,000 in 2022 [O]]; 2026 "over 40,000 expected" [R] | All-day arrivals; Saturday is likely the peak |
| Salon de l'auto (January; 10 days) | ≈17,000/day [E from 170,000 [O]]; weekend days higher | All-day arrivals |
| Marathon expo (October; 2 days) | Not published | All-day |

In the read, give these days a size equal to the daily attendance, but **switch off the start-time proximity term**. The crowd trickles in over 8–12 hours instead of arriving for a single start time, so it adds road load without a sharp spike. Watch for overlap: Comiccon 2026 (Jul 3–5) overlapped the last days of the Jazz Festival (to Jul 4), about 1 km away.
