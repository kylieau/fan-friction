## Boundary

I kept your line (City of Chicago, Cook County, plus the named collar towns) with two recommendations:

1. **Add Chicagoland Speedway, Joliet (Will County).** It seats about 47,000 [R] after being cut from 75,000 ([Herald-News](https://www.shawlocal.com/the-herald-news/2025/07/31/nascar-expected-to-return-to-joliets-chicagoland-speedway-in-2026/)); its 2026 Cup race sold out ([Jayski](https://www.jayski.com/2026/07/05/eero-400-at-chicagoland-speedway-sold-out/)); next race weekend June 25–27, 2027 ([Jayski](https://www.jayski.com/2026/08/18/chicagoland-speedway-returns-to-2027-nascar-schedule/)). It is one weekend a year, but it is a 47,000-person Chicago-market crowd on I-55/I-80. Coordinates about 41.475, -88.057 (A). Open-air.
2. **Watch Hammond, Indiana.** If the Bears build there, the largest crowd in the market leaves your boundary. Nothing opens before 2028 (see section 5), so no change is needed yet.

Also note: Schaumburg is in Cook County, so Wintrust Field is inside your line even though Schaumburg wasn't on your town list. Ravinia (Highland Park) is in Lake County but is on your list, so I kept it.

## 4. McCormick Place

**Yes, treat it as a venue, but size it per event-day, not by capacity.** It has no meaningful fixed crowd capacity, and its trade shows mostly move badge-holders by hotel shuttle. Its big public shows, though, put tens of thousands of people a day onto the same roads as Soldier Field, Wintrust Arena and Huntington Bank Pavilion:

| Event | Days | Total (reported) | Per-day average [E] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chicago Auto Show 2025 | 10 | 217,000 | ~21,700 (weekends will run higher; no official daily split found) |
| C2E2 2025 | 3 | ~100,000 | ~33,000 |

Fan Expo Chicago is **not** at McCormick Place: it runs at Rosemont's Donald E. Stephens Convention Center (row 28), about 80,000 over Aug 14–16, 2026. Treat it the same way.

My recommendation: enter McCormick Place only for public consumer shows (Auto Show, C2E2, the Marathon expo), using the per-day average, and skip trade-only shows unless you have a daily figure.

**How it interacts with Wintrust Arena.** Wintrust Arena is part of the same McCormick Square complex run by the state's MPEA. A Sky or DePaul game during a big show means two crowds on the same Cermak Rd / King Dr / Lake Shore Dr ramps and the same Green Line station. Count them as two events at one site (zero distance), not as one.
