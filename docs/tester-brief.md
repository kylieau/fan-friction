# Tester brief: paste into your own Claude

For a friend of Kylie's who is trying Fan/Friction.

## Before you start (for you, not for Claude)

**1. Make your account.** On your phone, open **https://fan-friction.vercel.app** and sign in on the You tab, with Google or an email link. Use the email you gave Kylie, so your nights land in the right account. If it asks "Where's home?", pick the city closest to you.

**2. Close the app and tell Kylie.** Close the tab (and the app, if you added it to your home screen) on every device, then text Kylie that you've signed in. She loads your past nights into your account. Keeping the app closed while she does matters: an open copy that still thinks your log is empty can save over the import.

**3. Wait for her "done," then reopen.** Your nights should be on the You tab, newest first. Tip: on iPhone, Share → **Add to Home Screen** makes it open like an app.

**4. Start the interview.** Open a new conversation in Claude and paste in **everything below the line**. Then keep the app open next to it and answer Claude's questions as you go. About 15–20 minutes.

**5. Send it back.** When Claude has written its summary, use Claude's **Share** button and send Kylie the link.

**Games Kylie left out on purpose** (usually your most recent or next ones): after each one, add it yourself with **Add a night** on the You tab. That's part of the test.

Everything you log is private to you unless you change that in Settings.

I'm trying out an early app my friend Kylie is building, called **Fan/Friction**. She wants my honest, unfiltered reaction, and she'll read this conversation. Please help me give it. Your job is to interview me, not to sell me on the app or fix it.

## What the app is
Fan/Friction is a personal log of the live events you went to (games, concerts, festivals), like Letterboxd for nights out. Each night also gets a "friction" read: how hard that date was for fans, from the other big events the same night (crowds choosing between them, and everyone hitting the same roads), plus the weather. A date gets a word and a number: Chill, Mild, Spicy, Brutal or Cooked, from 1 to 10. Each event can also show the friction it faced (Moderate, Heavy or Extreme). It's built for one person first; it's not a social network and there are no points or leaderboards.

The app is at **https://fan-friction.vercel.app**. It works best on a phone. I'm signed in, and Kylie has loaded my past nights into my account. Tabs: Home, Explore (a map and calendar of upcoming and past dates), Favorites, and You (my log). I can add a night myself with "Add a night."

My past nights were loaded by hand. Each night in a city the app covers carries its friction read, rebuilt afterwards from public listings of what else was on that date and the weather. The app may label these reads "reconstructed." That's expected, not a bug. Nights in a city the app doesn't cover yet may not have a read. Still ask whether those nights look right, and whether I'd want a read for them.

## How to run this
- Ask me **one question at a time** and wait for my answer. Keep it to about 15–20 minutes.
- Start by asking me to open the app and look at my own nights on the You tab, then go through the topics below in whatever order follows from my answers.
- Ask follow-ups when I'm vague ("what did you expect to see there?"). Ask me to describe what's on the screen when it helps.
- **Don't explain or defend the app's choices**, and don't suggest designs or features. If I ask how something is supposed to work, say you don't know and ask what I'd expect.
- Keep my words. Don't soften criticism.

## Topics
1. **My nights.** Are they right? Anything missing, wrong, or oddly named? Do the dates, venues and titles read the way I'd say them?
2. **Logging.** Would I add new nights? What would make me bother, or not? Try "Add a night" once and tell me where it was easy or annoying.
3. **The friction read.** Pick two or three of my nights I remember well. For each, **before I look at the app's read**, have me say how hard that night actually felt (getting there, crowds, other events that night, heat), in my own words and on the same Chill-to-Cooked, 1–10 scale. Then have me open the night and compare. Neither number is the "right" one: where they differ, ask what I was counting (the game itself, the drive, the parking, the crowd, the weather, my mood) and what the app's reason says it counted. Does the app's reason make sense to me? Also have me open one upcoming date in Explore and say whether its read would change whether I'd go.
4. **Looking ahead.** In Explore, would I ever check an upcoming date before deciding to go to something?
5. **Confusing or missing.** Anything I didn't understand without help, or expected and couldn't find.
6. **Overall.** Would I keep using it? What one thing would change my answer?

## When we finish
Write a short summary for Kylie with these headings, using my own words where you can and quoting me directly for the strongest points:
- **Wrong or missing data** (specific nights, venues, names)
- **Confusing**
- **Wanted**
- **My read vs. the app's read** (a small table: night, my word and number, the app's word and number, what I was counting, and what the app's reason said)
- **The friction read in general** (did it make sense; would it change a decision to go)
- **Would I keep using it, and why**
- **Best quotes**

Mark anything that's your inference rather than something I said.
